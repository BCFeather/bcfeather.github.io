Manchester City win a fourth title in five years

The club took the league on the final day on 22 May, and the margin was one point.

Posted 22 May 2022
By Caozhe Bao
2 min read

Manchester City won the league on the final day, 22 May, after coming from two goals down against Aston Villa. They won 3-2, and the goals came in the last 15 minutes. The title was their fourth in five years, and they finished a point above Liverpool. Their manager had changed the shape of the side at half time, and the substitutes scored twice. They had won 29 of their 38 matches, and they had scored 99 goals.

Liverpool had won their own match against Wolverhampton, and they needed City to drop points. They led the table for 20 minutes in the second half, and City's goals took it back. The Liverpool manager said his side had done everything and that the margin was a point. City finished with 93 points, and Liverpool with 92. That total would have won the league in 25 of the previous 30 seasons. City scored 99 goals, which is the second highest in a season, and they conceded 26. The Liverpool manager said his side had won 92 points and had lost one match in the last 18.

Their manager had won the title in four of his six seasons, and he said he would stay for the next one. The squad had cost 1 billion pounds in transfer fees, which is the most in the league. The club had won the league cup and had lost the Champions League semi-final. City had won 29 of their 38 matches, and they had lost three.

City won four matches 1-0 in the last 10 minutes, and they took 30 points from losing positions. The final day was watched by 3 million people in Britain, and the highlights drew 10 million. The club's shirt sponsor paid 60 million pounds a year, and the club's revenue was 600 million, which is the second highest in the league after Manchester United. The season had been decided by fine margins. Liverpool lost the league by a point for the second time in four years, and they had won the two cups. Their wage bill was 350 million pounds, which is the highest in the league.

A second case was opened in 2023. The club had been bought by a Abu Dhabi group in 2008, and it had spent 2 billion pounds on players since. The league had charged the club with breaking its financial rules in 2020, and the case was dismissed on appeal in 2022. The club's stadium held 55,000, and the average attendance was 53,000.

City won the league again in 2023 and completed the treble, and they won a fourth consecutive title in 2024. The next season was harder. Their manager said the 2022 title was the hardest, because Liverpool had pushed them to the last day. The league's case concerned payments to sponsors over nine years, and it was heard by an independent panel. City's revenue was 600 million pounds, and their wage bill was 350 million.
