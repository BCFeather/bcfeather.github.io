AMD launches the chip it hopes will challenge NVIDIA

The MI300X has more memory than the rival accelerator, and the company said the customers include Microsoft and Meta.

Posted 6 Dec 2023
By Caozhe Bao
2 min read

AMD introduced the MI300X accelerator on 6 December, a chip for the artificial intelligence that carries about 192 gigabytes of the memory, more than the comparable product from NVIDIA. The company said the chip would be in the volume production in the first quarter, and the shares rose about 2 per cent on the day. The event was held in San Francisco, and the company put the chip on the stage beside the systems from the server makers that will sell it.
