---
title: "Samsung folds the phone smaller and puts a stylus in the pocket"
date: 2022-08-10 23:40:00 +0800
categories: [technology]
description: "The Fold and the Flip arrive with slimmer hinges, a cheaper price for the clamshell and no new Note."
---

Samsung announced its fourth generation of folding phones at an event on Wednesday, and the two models are the ones that the company has been selling for a year with the faults taken out. The Fold is a tablet that closes into a phone, and the Flip is a phone that closes into a square. Both have a redesigned hinge that leaves less of a gap when they shut, and both use the same processor as the flagship slab. The price of the Fold stayed at about eighteen hundred dollars and the Flip came down to about a thousand.

The changes are mostly about durability. The screen on the earlier models had a crease down the middle and a layer that could be peeled off by accident, and the new panels are stronger and the crease is shallower. The hinge has fewer parts and it is rated for more folds than the previous one. The company also added water resistance to both, which is the feature that the reviewers had asked for since the first model. It has not added dust resistance, and the engineers said that is still a problem for the hinge.

The software is where the folding shape earns its price. The Fold runs three applications side by side and it turns a list into a board of thumbnails when it is open, and the Flip can be used as its own tripod when it is half closed. The outer screen on the Flip is larger and it now runs full applications rather than small widgets. Both phones ship with the pen support that the Note used to have, and the company has stopped making the Note.

At the same time, the note is the part of the line that the announcement closed. The Note was the phone with the stylus, and it was the largest seller in the premium segment until the Fold took its place. The company said the stylus is now a feature of the Fold rather than a product, and the customers who wanted one have been told to buy it separately. The analysts said the move protects the margin on the folding line and ends a brand that had been losing ground for three years.

Meanwhile, the market for folding phones is still small and it is growing. Shipments were about ten million units last year, which is less than one per cent of the total, and the forecasts expect them to double this year. The Chinese manufacturers have released cheaper models that fold in the same way, and the prices have fallen faster than the quality has risen. The Korean company's advantage is the supply of the panels, which it makes itself, and the disadvantage is the price of the result.

What the announcement shows is a category that has moved from demonstration to product. The first Fold was a prototype sold as a phone and it broke in the hands of the reviewers. The fourth one is a device that a person can carry for two years without treating it as a curiosity, and the price is still the barrier. The next model will decide whether the shape becomes the standard or stays a premium option, and the answer will come from the people who buy the cheaper Chinese versions.
