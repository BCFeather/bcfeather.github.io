---
title: "Draghi resigns and Italy prepares for an election in September"
date: 2022-07-21 23:55:00 +0800
categories: [news]
description: "The prime minister leaves after a confidence vote that three parties refused to support, and the president dissolves the parliament."
---

Mario Draghi resigned on Thursday morning after a confidence vote in the senate the night before, and the president dissolved the parliament and called an election for 25 September. The vote was won, but three of the parties in the coalition refused to take part, which removed the majority that the government needed. The prime minister had offered his resignation a week earlier and the president had asked him to try again. The second attempt failed within five days.

It began with a confidence vote on a package that included a payment for the poor and a plan for delivering aid to Ukraine. The Five Star Movement, which had been the largest party in the last election, refused to support the package, and its leader said the government had ignored its demands on a minimum wage and on a windfall tax. The other two parties that left the coalition had been weakening for months, and the vote gave them a reason.

On 25 September Italians vote for the first time since 2018, in the middle of an energy crisis. The parties of the right have been leading in the polls for a year, and they have agreed to run as a coalition with a single candidate for the prime minister. The Democratic Party and its allies have been unable to agree on a common list, and the Five Star Movement has been polling at about a tenth of its previous vote. The campaign will be short and the parliament will be dissolved for most of it.

The next government inherits a more fragile economy than the one Draghi found. The debt is about a hundred and fifty per cent of output, and the interest that is paid on it will rise as the central bank raises rates. The recovery fund money from the European Union is tied to a plan of reforms that the previous government wrote, and the next government will have to implement it. The budget for next year has to be presented in the autumn, and the election leaves little time.

The constitution left the president little choice. A prime minister who cannot command a majority has to resign, and a parliament that cannot produce a government has to be dissolved. The head of state asked the parties to reflect before he acted, and he said in his statement that the country needed a government that could take decisions. The parties that caused the crisis said they had done so in the interests of their voters.

Italians now face a clear choice that the last eighteen months had postponed. The two coalitions have different views about the war, about the debt and about the European Union, and the voters will now have to choose between them rather than between personalities. The campaign will be fought over the price of gas and the minimum wage, and the result will decide whether the country stays with the plan that the fund has approved. The election is in nine weeks, and the winter bill will arrive before the new government does.
