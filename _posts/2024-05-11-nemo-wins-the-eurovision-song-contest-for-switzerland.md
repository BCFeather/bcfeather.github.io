---
title: "Nemo wins the Eurovision Song Contest for Switzerland"
date: 2024-05-11 23:45:00 +0800
categories: [culture]
description: "Nemo won the Eurovision Song Contest in Malmö on 11 May with the song The Code, taking Switzerland's third title, at a final held under heavy security and overshadowed by protests over Israel's participation."
---

Swiss singer Nemo won the Eurovision Song Contest in Malmö on the eleventh of May with the song The Code, a track that mixes rap, opera and pop and that the performer described as a story about discovering a non-binary identity. Entry took 365 points from the juries and 226 from the public, which put it ahead of the Croatian entry by 42 points. Switzerland had won the contest twice before, in 1956 and 1988.

Result was close enough to be uncertain until the final set of votes. Croatia's entry, a folk-rock number performed by Baby Lasagna, led the public vote and was considered the favourite, and it finished second. Ukraine took third, and France and Portugal were in the top five. Nordic countries did less well than their delegations had hoped, and the host country finished in the lower half of the table.

Contest itself was held under conditions that had little to do with music. Police from several countries were deployed in Malmö, demonstrators gathered in the days before the final to protest against Israel's participation, and the Israeli delegation was required to change the lyrics of its song after the organizers judged them to be political. Security around the arena was heavier than at any contest in recent memory, and the organizers said they had received threats.

Protest inside the arena was contained but visible. Several artists wore symbols or made statements during their performances, and the Irish entrant was reported to have had a dispute with the Israeli broadcaster during rehearsals. Applause for the Israeli entry in the semi-final was mixed with booing, which the broadcaster described as political and the organizers described as within the rules. Police said they had made some arrests outside the venue and that no one had been hurt.

Televoting figures were affected by the war in ways that the organizers do not publish in detail. Votes from some countries were removed after the contest because of irregularities that the organizers attributed to organized campaigns, and the European Broadcasting Union said it had detected attempts to manipulate the result. That is a problem that has grown with the contest's online audience, and the union has been slow to address it.

Contest also drew its smallest television audience in several years in some markets, which the organizers attributed to the war and to the availability of the show on streaming platforms, and which broadcasters said would be studied before next year's event.

Meaning of the victory for the contest is a return to a kind of song that has done well in recent years. Winners since 2021 have been acts from outside the traditional centres of European pop, and Nemo is the third Swiss or Italian winner in a decade after a long period in which the contest was dominated by Scandinavian and Eastern European entries. Contest will be held in Switzerland next year, in a city that has not yet been chosen, and the organizers will have to decide how much of the security apparatus of Malmö to reproduce.
