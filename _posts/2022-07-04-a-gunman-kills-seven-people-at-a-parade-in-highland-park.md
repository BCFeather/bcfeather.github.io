---
title: "A gunman on a roof kills seven people at a parade in Highland Park"
date: 2022-07-04 23:55:00 +0800
categories: [news]
description: "The attacker fires into the crowd from a business above the route, then escapes among the people who are running."
---

Seven people were killed and more than thirty were wounded on Monday morning when a gunman fired into the crowd at the Independence Day parade in Highland Park, a suburb north of Chicago. The shots came from the roof of a business that overlooks the route, and the police said the man had climbed up with a rifle. The parade had been under way for about ten minutes when the firing began. The wounded included children, and several of them were taken to hospitals in the city.

In the confusion of the first minutes he walked out of the building and drove away. He had dressed in women's clothing, according to the police, and he walked out of the building with the crowd that was running from the route. He was arrested eight hours later after a chase that closed a stretch of the motorway, and the prosecutors said he had planned the attack for weeks. The rifle was legally purchased, and the authorities said it had been bought by the man himself, who was twenty-one.

By the afternoon the argument had turned to guns. Illinois has a licensing system that is stricter than most states, and the state's governor said the attack showed that the rules had gaps. The suspect had been reported to the police twice in 2019, once for a threat and once for a suicide attempt, and the reports had not prevented the purchase. The local police said the records had been handled in the way the law required and that the law allowed the sale.

For a suburb on the north shore, the fourth of July is the day the whole town stands on the same street. The route goes through a business district of low buildings, and the roof that the attacker used was about a hundred metres from the crowd. The videos that the witnesses recorded show people leaving behind chairs, bags and pushchairs, and the police spent two days collecting them for the owners. The town cancelled the fireworks that had been planned for the evening.

Washington responded in the way it has responded after each of these shootings, and nothing was agreed. The president said the country had to do more and the Senate was in the middle of a negotiation about a modest package of gun measures. The governor of New York, whose state had passed a stricter law in the week before, said the patchwork of rules across the states was the problem. The polls showed that a majority supported a ban on the kind of rifle that was used.

Everyone in the country has seen this pattern before. A young man with a legally bought rifle fires into a crowd from a height, the police arrive after the firing has stopped, and the argument turns to the law before the funerals have been arranged. The seven people who died had come to watch a parade with their families, and the town will hold the parade again next year. The survivors will ask a question that no debate has answered.
