---
title: "Real Madrid beat Barcelona 3-1 and go top of La Liga"
date: 2022-10-16 23:35:00 +0800
categories: [sport]
description: "Benzema, Valverde and Rodrygo score at the Bernabéu, and Barcelona lose for the first time this season."
---

Real Madrid beat Barcelona 3-1 at the Bernabéu on Sunday and went top of La Liga, and the margin was kinder to the visitors than the play. Benzema scored the first after twelve minutes, Valverde the second before the interval and Rodrygo a penalty in stoppage time, with Ferrán Torres pulling one back late. Barcelona had arrived unbeaten in the league and left three points behind their rivals. The stadium was full an hour before kick-off, and the home supporters had prepared a display for the occasion.

A move that Barcelona's defenders had been warned about all week produced the first goal. Vinícius broke into the space behind the right-back, drew the goalkeeper and squared for Benzema to finish into an empty net. The second was a shot from Valverde that took a deflection off a defender and looped over the goalkeeper. The third came from a penalty that the referee awarded after a review, and Rodrygo sent it low and to the left. Barcelona had a goal ruled out for offside at the other end in the minutes before it, which the coach called the turning point.

Madrid now has twenty-five points and Barcelona twenty-two, and the two are level on games played. It is the first defeat of the season for the visitors, and the first win for Madrid against a team above it. The gap is small enough that the coaches declined to describe the result as decisive, and large enough that the table now looks different from the way it looked on Saturday.

Barcelona's problems began at the back. Koundé, Araújo and Christensen were all unavailable, and the coach had to use a midfielder as a centre-back for part of the match. The club's finances remain the subject of a dispute with the league over spending limits, and the squad is thin in exactly the positions that were missing. Two of the defenders are expected back within a month, which is the only part of the evening that the club can call good news.

Xavi set his side up to press high and to hold a line near the halfway circle, which is the method he has used since he took the job. Ancelotti's answer was to let Barcelona have the ball and to attack the space behind that line with Vinícius and Rodrygo. The plan worked within twelve minutes, and the second goal came through the same gap. Xavi changed to a back three at half-time, which steadied the game and produced no goals. Ancelotti brought on a defender for the last twenty minutes, and the home crowd applauded the substitution as if it were a goal.

A result like this does not decide a league in October, and it changes the mood of both clubs. Madrid has now won five consecutive matches in all competitions, and Barcelona has to find defenders before the next one. The two meet again in the second half of the season, by which time the table will have more to say. The coaches shook hands at the end, and the home crowd stayed to sing.
