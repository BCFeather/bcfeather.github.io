title: "The winter games open and the snow that has to be made"
date: 2025-02-07 21:20:00 +0800
categories: [sport]
description: "The Asian winter games opened in Harbin on the seventh of February, and the snow on the slopes is a manufactured product as much as a weather event."
---

The Asian winter games opened in Harbin on the seventh of February, with a ceremony in a stadium that was built for the event and a schedule that runs for eight days across two clusters of venues. The competition is regional rather than global, and the scale is smaller than the Olympics that the same city hosted in 2009 for the winter universiade, and the local organisers describe it as a test of the infrastructure that the province has built.

The snow is the part of the games that the visitors do not think about. A slope that is prepared for a race needs a base that is several metres deep, and the base is made by machines that spray water into cold air rather than by the sky. The water is pumped from a reservoir, the compressors run on electricity, and the cost of a season of snow is a line in the budget of every ski resort in the world.

The chemistry of the snow is also a technical subject. The machine-made snow is denser and it holds its shape longer than the natural kind, which is why the race courses are built on it, and the crystal that the machines produce is a small round pellet rather than a flake. The skiers notice the difference in the sound the surface makes under the edge.

The games are also an argument about the climate. The winter sports have a shorter season than they had thirty years ago at the same latitude, the resorts at the lower altitudes have closed, and the competitions have moved to the higher ones. The artificial snow is the adaptation that has kept the sport going, and the cost of it is the reason the smaller resorts have disappeared.

The economic calculation of the event is a five-year plan rather than a fortnight. The venues that were built for the games become the training centres and the tourist attractions, the hotels that were added become the base of a winter season, and the airport that was expanded carries the visitors in the years after. The accounting is done over a decade and the games are the excuse.

The local culture is the other asset that the event sells. The ice sculptures that the city has built every winter for decades, the frozen river that becomes a road, the food that the visitors photograph. The games are a platform for that, and the platform is the reason the province bid for them.

For the athletes, the event is a step in a calendar that is crowded. The world championships follow in March, the season ends in April, and the training for the next year begins in June. The games are a competition and they are also a rehearsal, and the results that matter are the ones that the coaches record rather than the ones the medal table shows. The medals go to the national teams with the largest programmes, and the technique that a smaller delegation brings home is the part that lasts.
