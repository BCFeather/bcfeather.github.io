---
title: "A fire in a Coptic church near Cairo kills forty-one people"
date: 2022-08-14 23:50:00 +0800
categories: [news]
description: "The blaze starts in the air conditioning during the morning service, and most of the dead are children in a nursery."
---

A fire broke out in a Coptic church in the city of Giza on Sunday morning during the liturgy, and at least forty-one people died. The church was packed for the service, and the smoke filled the building within minutes. Most of the victims were children who had been in a nursery on an upper floor, and the health ministry said sixteen people were injured. The fire began in the air conditioning unit on the second floor, according to the prosecutors, and the building had no emergency exit.

The congregation was one of the largest in the district, and the church is in a crowded neighbourhood where the streets are narrow. Witnesses said the doors were blocked by people trying to leave at the same time, and the videos taken from outside show smoke coming out of the windows for an hour. The fire service arrived within minutes and it needed another hour to reach the upper floor. The families waited outside the church for the bodies to be brought out, and the list of the dead was read over a loudspeaker.

The Coptic Church is the largest Christian community in the Middle East and it numbers about ten per cent of the population. The community has complained for years about the rules that make it hard to build and to repair churches, and the building that burned had been licensed as a service centre rather than as a church. The authorities have promised to close the gap, and the president offered his condolences to the pope of the church in a telephone call.

At the same time, the cause is under investigation and the early findings point to an electrical fault. The air conditioning was in a room next to the nursery, and the smoke travelled through a staircase that had no fire doors. The prosecutors said they had detained several people, including the contractor who installed the unit, and they were examining whether the materials used in the building met the code. The fire brigade said its equipment was adequate and the access was difficult.

Meanwhile, the political context is the one that the community watches. Coptic Christians have been attacked by Islamist groups over the last decade, and the churches have been guarded by the police since a bombing in 2016. The community's leaders have asked for a review of the safety rules for the older churches, most of which were built before the modern code. The government has said it will fund the repairs and the compensation, and it has also said it will speed the licensing of churches that have been waiting for years.

What the fire shows is the cost of a building that was never meant to hold a crowd. The church was full because the feast was being celebrated, and the nursery was full because the parents were at the service. The rules that are meant to prevent this kind of fire are written at the level of the city and enforced at the level of the district, and the district had approved the air conditioning. The funeral was held on Monday, and the investigation will report to a committee that has promised to publish its findings.
