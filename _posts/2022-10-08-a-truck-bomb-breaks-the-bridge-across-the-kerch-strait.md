---
title: "A truck bomb breaks the bridge across the Kerch Strait"
date: 2022-10-08 23:30:00 +0800
categories: [news]
description: "The road span of the bridge to the Crimean peninsula partly collapses and a train of fuel tankers burns, cutting the main supply route to the south."
---

A truck exploded on the road span of the bridge across the Kerch Strait shortly after six in the morning, and the blast brought down two lanes and set fire to a train of fuel tankers on the parallel rail line. Russian investigators said three people had died, and they opened a criminal case. Traffic stopped in both directions, and the ferry crossing was closed for hours. The bridge is the only road link between Russia and the peninsula it annexed in 2014.

The structure was opened in 2018 by President Vladimir Putin, who drove a lorry across it himself to mark the occasion. It runs for nineteen kilometres over the strait, with a road span and a rail span side by side, and it cost about two hundred and twenty-eight billion roubles. Engineers had called the project difficult because the water is deep, the weather is bad and the seabed is unstable. The rail span was finished a year after the road, and the two were designed to carry freight as well as cars.

Its military value is the reason it was built in a hurry. The rail span carries fuel, ammunition and heavy equipment to the southern front, and the road span carries lorries that supply the garrisons at Melitopol and Kherson. Without the crossing, the army would have to move freight along the land route through the occupied south, which adds hours and hundreds of kilometres. Trucks queue at the ferry terminal whenever the bridge is closed, and the queue was several kilometres long by the evening.

Officials in Moscow described the explosion as a terrorist act and promised that the crossing would be repaired. The governor of the region said the ferry would start running again within hours and that the rail line would reopen for limited traffic. Engineers were sent to inspect the span, and one lane of the road was reopened for cars by the afternoon. The railway operator said eight tankers had been destroyed and that the track underneath would need to be rebuilt.

Officials in Kyiv did not claim responsibility, and several of them made jokes about the bridge on social media. One presidential adviser posted a line about the symbolism and later said it had been a comment about the meaning of the structure rather than a claim. Western officials said they had no confirmation of who had placed the device, and analysts said the timing suggested an operation that had been prepared for weeks.

For the peninsula the crossing is the main supply line for everything that arrives by land, and losing it for even a few days forces a rethink of how the south is fed. For the war it is a symbol as much as a road, since the bridge was the visible proof that the annexation had been made permanent. Repairs to the rail span will take weeks, and the road span will carry less weight when it reopens. The traffic that the bridge used to take will now arrive by ferry or by a longer route, and both of those options cost time.
