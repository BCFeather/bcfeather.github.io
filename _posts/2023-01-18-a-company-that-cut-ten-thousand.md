Microsoft said on 18 January that it would cut about ten thousand jobs, which is fewer than five per cent of its global workforce and the largest reduction the company had made in eight years. The announcement came in a memo from the chief executive, Satya Nadella, and the cuts were to be spread across the remainder of the fiscal year rather than made in a single day. The company employed about 220,000 people, which made the reduction modest in proportion and enormous in the number of households it touched.

The company had hired aggressively through the pandemic, adding staff in cloud engineering, sales and support while its revenue grew at rates that its own forecasts had underestimated. Growth in the cloud business had slowed in the preceding two quarters, and the personal computer market had contracted after two years in which every household that wanted a laptop had bought one. Its headcount had grown by about forty per cent since 2019, a rate that no mature software business had sustained before and that the pandemic made look permanent. The growth had been justified by demand that arrived and then slowed.

Nadella's memo framed the reduction as a rebalancing rather than a retreat, and said that the company would continue to hire in artificial intelligence. The phrase was the standard one in that season, and it was also the true one: the money that had been spent on sales capacity was being moved to computing capacity, and the two kinds of spending employ different people. It also said it would take space out of its property portfolio, which is the part of a restructuring that shows up in the accounts years later.

The charge against earnings was small relative to the company's profits, and the share price rose on the day. What the market was pricing was the signal rather than the saving, because a company that had been the most reliable compounder in the industry was now behaving like the others. Analysts noted that the operating margin had fallen for three quarters, and that the reduction was the fastest way to stop it falling.

By then the pattern was continental. Each announcement was received as a separate event and each belonged to the same correction, which was the unwinding of the hiring that the pandemic had justified. The announcements came separately and the effect was the same, because the same interest rates had raised the cost of capital for all of them at once.

Ten thousand jobs is a line in a memo and ten thousand households doing arithmetic about a mortgage. The memo took eleven paragraphs, and the arithmetic will take longer. The memo's last paragraph asked the people who remained to treat their departing colleagues with compassion, which is the sentence every memo of the kind contains.
