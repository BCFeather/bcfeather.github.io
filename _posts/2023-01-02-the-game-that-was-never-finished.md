---
title: "The game that was never finished"
date: 2023-01-02 23:55:00 +0800
categories: [sport]
description: "Damar Hamlin, a twenty-four-year-old safety for the Buffalo Bills, suffered a cardiac arrest on the field in Cincinnati on 2 January 2023, and the game between the Bills and the Bengals was suspended and later abandoned."
---

Damar Hamlin, a safety in his second season with the Buffalo Bills, tackled the Cincinnati receiver Tee Higgins early in the first quarter of a Monday night game, stood up after the collision, and fell backwards onto the field. Medical staff reached him within seconds and began cardiopulmonary resuscitation. An ambulance crossed the turf, the two teams watched from their sidelines, and the game stopped. The collision was an ordinary one, of the kind that happens a dozen times in a game, which is the detail that made the scene unbearable to watch.

What happened next was televised for the better part of an hour, and the broadcasters did not know what to do with it. Players from both teams knelt and prayed in a circle at midfield. The stadium, which had been loud, went quiet enough that the sirens could be heard. The league announced that the game would be suspended, and the following day it announced that the game would not be resumed at all. The rulebook has no clause for a game that stops in the first quarter, and the officials on the field improvised for an hour while the ambulance left.

Hamlin was twenty-four. He had been drafted in the sixth round in 2021 and had become a starter in his second year, which is the narrow window in which a late pick either makes a career or loses it. In the days after the collapse, the charity page he had set up for a Christmas toy drive in his hometown filled with donations from across the country. He had played in every game of the season until that night, and the tackle that hurt him was one he had made hundreds of times.

Doctors at the University of Cincinnati Medical Center said he had been placed in critical care, and for two days the hospital released statements that described his condition without naming a cause. The league and the players' union said afterwards that they would review the medical arrangements at every stadium, and the review became a discussion about how quickly a defibrillator can reach a player on the far side of a field. His family arrived during the night, and the team's medical staff stayed at the hospital rather than travelling with the club.

The football consequences were immediate and awkward. The Bills and the Bengals were competing for the same playoff positions, and a game that had no result left the standings with an asymmetry that the league had to legislate. A coin toss was proposed and then abandoned, neutral venues were discussed, and the eventual solution satisfied nobody completely. The players' association asked for the game to be abandoned rather than replayed, and the league's decision followed the request within a day.

A sport that measures everything in yards and seconds discovered that the interval that mattered was the one between the impact and the first compression. The rest of the season would be played with that interval in mind. What the league could not schedule was the anxiety of the players who had watched it, and that anxiety came back every time a receiver crossed the middle of the field.
