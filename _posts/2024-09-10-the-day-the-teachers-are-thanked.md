description: "Teacher's Day was fixed in 1985 and it is marked in every school with flowers, a speech and a red pen. The day celebrates a profession that is short of people in the places that need them most."
---

Teacher's Day falls on the tenth of September and it was fixed in 1985. The date was chosen so that it precedes the national holiday and follows the start of term, which puts it in the second week of the school year.

The ceremony in a school is small. A pupil reads a poem, the head teacher says a few words. The parents' committee brings flowers for the staff room. The whole thing takes half an hour and the lessons resume at nine.

The gift is the part that the rules restrict. A teacher may not accept a gift worth more than a token. A school that receives a parcel of fruit for the staff room puts it there rather than in a drawer.

The profession is short of people in the counties. A rural school that advertises a post in the summer fills it late or not at all. A teacher who takes it is often the only one in the school who teaches that subject.

The pay has risen and the workload has risen faster. A teacher's salary is set by a provincial scale that has been raised twice in five years. The hours include the marking, the parents' messages and the duty that is assigned in rotation.

The head teacher is the role that nobody wants. A head who is responsible for the building, the budget, the safety inspections and the exam results is a head who teaches less. The post is advertised twice in some districts before it is filled.

The rural subsidy is the instrument that is used to move people. A teacher who accepts a post in a remote county receives an allowance on top of the salary and a priority in the housing list. The allowance is the part that keeps them for a second year.

The training is the part that has changed. A programme that pairs a young teacher with an experienced one has been running for a decade. The pair is assessed on the lessons rather than on the exam.

The parents are the ones who mark the day most visibly. A class group buys a cake and a card, and a parent who sends a red envelope in a message is told by the teacher that the message will not be opened.

The recognition is national as well as local. A list of model teachers is published each year and the ones on it receive a certificate and a trip, which is a small reward for a job that is measured in decades.

The students who return are the measure that teachers value. A graduate who comes back to the school in the first week of the job to say hello is the gift that the day is about, and the ones who come back are usually the ones who struggled.

The day ends with the staff room. The flowers are in a bucket, the cake is cut, and the teachers who have a class at two go back to it with a red pen in hand.
