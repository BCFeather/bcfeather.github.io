---
title: "The last day of term, and the school gates in July"
date: 2022-07-31 18:20:00 +0800
categories: [essay]
description: "A walk past a school on the afternoon the term ends, and what the noise of the yard says about the year that has just finished."
---

The term ends on a Friday afternoon in the middle of July, and the street outside the school gates fills with cars before the bell. Parents arrive early and stand in the shade of the wall, and the ones who have come on foot lean against the railings with their phones. The heat has been in the country for a week and the tarmac is soft, and the air above the road bends the view of the houses at the end of it. Nobody is in a hurry, because everybody has been waiting for this hour for a year.

Before anything is seen, there is the noise. The yard is on the other side of the wall and the sound of it reaches the street before the doors open, a mixture of shouting and of something metallic being dragged. Then the gates are opened and the children come out in the order that they have been dismissed, class by class, and the parents find their own and take the bags from them. The bags are heavier than they were in September, which is the measure of the year. The ones who are leaving the school for the last time walk out more slowly than the others.

At the gate the teachers stand and say goodbye. Some of them have been at the school for twenty years and some of them arrived in September, and the ones who are leaving are given cards and are photographed with the classes. A woman in a linen dress is holding a bunch of flowers that she has been given by a group of girls, and she is trying to keep the wrapping from falling apart while she talks to the parents. The head teacher stands by the door and shakes the hands of the ones who are leaving for good.

Within twenty minutes the street empties, and the school becomes the building it is for the rest of the year. The caretaker closes the gates and the windows on the upper floor are shut one by one, and a van arrives to collect the furniture that is being stored for the summer. The noticeboard beside the gate still has the timetable for the exams that were taken in June, and the paper has curled at the edges. The bins are put out and the road sweeper comes along and turns at the corner.

The heat makes the day feel longer than it is. The forecast says the temperature will pass forty degrees at the start of the next week, and the shops have sold out of the fans and of the bottled water. The parents who are going away are worried about the trains, and the ones who are staying are worried about the bills. The holiday that begins today is the one that the children have been counting down to since Easter, and the adults have been counting in a different way.

No ceremony marked the end, and none was needed. The children will forget most of what they were taught and will remember the teachers who were kind to them, and the teachers will remember the class that was difficult and the one that was easy. The street will be quiet for six weeks and then it will fill again with the same cars and the same noise. The summer is long and the autumn is certain, and the gate will open on the first Tuesday of September as it always does.
