---
title: "Peshawar: a mosque inside a police compound"
date: 2023-01-30 23:55:00 +0800
categories: [news]
description: "A suicide bombing at a mosque inside the Police Lines compound in Peshawar on 30 January 2023 killed about a hundred people, most of them police officers, and injured more than a hundred and fifty."
---

The bomber struck during the midday prayer on 30 January, in a mosque that stands inside the Police Lines compound in the centre of Peshawar. Several hundred people were in the prayer hall, almost all of them police officers and staff of the city's security services, and the blast brought down the wall and an inner roof onto the worshippers. The prayer was the Zuhr, the midday one, and the hall was fuller than usual because the police shifts change at that hour and the officers who had finished came to pray before going home.

By the following day the toll stood at about a hundred dead, of which twenty-seven were police. More than a hundred and fifty people were injured, and the Lady Reading Hospital, which is a few minutes from the compound, treated the wounded in corridors. Rescuers worked under the rubble for hours with their hands and with a crane. The rescue continued into the second day, and the provincial government announced compensation for the families of the dead and for the wounded. A crane lifted the fallen roof in the afternoon while the ambulances waited at the gate.

Inside the Red Zone, a district of government buildings and barracks guarded at every entrance, sits the mosque. The bomber had passed through a gate, which is the fact that the investigation will have to explain, and the compound also houses the offices of the province's counter-terrorism department. The gate had a metal detector and a guard post, and the investigation will establish whether the bomber was searched or whether he was wearing a uniform.

Suspicion fell on the Tehreek-e-Taliban Pakistan, which had fought the state for fifteen years and had recently declared an end to a ceasefire. The group's spokesmen did not confirm the attack in the days that followed, and a faction claimed it while the leadership stayed silent, which is the pattern the group has used before. The group had killed more police officers than any other organization in the country over the previous decade, and its attacks had increased since the ceasefire ended.

The politics of the moment made the attack worse. Pakistan was in the middle of a negotiation with the International Monetary Fund over a loan it needed to avoid default, the former prime minister was in an open conflict with the army, and the province of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa had a government that had promised to bring the militants into a settlement. The provincial government had promised to reintegrate fighters who laid down their arms, and the attack was read by its opponents as the failure of that promise.

A mosque inside a compound is a place where the ordinary and the guarded stand in the same line. The policemen who were killed had gone to pray in the middle of a shift, and the shift was the reason they were all in one room. The room where they died had no windows and one door, and the wall that fell was the wall beside the door.
