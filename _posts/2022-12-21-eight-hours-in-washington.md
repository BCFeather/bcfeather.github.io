title: "Zelensky leaves Ukraine for the first time and spends eight hours in Washington"
date: 2022-12-21 23:30:00 +0800
categories: [news]
description: "Zelenskyy leaves Ukraine for the first time since the invasion, meets Biden and addresses a joint session of Congress."
---
Arrangements were made in secret and confirmed only when he was on his way. Zelenskyy had not left the country since the invasion began in February, and his journey began with a train to Poland and continued on an American aircraft. He arrived in Washington on the morning of 21 December, and the White House had announced the schedule a few hours before he landed. The trip had been discussed for weeks and kept quiet until the security arrangements were settled, and the itinerary was released while he was in the air.

At the White House the two presidents held a joint press conference in the East Room, and Biden restated the commitment that the United States would support Ukraine for as long as it took. He announced a further package of security assistance worth one point eight five billion dollars, which included a Patriot air defence battery and munitions for the systems already delivered. That package was the twenty-eighth drawn from American stocks since the war began. Two men exchanged a tie pin and a challenge coin, and they were photographed in the Oval Office before the cameras were cleared.

Patriot was the item that mattered most in Kyiv. It is the most capable air defence system the United States exports, and it can intercept aircraft, drones and some missiles at long range. Training crews takes months, and the battery itself had to be taken from American inventory rather than bought new. Russian officials said before the visit that the system would be a legitimate target, and the threats were repeated after it.

Zelenskyy then went to the Capitol, where he spoke to a joint session of Congress in the evening. He wore his usual fleece and thanked the members for the money they had voted, and he told them that the war would decide the shape of the century. He also said that the money was an investment in security rather than a charity, and he invited the members to visit Kyiv. The chamber applauded him several times, and the Republican leadership sat behind him in the front row. Several members asked for photographs afterwards, and the sergeant at arms had to remind them that the chamber was still in session.

Timing had been chosen for the end of the congressional session, before the new majority took its seats. A package of forty-five billion dollars for Ukraine was working through the Senate, and the speech was designed to make its passage easier. Some Republicans had begun to ask whether the money should be audited and whether Europe was paying its share, and the visit was an answer to that argument as much as to Moscow.

It ended that night, and the president was back in Kyiv by the following evening after a stop in Poland. He had been to Bakhmut the day before, where the fighting was the hardest of the war. Eight hours in Washington, a battery that will take months to arrive, and a speech that will be quoted in budget debates for a year.
