Typhoon Yagi formed in the South China Sea in the first week of September and strengthened faster than the forecast allowed. It landed on the north coast of Hainan on the sixth as a super typhoon. The wind at landfall was measured at more than two hundred kilometres an hour. A category of that size is rare in the sea. Strongest storm on record to hit the island.

Island closed before the wind arrived. Schools shut, flights were cancelled, the bridge to the mainland was closed. Fishing fleet was ordered into harbour two days ahead. The power went out in the counties. A grid that carries a storm load loses lines to fallen trees and to the salt that the wind carries. A county that is repaired in a day is a county with a small population.

Across the gulf the storm hit the mainland, then crossed the border into Vietnam. A plantation of areca and banana on the eastern coast was flattened in an afternoon. Trees that produce the fruit take four years to grow back. The storm crossed the gulf and hit the mainland, then crossed the border into Vietnam. Damage there was worse, because the storm met a coast that was lower and a population that was less prepared.

Rainfall was the second hazard. A storm that has slowed over land drops five hundred millimetres in two days. A hillside that loses its cover sends a flood down a valley that has a village in it. The evacuations worked better than the buildings. A programme that moves people out of the low-lying districts before a storm has been run for a decade. Death toll in the province was lower than in a comparable storm ten years ago.

Insurance is thin in the region. A farmer who loses a crop is compensated by a provincial scheme at a rate that covers a share of the cost. A household with a damaged roof pays for the repair itself. The naming follows a list that the regional committee rotates. A storm that is named after an animal is no stronger than one named after a fruit. List is redrawn every few years so that a name that caused a disaster is retired.

Storm is measured at the port. A harbour master records the wind and the tide every hour. Record is what the engineers use when they design a breakwater for the next fifty years. The frequency is the question that the climate scientists argue about. A warmer sea holds more energy, and the storms that reach the highest category are expected to become more common even if the total number of storms falls.

Reconstruction is the long part. A bridge that is rebuilt takes a year. A plantation takes four. The money for both arrives in a budget that was written before the storm, and the county that spends it in September borrows against the revenue of the next year. By the end of September the count was complete, the bridges were being repaired, and the island had begun to argue about what should be rebuilt where.
