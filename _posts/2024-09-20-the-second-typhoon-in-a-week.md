Typhoon Pulasan landed on the coast south of Shanghai on the evening of the nineteenth. It was the second storm to reach the city in a week. First had been the strongest to make landfall there in seventy-five years. Bebinca had arrived on the sixteenth with a wind that closed the airports and the bridges. City had spent two days clearing fallen trees. Crews were still working when the second warning was issued.

Second storm was weaker and wetter. A typhoon that has lost its structure over the sea carries less wind and more rain. Rainfall in the twelve hours after landfall was the part that flooded the streets. The drainage is built for a storm that returns every few years. A system of pipes and pumps that handles fifty millimetres an hour is overwhelmed by eighty. Water that stands in an underpass is the water that the river cannot take.

Pumps are the constraint. A station that lifts water from a low-lying district runs at capacity for six hours and then fails when the power goes. Generator that is meant to cover it is tested once a year. The schools closed again. A city that had sent its pupils home on the sixteenth did it again on the nineteenth. Term lost four days in the first month.

Transport stopped at seven in the evening. A metro that runs until midnight closed its lower sections. Passengers who were on it were taken to the nearest dry station. The fishing fleet had not gone back out. A boat that had returned for the first storm stayed in the harbour for the second. Crews who had repaired their nets did it again.

Insurance claims were filed in the same week. A household that had a flooded ground floor on the sixteenth had it again on the twentieth. Assessor who visited twice wrote the same report twice. The comparison is the one that the weather service makes. Two storms in a week is unusual and it is not unprecedented. Record shows a September in the 1970s with three. The warning system is the part that has improved. A message that reaches a phone an hour before landfall tells a household to move a car to higher ground. Those who received it moved the car.

Sandbags ran out in the shops on the sixteenth. A hardware store that stocks two hundred for the season sold them in a day, and the second storm found the shelves empty and the customers calling the district office. The two storms are counted together in the statistics. A season that produces one landfall in Shanghai is normal, and a season that produces two in four days is the kind of event that the climate models are asked to explain. The clean-up was faster the second time. A crew that had learned which streets flood and which pumps fail started in the right places, and the city was dry on the twenty-first. By the end of the month the repair bills had been sent, the two storms were a single line in the seasonal report, and the pumps had been tested again.
