---
title: "Europe wins the Ryder Cup at home again"
date: 2023-09-29 23:35:00 +0800
categories: [sport]
description: "Europe beat the United States 16.5 to 11.5 at the Ryder Cup outside Rome on 1 October 2023, extending a home winning run that the American team has not broken since 1993."
---

Europe won the Ryder Cup at Marco Simone Golf and Country Club outside Rome, taking the singles session on the final afternoon to finish at 16.5 points against 11.5, and the winning putt was holed by Rory McIlroy. The team had led from the first morning, when it won all four of the foursomes matches, and it never surrendered the advantage across three days of play. Luke Donald, who was appointed captain after the previous captain was removed, became the first European captain to win the trophy in his first attempt since 2010.

Course was built for the home team. Marco Simone is tight, with thick rough and fast greens, and the Europeans knew it because the course was designed with their input, and the Americans arrived without having played a competitive round on it. The setup produced the kind of golf that the Ryder Cup has produced in Europe for three decades: long putts, loud crowds and a home team that made the putts when they mattered.

American team looked stronger on paper and lost the sessions that mattered. Its players included the world number one and several major winners, and its captain, Zach Johnson, made choices in the foursomes that were criticized afterwards, including leaving some of his best players out of a session. The team's only bright spot was the singles play of its younger players, several of whom won their matches comfortably, and by the time the last match reached the sixteenth green the contest was over, with the crowd already singing.

Noise followed the golf, as it now does at this event. A caddie was heckled after a group of spectators removed their hats and waved them at him, the American team's wives were abused from behind the ropes, and the European captain said afterwards that the crowd had crossed a line in a few places. The tour has been dealing with the aftermath of the agreement between the European and American tours and the Saudi-backed series, which has made the competition a stage for an argument about who plays where.

Result extends a pattern that the Americans have been unable to break. Europe has won every home Ryder Cup since 1993, and the United States has won every one held on its own soil in the same period bar one in 2012, which makes the captain's picks and the course setup more important than the rankings. The next edition will be in New York in 2025, and the American team will be asked why it cannot win away from home.

A team that was supposed to be weaker won by five points in front of a crowd that treated the week as a festival, and its captain was carried around the eighteenth green by his players. The cup returns to Europe for another two years, and the argument about why the Americans cannot win on the road will continue until the next one, in New York, where the crowd will be on the other side.
