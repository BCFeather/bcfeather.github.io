title: "The model that answers in sentences"
date: 2023-03-14 23:30:00 +0800
categories: [technology]
description: "OpenAI announced GPT-4 on 14 March 2023, a model with a context window of 8,192 tokens, a version with 32,768 tokens, and the ability to accept images as input as well as text."
---

The announcement on 14 March came in a blog post and a technical report, and it described a model rather than a product. GPT-4 was a system that could take text and images as input and produce text, and its release was accompanied by a demonstration of its ability to answer questions about a diagram. The company said it had trained the model on a larger and more curated corpus than its predecessor, and it published a benchmark table rather than a training description.

Benchmarks were the part of the release that the industry read first. The model answered a standardized examination covering fifty-seven subjects at a level above the average human candidate, and it scored well on tests of reading comprehension and of mathematical reasoning. The report also said the model had reduced its rate of confident errors, which is the failure mode that had made the previous version difficult to use in professional settings.

Context ran to 8,192 tokens, and a second version accepted 32,768, which is about fifty pages of text. The window is the amount of material the model can consider at once, and its size determines whether a document can be summarized in one pass or has to be broken into pieces. The larger version was offered to a limited set of customers, and the pricing was three cents per thousand tokens for input and six cents for output.

Cautious framing marked the company's own account, and the coverage was louder. It said the model would still make mistakes, that it should be used with human review, and that its ability to reason about a problem did not extend to the problems that require a proof. The caution was written into the report's limitations section, and the section was shorter than the list of things the model could do, which is how a technical document communicates optimism.

The reception was shaped by the previous six months. A competing system had been released in the autumn and had produced a wave of enthusiasm and a wave of plagiarism cases, and the new model arrived into a market that had already decided what the category was worth. Within a month the company's enterprise customers had signed contracts for a product that did not exist when the year began, and the venture capital that had fled the sector in 2022 had returned. Enterprise contracts signed in the spring were for a product whose terms of use were still being drafted.

What the release changed was the register of the conversation about machine intelligence. A model that could write a plausible paragraph had been a novelty in 2022, and a model that could read a page and answer a question about it was a tool in 2023. The gap between the two was smaller than the gap in how they were described, and the description became the thing that companies were selling. The company had also begun to sell access through an interface that a customer could point at a document, which was the feature that made it useful to a law firm.
