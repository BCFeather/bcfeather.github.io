The solar term that marks the end of summer fell on the twenty-second of August. Chu Shu means the heat withdraws, which is a description of the average year rather than of this one.

The forecast for the week was thirty-four degrees in the river valley. A high of thirty-four in late August is not unusual. The overnight low is what makes it hard. A night that stays above twenty-eight keeps the buildings warm until the morning.

The grid keeps its summer peak until the middle of September. The peak hour has moved later in the past decade, from two in the afternoon to eight in the evening, because the load is now air conditioning in flats rather than machinery in factories.

The evening peak is the harder one to meet. A plant can be asked to run at night. A household cannot be asked to switch off at eight. The reserve that covers the evening is made of pumped storage and of gas turbines that run for two hours.

The rain arrives in bursts. A thunderstorm that drops twenty millimetres in an hour cools the street for a day and floods the underpass for two. The drainage in an old district was built for a storm that returns every three years.

The farmers read the term differently. A field that is ripening needs dry weather. A week of rain in late August damages the grain before the harvest. The term that ends the heat also begins the month that the harvest is counted in.

The schools open in the first week of September. A classroom that has been shut for two months is unbearable in the first week. The schedule puts the physical education class in the morning for that reason.

The autumn is shorter than it used to be. A city that had six weeks between the heat and the cold now has four. The season that the poets wrote about is the one that has changed most.

The term is a date rather than a temperature. The calendar divides the year into twenty-four parts of fifteen days. The parts were fixed for a climate that moved more slowly than this one.

The city is hotter than the field beside it. A district of concrete and asphalt holds the heat of the day and releases it after sunset, and the difference between the centre and the edge of the built-up area is five degrees on a still night.

The installer is the busiest trade in the month. An air conditioning unit that is bought in July is fitted in August, and the technician who fits it works from seven to seven with a list of forty addresses.

The tariff is stepped and the third step is the one that matters. A household that uses more than four hundred units pays a rate that doubles, and the bill that arrives in September is the one that the family argues about.

The heat broke in the first week of September. A front came down from the north and the high fell by ten degrees in two days, which is the way the season ends in this part of the country.
