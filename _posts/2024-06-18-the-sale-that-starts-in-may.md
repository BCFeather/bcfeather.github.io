The mid-year sale reaches its loudest day in the middle of June. The promotion was announced in May and the discounts were fixed in April. A brand that wants a place on the front page of an app has to commit to a price months before it knows the demand.

The mechanism is a deposit. A buyer pays a small sum in late May to reserve a price, then pays the rest in the sale window. The deposit makes the buyer committed and it gives the platform a forecast. A brand that collects more deposits than it has stock cancels the extra orders and refunds the deposits.

The subsidies this year came from a government scheme rather than from the platforms. A household that trades in an old appliance for a new one receives a share of the price back. The scheme covers air conditioners, refrigerators, washing machines and cars, and the paperwork runs through the retailer.

The parcels are the visible part. The sorting centres hire temporary staff for three weeks. A courier who normally delivers a hundred and fifty parcels a day delivers two hundred and fifty. The warehouses run two shifts and the vans run until midnight.

The returns arrive a week later. A shopper who buys three sizes of the same shirt returns two of them. The rate of return on clothing is high enough that the platforms price it into the discount. A brand that sells a dress for ninety yuan has already allowed for the cost of taking it back.

The live streams are where the volume concentrates. A host with a large following sells for six hours without a break. The count of viewers is displayed beside the price and the count is what the buyer is responding to. A room with fifty thousand people will clear a stock of ten thousand units in a quarter of an hour.

The shops on the street have adapted by joining the sale. A chain store offers the same discount as its app and accepts the online voucher at the counter. The customer who walks in has usually checked the price already, which is why the staff no longer explain the offer.

The sale is a fixed point in the retail calendar now. It began as a single company's anniversary and it has become an industry-wide date. The discount is real for some categories and nominal for others, and a shopper who compares two screens will find out which.

The labour behind the parcels is temporary. A sorting centre hires students and agency workers for the three weeks of the sale. The shift runs ten hours and the pay is counted by the parcel. A worker who lasts the whole period is offered a permanent contract at the end, which is how the operators keep the ones who are good.

The prices are monitored by the regulator. A rule requires the seller to show the lowest price of the past thirty days beside the discount. A brand that raised its price in May and cut it in June has to disclose both numbers. The rule was written after a year of complaints and it has changed how the promotions are designed.
