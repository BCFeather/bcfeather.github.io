title: "An eighth Ballon d'Or for Messi"
date: 2023-10-31 22:50:00 +0800
categories: [sport]
description: "Lionel Messi won his eighth Ballon d'Or in Paris on 30 October 2023, beating Erling Haaland and Kylian Mbappe, and Aitana Bonmati took the women's award after Spain won the World Cup."
---

Lionel Messi collected the Ballon d'Or for the eighth time on 30 October, extending a record that he has held alone since 2021, and the vote was closer than the margin suggests. He finished ahead of Erling Haaland, who scored 52 goals in a season that ended with a treble at Manchester City, and Kylian Mbappe, who was third. The award recognizes the 2022-23 season, which for Messi ended with a World Cup winner's medal and for Haaland ended with three trophies.

The voting rules explain part of the result. The jury of journalists from the hundred countries that vote weighs individual performance, team achievement and fair play, and the World Cup sits at the top of that hierarchy. Messi's tournament in Qatar, with seven goals and the decisive performance in the final, dominates a comparison with a club season in which he played in a weaker league and his team exited the Champions League early. That hierarchy has been criticized for weighting a four-week tournament above nine months of club football.

Haaland's case rests on volume and on prizes. Fifty-two goals in all competitions is a number only a handful of players have reached, and the treble that City won included the club's first European Cup. The counterargument is that City's system created many of those chances and that the team's structure, rather than one player's brilliance, is what won the trophies, which is a hard argument to make against a forward who scored in every competition. He also finished as the top scorer in the Champions League and in the Premier League.

Aitana Bonmati took the women's award, having won the World Cup with Spain and the Champions League with Barcelona, and she has become the most decorated player of her generation at club level. The award for the best young player went to Jude Bellingham, the goalkeeper's prize to Emiliano Martinez, and Manchester City was named club of the year. The ceremony has expanded its categories steadily, and the men's and women's awards now sit at the same level in the sport's calendar.

That result also measures what a career is worth in an era when the calendar has multiplied. Messi has now won the prize in 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2015, 2019, 2021 and 2023, and the gaps between those years track the teams he played in rather than any decline in his own performance. The move to Miami in the summer, which took him out of European competition, did not affect the vote because the window closed before it.

For Haaland the question is when rather than whether. He is 23, he plays for the best team in Europe, and he has a contract that runs for years. The Ballon d'Or has gone to a player from the Premier League only twice in the past two decades, and the Norwegian will need either a World Cup or another season like the last one to break the pattern. The award will be argued about in Norway for a year, and the next tournament will give the answer.
