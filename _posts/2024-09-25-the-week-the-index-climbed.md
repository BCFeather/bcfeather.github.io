---
title: "The week the index climbed"
date: 2024-09-25 21:30:00 +0800
categories: [business]
description: "The main index rose for four sessions after the package was announced, and the turnover on one day was the largest in the history of the exchange. The buyers were domestic, and the sellers were the ones who had waited a year."
---

Main index rose for four sessions after the package of the twenty-fourth of September. Turnover on one of those days was the largest that the exchange has recorded. The buying was domestic. A brokerage reported that the accounts opened in the week were mostly retail. Institutional money that had been short of the market for a year was slow to return.

Foreign investor was a smaller part of the flow than the headline suggests. A fund that had reduced its position in the spring bought back a share of it. Buying was in the large caps rather than in the small ones. The sectors that rose first were the ones the package touched. A broker, a developer and a bank rose on the first day. Order reflects the sequence of the measures rather than the fundamentals of the companies.

Turnover is the number traders watch. A market that trades a trillion yuan in a day is a market with a lot of people who want to own it for a week. Same number in a month of falls means nothing. The short seller was restricted during the rally. A rule that limits the sale of borrowed stock when the index moves sharply was applied twice in the week. Effect was to remove a source of selling.

Margin balance rose. A trader who borrows to buy posts a deposit and pays a fee. Balance that is published each week rose by an amount that is small against the total. The valuation is still low against its own history. A market that trades at ten times earnings in September traded at thirty in 2007. Rally returned it to twelve rather than to thirty.

Policy made the move and earnings give reason to doubt it. A company that reports lower profit in October will not be helped by a rate cut in September. Analysts who raised their targets did so for the multiple rather than for the earnings. The retail investor is the one who carries the risk. A household that bought in the fourth session at a price that was twenty per cent higher than the week before is the last buyer in, and the ones who bought first are the ones who sold to them.

Funds that track the index saw inflows. A product that a retail investor can buy in an app received more money in a week than in the previous quarter, and the buying was concentrated in the largest names. Exchange itself benefits from the volume. A fee that is charged on every trade rises with the turnover, and the shares of the brokers rose on the same day as the index. The market closed the week higher than it opened and lower than the peak of the third day. The pattern of a rally that starts with a package and ends with a profit warning is the pattern that the older traders have seen before. By the following Monday the turnover had fallen back, the index had given up a third of the gain, and the argument about whether the package had changed anything had begun.
