---
title: "Real Madrid wins a fourteenth European Cup with one goal in Paris"
date: 2022-05-28 23:50:00 +0800
categories: [sport]
description: "Vinícius Júnior scored in the fifty-ninth minute at the Stade de France on 28 May, Thibaut Courtois made nine saves, and a final that had started thirty-six minutes late because of ticketless fans ended with Real Madrid's fourth title in nine seasons and Liverpool's third defeat in a final that season."
---

Real Madrid beat Liverpool 1-0 at the Stade de France on 28 May to win the European Cup for the fourteenth time, a goal from Vinícius Júnior in the fifty-ninth minute separating the teams. Thibaut Courtois made nine saves, more than any goalkeeper in a Champions League final since records began, and was named man of the match. The kick-off was delayed by thirty-six minutes because thousands of supporters without tickets had forced their way into the approaches to the stadium, and the police used tear gas outside the ground while the players waited inside.

Liverpool had the better of the match by most measures. They took twenty-four shots to Real's four, hit the frame of the goal, and forced Courtois into a series of saves that ranged from the routine to the extraordinary, including one from Mohamed Salah in the first half and two from close range after the interval. Real's goal came from a move that had been their pattern all season: a ball into the space behind a high line, a cut-back, and a finish from the player arriving at the far post.

The season had been built on comebacks rather than control. Real had been two goals down to Paris Saint-Germain in the previous round and won the tie in the last minutes, had gone behind to Chelsea at the Bernabéu and survived, and had been minutes from elimination against Manchester City in the semi-final before scoring twice in added time and once in extra time. Karim Benzema finished the campaign as the competition's leading scorer with fifteen goals, and the pattern of refusing to lose became the team's defining quality.

For Carlo Ancelotti the match produced a record: he became the first coach to win the European Cup four times, having lifted it twice with Milan and once with Real in 2014. He had returned to the club in 2021 after a decade away, in a season that began with the departure of Sergio Ramos and Raphaël Varane and a squad that the club's own president described as being in transition. The transition ended with a league title won by four points with four games remaining and this cup.

Liverpool's season ended with two trophies and two defeats in finals. They had won the League Cup and the FA Cup on penalties, had taken the Premier League title race to the last day and finished a point behind Manchester City, and lost the Champions League final in Paris. Jürgen Klopp's team played the final in the manner that had taken them to three finals in five seasons, pressing high and creating chances, and the difference on the night was a goalkeeper and a deflection that went the other way.

Outside the stadium, the French government ordered an inquiry into the policing of the event and the distribution of tickets, which had been sold in a system that left genuine holders queueing at gates that closed. Inside it, a team that had spent the spring winning matches it should have lost won the one that mattered, and the fourteenth cup was lifted by a captain, Marcelo, who had been at the club since 2007.
