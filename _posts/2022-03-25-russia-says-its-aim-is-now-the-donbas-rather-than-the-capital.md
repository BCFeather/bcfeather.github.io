---
title: "Russia says its aim is now the Donbas rather than the capital"
date: 2022-03-25 23:50:00 +0800
categories: [news]
description: "A general announces that the first phase is complete and the war will concentrate in the east, and the troops around Kyiv stay where they are."
---

A deputy chief of the Russian general staff told a briefing in Moscow on 25 March that the first phase of the operation was complete and that the main goal was now the Donbas. Sergei Rudskoy said the armed forces had "generally" fulfilled the tasks of the first stage, that the combat capability of the Ukrainian army had been significantly reduced, and that the units would concentrate on the liberation of the Donbas. He did not say that the advance towards Kyiv, Chernihiv and Sumy had failed, and he did not say that the troops there would withdraw.

The announcement marked a change in the way the war was described rather than in the way it was fought. The original aim, as the president had put it in February, had been to demilitarize and denazify the whole country and to change its government, and the columns that advanced on the capital carried the expectation of a parade. Five weeks later the government in Kyiv was intact, the Russian army had lost more armour than it had expected, and the front around the capital had become a series of positions in the suburbs.

Troops did not move at once. Fighting continued around Kyiv through the last week of March, and the shelling of Chernihiv and Kharkiv intensified. The change that the briefing anticipated came at the end of the month, when the Russian ministry said it would reduce its activity around the capital and concentrate on the east, and the troops that had occupied the towns north of Kyiv withdrew in the first week of April. The withdrawal left behind the bodies of civilians in Bucha, which turned the announcement into the prelude to the war's most documented atrocity.

The east had been the objective of the second front since the beginning. The separatist republics of Donetsk and Luhansk had been at war with Kyiv since 2014, and the Russian army had recognized them as independent states in February, which gave the campaign in the Donbas a legal frame that the advance on Kyiv never had. The offensive that began in April aimed to take the whole of Luhansk and Donetsk, and the battle for Sievierodonetsk and Lysychansk consumed the summer.

Western officials read the announcement as an admission of failure and as a search for something that could be called a victory. The Institute for the Study of War described it as a change in the definition of success rather than in the plan, and the British defence ministry said the Russian forces had been forced to reduce their objectives because of the losses. The Russian side presented it as a decision, which is what a general does when the alternative is a sentence about a mistake.

What the statement fixed was the shape of the war for the rest of the year. From the last week of March the campaign was about the east and the south, about the land bridge and the ports, and about a front that moved slowly and cost thousands of lives per kilometre. The capital was left alone, the war was given a border that could be defended, and the figure of the second phase entered the vocabulary that both sides used until the autumn.
