---
title: "The box that costs more than the cake"
date: 2024-09-09 20:50:00 +0800
categories: [business]
description: "A rule on mooncake packaging limits the box to three layers and fifteen per cent of the price. The rule has changed the shelf, and it has not changed the habit of buying a box to give away."
---

Mooncake season begins in August and ends on the day of the festival, the seventeenth of September. Shelf in a supermarket has been rearranged three times since June. The packaging is the part that is regulated. A rule introduced last year limits the box to three layers and to fifteen per cent of the retail price. It bans the combination of a cake box with a tea set or a wine bottle.

Rule has changed the box. A tin that used to weigh more than the cakes inside is now a cardboard sleeve. Shelf space that held lacquer boxes in September now holds paper. The price has not changed much. A box of eight cakes sells for a hundred and sixty yuan in a department store and for forty in a supermarket. Difference is in the brand rather than in the cake.

Gift is the transaction that matters. A household buys one box to eat and four to give. Four are chosen for the receiver rather than for the taste. A box that is recognisable is worth more than a box that is good. The corporate order is the largest single item. A company that buys three hundred boxes for its staff orders them in July and has them delivered to the office. Invoice is booked as a welfare cost.

Vouchers are sold separately. A card that can be exchanged for a box in any store of a chain is the form that a gift takes when the giver does not know the receiver's taste. Card is resold at a discount in the week after the festival. The unsold stock is the part that the industry does not discuss. A cake that is not sold by the day of the festival is sold to a discount channel or returned to the factory. A factory that receives a return can reuse the filling and not the pastry.

Counter-example is the old shop. A bakery that has made the same five kinds for forty years sells out in a week and does not advertise, and its queue in the morning is made of people who have bought there since they were children. The new flavours are a marketing exercise. A cake with a filling of cheese or of coffee is produced for a photograph and sold for a season. Survivors are the ones a family will buy twice.

Export is small and it is growing. A box that is sold to a Chinese community abroad carries a label in a language that the buyer reads, and the shipment leaves in August to arrive before the festival. The courier is the bottleneck in the last week. A box that has to arrive on the thirteenth is sent on the tenth, and the depot that handles a million parcels a day in a county town adds a night shift for the season. The festival ends and the shelf changes within a day. The boxes are gone on the eighteenth and the space is taken by the national day promotion, which is a week away and a larger season.
