---
title: "Ukrainian troops reach Izium and the Russian army pulls back from the north-east"
date: 2022-09-11 23:45:00 +0800
categories: [news]
description: "A week of advances in the Kharkiv region takes back Balakliia and Kupiansk and forces Moscow to describe a retreat as a regrouping."
---

Ukrainian units entered Izium on Sunday morning, and by the afternoon the flag was on the town hall and the road south to the Donbas was in range of their artillery. The advance had begun ten days earlier at Balakliia, where a thin line of Russian-held positions collapsed within a day, and it continued along the corridor that runs east from Kharkiv. Kupiansk, the railway junction that had supplied the Russian army in the north-east, fell on Saturday. The Russian defence ministry said its troops had left the area to regroup in the Donetsk region.

The operation was the fastest movement of the war for Ukraine's army. Armoured columns moved along the roads rather than clearing every village, and the reserves that Russia had moved to the south in the summer left the north-east thin. Analysts said the command had kept the plan quiet even from its own allies, and that the units involved had been trained for a breakthrough. The president put the area retaken since the start of the month at several thousand square kilometres and said the count was rising each day.

Moscow's response was a statement about withdrawal. The ministry published a map with arrows pointing east, and its spokesman said the units had been redeployed to strengthen the front around Donetsk. Commentators in Moscow described the announcement as a defeat that the state had decided to call a decision. The retreat also left behind ammunition, vehicles and documents, and Ukrainian officials said the depots they found were enough to supply their own units for weeks.

The political effect was immediate in the capitals that have been supplying the army. The American secretary of state said the advance showed that the weapons were being used well, and he announced another package within hours. The German foreign minister said the argument that Ukraine could not win had been answered on the ground. In Kyiv, the government said the offensive proved that the war could be fought on the terms it had chosen rather than on the terms the Kremlin had set.

Cost of the advance is visible in the towns it passed through. Balakliia has lost its gas supply and most of its water, and the hospital is running on a generator. Villages along the corridor were emptied in the spring and have not been repopulated, and the fields are full of unexploded shells. The local authorities began to clear mines and to restore the substations within a day, and they asked residents not to return until the sappers had been through.

Nothing about the campaign is finished. The Russian army still holds the south and the east, and its units in the Donbas are dug in along lines that have not moved since 2015. The Ukrainian general staff says the next target is the supply line through the south, and that the weather will decide how long the offensive can continue. The winter arrives in this part of the country in November, and the roads turn to mud two weeks before the frost.
