FAQ

Frequently asked questions about this blog, its content and how it is produced.

Where do the articles on this blog come from?

They come from several sources: pieces that I write myself, notes and summaries from my conversations with AI and other people, and curated or aggregated reporting drawn from the news media. I aim to cross-reference facts across these sources and present them with enough context to stand on their own. AI might be used to refine sentences if I think it's necessary. Also, the experiences written in the essays could be imaginary.

How often is the blog updated?

There is no fixed schedule. I publish when an event or a question feels worth thinking through slowly rather than chasing a headline. In most cases a single day brings one or two posts, while some other days may pass quietly.

What does the blog cover?

I write mainly about current affairs and social phenomena, tracing the hidden logic beneath surface events, and about advances in technology, with a particular focus on artificial intelligence and the life sciences. The underlying interest is how structures, narratives, emerging technologies and individual lives intersect.

Are the views here official or institutional?

No. Every article expresses my personal reflection and is separate from any institutional stance. Where facts are reported, I try to attribute them clearly; where interpretation is offered, it is mine.

Can I quote or share the articles?

Yes, for non-commercial purposes, provided you credit the author and link back to the original post. For republication or commercial use, please get in touch first.

How can I get in touch?

You can leave a comment on any post, or use the contact details on the About page. I welcome calm, respectful discussion.
