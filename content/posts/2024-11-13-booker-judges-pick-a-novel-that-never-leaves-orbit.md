Judges gave the Booker Prize to a novel of a little over a hundred pages that takes place in low orbit, follows six astronauts through sixteen sunrises, and has almost no plot in the sense that a reader expects. The judges described it as a book about the planet seen from outside, and the award did what awards occasionally do: it made a quiet book the subject of a loud argument. A hundred pages is a length that a judge can read twice, and the second reading is where the craft shows.

Argument was partly about length. A prize that has often gone to a long novel of family and history went to a short one with no families in it, and the commentary that followed was dominated by the question of whether a hundred pages counts as a novel. The rules do not care, and the readers who bought it did not seem to mind either. A prize that goes to a short book is a prize that will be accused of choosing the experiment over the achievement.

Method is the reason it works. A narrative that stays in one place has to hold attention with observation rather than event, and the observations are of weather systems, of the terminator line between day and night, and of the small routines that keep a crew alive. A reader who expects a plot finds a description, and the description accumulates into something. A crew of six is a cast small enough that every line of dialogue has to carry a character. A sentence that describes a sunrise from orbit is a sentence that has to be exact about the light and the terminator.

Announcement also did something for the market. A short book with a striking cover is a book that a reader can finish on a train, and the sales that followed the announcement were larger than the publisher had planned for. An award that moves units is an award that publishers pay attention to. A cover with a blue planet on it is a cover that a bookseller can put in a window.

Domestic press emphasized the British angle. Harvey is a British writer who had been shortlisted before and had never won, and the prize returned to a home author after several years of international winners. That is a story about a prize rather than about a book, and it is the story the coverage told. A shortlist that included several long novels is a shortlist that the judges had to argue about.

What the award establishes is that the judges will reward a formal experiment when it is executed with enough control. A book that could have been a gimmick became a meditation, and the difference between the two is the quality of the sentences rather than the premise. A meditation that holds for a hundred pages is a meditation that had to be edited hard.
