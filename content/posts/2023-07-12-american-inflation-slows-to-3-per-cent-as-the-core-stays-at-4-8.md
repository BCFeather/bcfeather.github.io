title: "American inflation slows to 3 per cent as the core stays at 4.8"
date: "2023-07-12T09:20:00+08:00"
categories: [economy]
description: "The June consumer prices rose by the smallest amount in more than two years, while the measure excluding the food and the energy fell only a little."
---

American consumer prices rose by 3 per cent over the year to June, down from 4 per cent in May and the smallest increase since March 2021. The monthly change was 0.2 per cent, and the core measure rose by 0.2 on the month and by 4.8 over the year, down from 5.3. The figures were a little below the forecast. The figure is the twelfth consecutive month of decline from the peak of 9.1 per cent, and it is the first at the level of the target since the spring of 2021. The measure is about 3 per cent on the three-month annualized basis, which is the number the committee has said it looks at.

Energy did the work again. The energy index fell by about 16.7 per cent over the year, and the petrol by about 26, as the base of the summer of 2022 dropped out. The electricity rose by about 0.9 per cent on the month, and the utility gas fell by about 2.6 as the heating season ended. The fuel oil fell by about 20 per cent over the year, and the one of the propane by about 15. The crude was about 75 dollars a barrel at the end of June, which is about 30 per cent below the level of a year ago.

Shelter slowed. The rent rose by about 0.4 per cent and the owners' equivalent rent by about 0.4, which is the smallest monthly gain since the end of 2021. The measure is still about 7.8 per cent higher than a year ago, and the market rents have been falling for a year. The measure for the new tenants has been rising by about 1.5 per cent, and the gap with the stock is the reason the index is still high.

Used cars fell. The prices dropped by about 0.5 per cent after two months of gains, and the wholesale prices suggest another decline in July. The new cars were flat, and the car insurance rose by about 1.7 per cent as the insurers repriced the repairs. The car insurance has risen by about 17 per cent over the year, and the one of the repairs by about 12.

Services were mixed. The air fares fell by about 8 per cent on the month, and the one of the hotels by about 2, while the medical care rose by about 0.4. The food away from home rose by about 0.4, and the food at home was flat for a third month. The health insurance measure fell again, and the statisticians have said the method will keep it falling into next year.

Markets took it as the last piece before July. The traders held the odds of a rise on 26 July at about 90 per cent, and the two-year yield fell by about 12 basis points. A headline at 3 per cent with the core at 4.8 is the split that the committee has been describing, and the core is the part that the meeting will weigh. The committee meets on 25 and 26 July, and the market expects the rate to peak at about 5.5 per cent.
