The mosquito that carries dengue in southern China is Aedes albopictus, a species that breeds in small containers rather than in marshes: a flower pot, a discarded tyre, a bucket left under a tap. A wet spring fills those containers, and the larvae that hatch in them develop faster in warm weather.

This year the first generation emerged about two weeks earlier than usual. The winter was mild, the spring rain was heavy, and the temperatures in May were high enough that the development time from egg to adult shortened.
