South Korea's president declared martial law shortly before eleven at night on 3 December, citing a parliament that had blocked his budget and impeached officials, and within six hours the order had been lifted. Soldiers that had been sent towards the national assembly withdrew after lawmakers climbed into the chamber and voted. A decree issued at night is a decree that assumes the opposition is asleep. A declaration that arrives after a budget vote is a declaration aimed at the assembly rather than at an external enemy.

Vote was unanimous among those present. An assembly that can convene in defiance of a decree is an assembly that has the legal authority to revoke it, and the constitution gives the legislature that power explicitly. A president who declares martial law without the assembly's consent is a president who has tested a clause that the drafters assumed would never be used. A chamber that fills within two hours is a chamber whose members had planned for the contingency. A chamber that convenes at one in the morning is a chamber whose members had discussed the contingency.

Military's behavior decided the outcome. Troops deployed to the building but did not force entry, and the commander's reluctance to fire on civilians was the difference between a decree and a coup. An army that hesitates is an army that has decided where its loyalty ends. A commander who waits for a lawful order is a commander who has read the constitution. A garrison that follows the constitution is a garrison that decides the outcome before the politicians do.

Economic consequence was immediate. The currency fell to a level it had not reached in more than a decade, and the central bank and the finance ministry spent the following days assuring markets. A country that is a major exporter of semiconductors and cars is a country whose politics is priced in the won. A currency that falls is a currency that prices a political risk the country had not carried.

Legal aftermath is the longer story. The assembly impeached the president on 14 December, and the constitutional court has a fixed period to decide whether to remove him from office. A process that runs on a statutory clock is a process that keeps the government in limbo for months. A court that has six months to rule is a court whose calendar becomes the country's calendar. A finance ministry that promises stability is a ministry that has already called the central bank.

What the episode establishes is that institutions can bind a leader who tests them. A decree that lasts six hours is a decree that failed, and the failure was produced by a parliament and an army that declined to follow. A president who is suspended is a president whose party has to find a candidate. A president who is suspended is a president whose party must find a candidate within sixty days if the court agrees.
