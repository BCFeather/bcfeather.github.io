categories: [business]
description: "The quarter to June fell by 1 per cent on the hardware, while the services reached an all-time high and the iPhone held in the largest market."
---

Apple reported a revenue of 81.8 billion dollars for the quarter to June, down 1 per cent, and a net profit of 19.9 billion. The earnings per share of 1.26 dollars beat the forecast of 1.20. The shares fell about 2 per cent as the revenue in China was a little weaker than the market had expected. The revenue was about 800 million above the estimate, and the constant currency growth was about 3 per cent. The company said the wearables fell by about 4 per cent, and the one of the home products by about 6.

Services did the work. The revenue from the services was about 21.2 billion dollars, which is a record, and the one from the iPhone about 39.7, down about 2 per cent. The Mac fell by about 7, and the iPad by about 20 as the tablet market stayed weak. The services grew by about 8 per cent, and the company said the advertising and the cloud had been the fastest parts. The number of the paid subscriptions was about 1 billion, up about 150 million over the year. The iPad revenue was about 5.8 billion dollars, and the one of the Mac about 6.8.

China held. The revenue in the greater China was about 15.8 billion dollars, up about 8 per cent, and the company said the iPhone had been the best-selling model in the cities. The upgrade rate was about the same as a year ago, and the company said the installed base was at a record. The revenue in the Americas was about 35 billion dollars, down about 1 per cent, and the one in Europe about 20.

Margins rose. The gross margin was about 44.5 per cent, up about 1 point as the services mix improved, and the operating margin was about 30 per cent. The company said the currency had subtracted about 4 points from the revenue. The company returned about 23 billion dollars to the shareholders in the quarter, and the cash was about 167 billion.

Guidance was quiet. The company did not give a revenue forecast for the current quarter, which is the practice it has followed for two years, and it said the growth would be similar to the June quarter. The market had expected about 90 billion dollars. The company said the September quarter would be the one with the new phone, and the launch is expected in the middle of the month. The company said the gross margin for the current quarter would be about 44 to 45 per cent, and the services would grow at about the same pace.

This quarter is the one the services carry. A company that earns 19.9 billion dollars while the hardware falls is a company whose installed base is the product, and the base is the part that the next decade of the growth sits on. The shares are up about 40 per cent this year, and the company is the most valuable in the world.
