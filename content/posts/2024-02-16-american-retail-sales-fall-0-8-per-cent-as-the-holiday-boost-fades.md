American retail sales fell 0.8 per cent in January from the month before, the largest decline in a year, according to the figures published on 15 February. The control group, which excludes cars, petrol and building materials, fell 0.4 per cent. The figure was well below the forecast, and the revision to December was small, which means the weakness is in the new month rather than in the old.

Timing explains part of it. The holiday spending was concentrated in December, and the January figures were compared with a month that had been unusually strong. A fall after a strong month is a fall that the seasonal adjustment cannot fully absorb.
