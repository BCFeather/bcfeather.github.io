Donald Trump announced Senator JD Vance as his running mate on 15 July, at the opening of the Republican convention in Milwaukee, and the choice was read as a decision about the party's future rather than about the election's present. Vance is a first-term senator who had won a statewide race once. A selection announced on the first day of a convention is a selection that the nominee had made before the delegates arrived.

Biography is the message. A candidate who grew up in a declining industrial town and wrote a book about it is a candidate who can speak to the voters the party has been losing, and the convention used the biography in every speech. A story that is told well is a story that substitutes for a policy. A book that explains a region to the rest of the country is a book that a politician can use for a decade. The book had been made into a film, which meant that the story was already familiar to voters who had never read it.

Reversal is the record. Vance had described the nominee as unfit for office in 2016 and had been elected with his endorsement in 2022, and the change is the kind that the party has normalized. A movement that rewards loyalty over consistency is a movement that treats a reversal as a qualification. A party that has already forgiven a reversal is a party that will forgive the next one, which is what the critics fear.

Senate seat is the arithmetic. A candidate in his first term gives up a seat that the party may not hold in a special election, and the governor who fills it is from the same party but not from the same faction. A selection that spends a seat is a selection that the party has priced. A governor who fills a Senate seat is a governor who owes the appointment to the faction that won the primary.

Donors were the other audience. A running mate who can talk to a technology investor and to a factory worker in the same sentence is a running mate who reassures two wings of a coalition, and the fundraising that followed the announcement was reported as strong. A ticket is a product that has to appeal to more than one buyer. A ticket that reassures two wings is a ticket that has to keep both of them in the same room for three months.

What the selection establishes is that the party has chosen a successor as well as a partner. A vice-presidential nominee who is forty years old is a nominee who is auditioning for the next two elections, and the convention was organized around that. A convention that is organized around a succession is a convention that has stopped arguing about the past. The party has four years to decide whether the succession is settled or open, and the convention did not settle it.
