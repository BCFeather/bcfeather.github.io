The holiday ended on a Tuesday, and the last day of it was spent buying the things that the term required: two exercise books, a new set of pens, a geometry box whose compass had lost its point. The shops near the school gate were full of people doing the same arithmetic, and the woman behind the counter had put the boxes on the counter in advance. The list was written on a piece of paper my mother had kept from the previous year, and the prices had moved by a few yuan on each line.

School began again on the first day of February, which meant that January had been a month of two halves: the examinations at the start and the holiday at the end, with the new year in between. The holiday had been long enough to forget the timetable and short enough to remember it again by the last week. The examinations had taken four days and the holiday had taken three weeks, and between them they had used up most of January.

On the first morning the classroom was colder than it had been in December, because the heating had been off for a month and the building had taken the cold into its walls. We sat in the coats we had worn in the examinations and waited for the radiator to do its work, and the teacher came in with a stack of papers and a sentence about the term.

Every term in the last year begins with a sentence about June, and the sentence gets shorter as the year goes on. Every term in the last year begins with a sentence about June, and the sentence gets shorter as the year goes on. In January it was a paragraph with dates in it; by the spring it would be a number written on the corner of the board.

I had put the new books in the desk and arranged the pens in the order I liked, which is a ritual that has no function and which I performed every term. The desk itself had a mark on the surface that I had made in the first year, and the mark was still there, and the fact that it was still there was the closest thing the room had to a record. The pens were in a row and the books were stacked in the order of the timetable, and the order lasted until the first break.

February would be shorter than January and would not feel shorter. There would be a mock examination at the end of it, and the mock would be marked by people who had already marked the real thing for years, and the marks would be taken seriously in a way that the January marks had not been. The last day of the holiday is always the day on which the year becomes finite. I went to bed early, with the alarm set, and the number in my head was the one that the board would show in the morning.
