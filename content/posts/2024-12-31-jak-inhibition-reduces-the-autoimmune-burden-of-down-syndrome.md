Down syndrome results from trisomy 21, and the extra chromosome brings immune dysregulation along with the developmental phenotype. A paper in *eLife* on 31 December 2024 reports a trial of a JAK inhibitor in that population. Interferon signalling is the pathway that the extra copies of interferon-receptor genes push upward. Gene dosage is an unusually clean rationale for a drug, because the pathway is up for a reason that can be counted. Interferon is also the pathway that defends against viruses, so a drug that lowers it trades one risk for another in a population that is already vulnerable. Counting the cause of the pathway is what makes the rationale explicit, and explicitness is rare in immunology.

A pathway that is up because of gene dosage is a pathway a drug can target. JAK inhibitors block the kinase that transduces interferon signalling, so the pharmacological rationale is direct. Endpoint choice decides what a short trial can show, and autoimmune burden is the endpoint a short trial can move. A short trial can move a symptom score and cannot move cognition, and saying so keeps the claim honest.

The trial measured autoimmune burden rather than cognition, which is the endpoint that a short study can move. Autoimmunity in Down syndrome appears as thyroid disease, coeliac disease and skin conditions, and a burden score that combines them is what the study reports. A composite score is a statistical device as much as a clinical one, and its validity depends on how the components are weighted. Phase two design is where dose finding happens, and this study supplies the effect size that design needs.

A composite endpoint is what a small trial needs to detect an effect. Reduction of the burden is the result. Hair loss and skin inflammation are visible and reversible, which makes them the phenotypes a small study can detect. Trading immune defence for immune calm is the central risk, and a larger trial would quantify it.

Hair loss and skin inflammation are the phenotypes that responded most clearly, and the pattern is consistent with an interferon-driven mechanism. Safety is the constraint, since JAK inhibition suppresses immune defence and the population is already vulnerable to infection. Dose finding under immunosuppression is the hard part, and the hard part is what a phase two design has to address.

A dose that lowers interferon without lowering resistance is the target. The study is small and short, and both limits are stated. Stated limits are what allow a result to be used honestly, and the limits here are size and duration.

Cognition was not the endpoint, so the result does not speak to the developmental phenotype. A larger trial with an infection endpoint is the next step, and the design of that trial is what the current result makes possible. A trial with an infection endpoint is a different study, and the current result is what justifies its cost.
