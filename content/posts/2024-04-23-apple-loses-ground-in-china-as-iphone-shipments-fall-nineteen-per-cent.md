Shipments of iPhones in China fell 19 per cent in the first quarter compared with a year earlier, according to estimates published by a research firm on 23 April, the steepest decline in several years. The company's share of the market slipped behind two local brands. The research firm estimates shipments rather than sales, and the two differ by the inventory that sits in the channel between the factory and the shop.

Price is part of the explanation. The models that sell best in the market cost between two and four thousand yuan, and the cheapest current iPhone sits above that band, which means the segment where volume lives is the segment the company does not serve. A product that competes only at the top is a product that loses when the market moves down. Discounts in the online channels have narrowed that gap, and the company has authorized promotions that it would not have run in earlier years.

Features are the other part. The local brands offer folding screens, faster charging and camera systems that the company has not matched, and they release new models twice a year rather than once. A product cycle that is slower than the local one is a product cycle that spends part of each year looking dated. The software that runs on the phones is the company's strongest asset in the market, and the applications that users cannot get elsewhere are the reason many of them stay.

Installed base is the cushion. The company earns more from services and from the ecosystem than from the handset itself in mature markets, and the users who stay in the ecosystem keep paying even when they postpone an upgrade. A base that is loyal is a base that a shipment number understates. The base is large enough that a small change in the upgrade rate moves more profit than a large change in the share of new buyers, which is why the company watches the replacement cycle.

Second half is the test. The company has said it will bring new intelligence features to the platform, and the market has generally treated that as a reason for users to upgrade, though the features will not be available in the country at launch. A selling point that is delayed is a selling point that the competition can copy. The features will arrive later in the country than elsewhere, and the local competitors have been shipping their own versions of the same functions since the spring.

What the quarter shows is that a premium brand can lose volume without losing profit, and the question for the company is how long that remains true in the market where the next billion users are expected to come from. A shipment number is a photograph of one quarter, and the question that the company has to answer is whether the fall was a pause before a new cycle or a shift in the market's structure.
