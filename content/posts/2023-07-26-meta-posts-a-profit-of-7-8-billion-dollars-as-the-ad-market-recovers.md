description: "The quarter to June grew by 11 per cent, the fastest in a year, and the company raised the forecast for the expenses as it rebuilds the advertising system."
---

Meta reported a revenue of 32 billion dollars for the quarter to June, up 11 per cent, and a net profit of 7.79 billion. The earnings per share of 2.98 dollars beat the forecast of 2.91. The shares rose about 5 per cent after the report, and the company said the ad impressions had grown by about 30 per cent. The revenue was about 700 million above the estimate, and the operating margin was about 29 per cent, up from 21 a year ago. The company said the currency effects had subtracted about 1 percentage point from the revenue. The company said the revenue from the Reels had been about 10 billion dollars on the annual basis, and the gap with the feed had been closing.

Its ad market turned. The revenue from the advertising was about 31.5 billion, up 12 per cent, and the company said the Chinese retailers had been the largest contributor to the growth. The average price per ad fell by about 16 per cent, and the volume did the work. The revenue in the United States and Canada rose by about 12 per cent, and the one in the Asia-Pacific by about 15. The company said the advertisers had been using the AI tools for the targeting, and the return on the spending had improved.

Its users held. The daily active users of the family of the apps were about 3.03 billion, and the one of Facebook was about 2.06 billion. The company said the Reels had been used by about 2 billion people a day, and the monetization of it was about half of the level of the feed. The time spent on the Reels rose by about 20 per cent, and the one on the stories held.

Reality Labs lost less. The unit reported a revenue of about 276 million dollars and an operating loss of about 3.7 billion, down from 4 billion. The company said the losses would grow in the next year as the headset and the glasses were developed. The company has sold about 20 million of the Quest headsets, and the new model is expected in the autumn.

Costs were the news. The company cut the forecast for the expenses this year to about 88 to 91 billion dollars, and it raised the one for the capital spending to about 28 to 30 billion. The headcount was about 71,000, down about 20 per cent from the peak. The company said the headcount would be flat for the rest of the year, and the hiring would be concentrated in the AI.

This quarter is the one the efficiency campaign shows in. A company that grows by 11 per cent while it cuts 20 per cent of the staff is a company that has changed the cost base, and the base is the part the next year rests on. The shares are up about 140 per cent this year, which is the best performance in the large technology group.
