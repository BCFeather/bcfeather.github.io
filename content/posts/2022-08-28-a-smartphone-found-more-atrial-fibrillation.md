Randomization covered 5,551 policyholders of a German health insurer who were free of atrial fibrillation at baseline to digital screening with a certified app on their own smartphones or to usual care, and evaluated abnormal findings with 14-day external electrocardiogram loop recorders. The paper appeared in *Nature Medicine*, 28 August 2022. A certified app on a participant's own phone is a medical device distributed through consumer hardware, and the distribution is the part that makes the trial siteless.

Its primary endpoint was newly diagnosed atrial fibrillation treated with oral anticoagulation by a physician not involved in the study, and it was met: screening more than doubled the detection rate in both phases of the trial, with odds ratios of 2.12 in the first and 2.75 in the second.
