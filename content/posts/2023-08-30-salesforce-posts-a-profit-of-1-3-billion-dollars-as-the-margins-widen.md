Salesforce reported a revenue of 8.6 billion dollars for the quarter to July, up 11 per cent, and a net profit of 1.27 billion. The earnings per share of 2.12 dollars beat the forecast of 1.90. The shares rose about 6 per cent after the report, and the company raised the guidance for the year. The revenue was about 100 million above the estimate, and the constant currency growth was about 11 per cent. The company said the restructuring charge was about 300 million dollars, and the programme would be finished by the end of the year. The company said the free cash flow was about 2.3 billion dollars in the quarter, and the one for the year would be about 8.

Its margin did the work. The operating margin was about 31 per cent on the adjusted basis, up about 8 points, as the company cut about 10 per cent of the staff and the real estate. The company said the target of 30 per cent for the year had been reached a quarter early. The company said the headcount was about 70,000, down about 10 per cent from the peak, and the hiring would resume in the next year.

Subscription revenue grew by about 12 per cent. The sales cloud was about 2 billion dollars, and the one of the service cloud about 2.2. The platform and the other products grew by about 15 as the Data Cloud and the AI features sold. The marketing cloud grew by about 9 per cent, and the one of the commerce by about 12.

Remaining obligations rose. The performance obligations were about 46 billion dollars, up about 12 per cent, and the current portion about 23. The company said the large deals had been closing slower, and the average contract value was about flat. The company said the attrition had fallen to about 10 per cent, which is the lowest in three years.

AI is the pitch. The company said the Einstein and the new GPT features had been included in about 20 per cent of the large deals, and the pricing had been a 10 per cent premium. The management said the customers were still in the testing phase. The company has about 150,000 customers using the Data Cloud, and the one of the AI features is about 5,000.

Its guidance was raised. The company expects the revenue of about 34.7 to 34.9 billion dollars for the year, and the operating margin of about 31.5 per cent. A software company that grows by 11 per cent and lifts the margin by 8 points is a company that has chosen the profit, and the choice is the one the investors demanded. The shares are up about 60 per cent this year, and the company is the best performer in the large application software group.
