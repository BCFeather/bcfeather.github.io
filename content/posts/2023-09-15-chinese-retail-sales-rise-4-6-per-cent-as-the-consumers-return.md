description: "The August figures showed a second month of faster growth in the shops, while industrial output and investment stayed weak against a shrinking property market."
---

Chinese retail sales rose 4.6 per cent in August from a year earlier, the statistics bureau said on 15 September, up from 2.5 per cent in July and the fastest pace since the spring. Industrial output rose 4.5 per cent and the fixed investment rose 0.2 per cent. The figure beat the forecast of about three per cent, and the year-to-date growth of the retail sales is now 7 per cent, which is still below the pace of 2019.

Shops did better than factories did. The recovery has been led by the services and the restaurants, and the catering revenue rose by about 12 per cent as the summer travel season ran its course. A recovery led by the services is a recovery that creates jobs quickly and adds less to the industrial capacity. The travel spending over the summer holidays rose by about a fifth, and the box office took about 20 billion yuan in the season, both records for the period.

Property dragged on everything else. The investment in the residential buildings fell by about 10 per cent, the sales of the new homes fell by about 20, and the developers have been cutting the construction. A building site that does not start is a demand for cement, steel and labour that never appears. The sales of the new homes fell by about a quarter in the first eight months, and the top fifty developers have defaulted on about a trillion yuan of the debt. The investment in the property development fell by about 9 per cent, and the share of the sales that are of the completed flats has risen above 30 per cent.

Base effects are part of the story. The comparison is with August last year, when the lockdowns in the southern cities closed the shops, and the economists say the underlying pace is closer to three per cent. A figure lifted by a weak base is a figure that flatters the trend. The comparison will turn harder from October, when the lockdowns of last autumn become the base, and the economists expect the growth to slow again.

Provincial governments have been spending. The infrastructure investment rose by about six per cent as the local governments used the special bonds, and the central bank cut a policy rate in the summer to lower the cost of the debt. A stimulus that is fiscal is a stimulus that shows up in the cement. The central bank cut the rate on its one-year loans in August and again in September, and the borrowing by the local governments has reached the limit of the quota.

August shows a consumer coming back more strongly than the factory, and the consumer is the part that policy has been trying to reach. A consumer that is spending on the holidays and not on the houses is a consumer that the policy can reach, and the reach is the measure of the stimulus. A shop that sells a holiday is a shop that the stimulus reaches faster than a building site.
