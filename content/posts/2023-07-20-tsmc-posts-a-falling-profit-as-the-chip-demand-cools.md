description: "The quarter to June produced a profit about a quarter lower than a year ago, and the company cut the capital spending for the year as the customers worked down the stock."
---

TSMC reported a revenue of about 15.7 billion dollars for the quarter to June, down about 14 per cent, and a net profit of 181.8 billion Taiwan dollars, down about 23. The profit was above the estimate of about 172 billion, and the shares rose about 2 per cent. The company cut the capital spending for the year to about 32 billion dollars from 36. The revenue was about 500 million dollars above the estimate, and the gross margin was about 54 per cent. The company said the AI demand had been the strongest line, and the revenue from it had doubled in the first half.

Demand is the problem. The revenue from the smartphones fell by about 15 per cent, and the one from the personal computers by about 20 as the customers worked down the stock. The high-performance computing fell by about 10, and the one from the automotive rose by about 10. The revenue from the internet of things fell by about 5 per cent, and the one from the consumer electronics by about 15. The customers have been reducing the orders for the mature nodes since the autumn, and the utilization of the 28-nanometre line was about 70 per cent.

Its advanced nodes held. The revenue from the 5-nanometre process was about 30 per cent of the total, and the one from the 7-nanometre about 23. The 3-nanometre has entered the production, and the company said the yield was better than the plan. The 3-nanometre process is being used for the new phone and the data centre chips, and the ramp is expected to reach about 15 per cent of the revenue next year.

Gross margin fell to about 54 per cent from 59. The company said the lower utilization and the higher electricity costs had taken about 4 points, and the margin would fall by another 2 in the current quarter as the ramp of the new node continued. The company said the electricity costs had risen by about 20 per cent this year, and the effect on the margin was about 1.5 points.

Guidance was cautious. The company expects the revenue of about 16.7 billion to 17.5 billion dollars in the third quarter, which is about flat on the quarter, and the market had expected a little more. The customers have been cancelling the orders for the mature nodes. The capital spending is about half of the peak of 2022, and the company has slowed the construction of the new plants in Arizona and in Japan.

This quarter is the one in which the inventory cycle reached the foundry. A company that earns 181 billion Taiwan dollars while it cuts the capital spending is a company that is matching the capacity to the demand, and the matching is the part the customers pay for. The shares are up about 25 per cent this year, and the company is the largest foundry with about 55 per cent of the market.
