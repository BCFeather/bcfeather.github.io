description: "Employers advertised 8.06 million vacancies in April, down from more than twelve million at the peak, and the number of people quitting also fell."
---

American employers advertised 8.06 million job openings at the end of April, the fewest since February 2021 and down from a peak of more than twelve million two years earlier. The figure was published on 4 June. The figure was below every forecast in the survey, and the previous month was revised down, which made the report the weakest of the year.

Ratio of openings to unemployed workers is the number the central bank watches. It has fallen from two to about 1.2, which means the labour market has moved from a shortage of workers to something closer to balance without a large rise in unemployment. A ratio that falls through quits rather than through layoffs is a ratio that cools gently. The measure has fallen by a third from its peak without a corresponding rise in the number of people out of work, which is the outcome the central bank described as the soft landing it was aiming for.

Quits are the leading indicator. The number of people who left a job voluntarily fell again, and the surveys show that workers are less confident of finding another place at the same pay. A worker who stays put is a worker who is not bidding up wages. The industries that pay the highest wages have seen the largest fall in quits, and the economists read that as a sign that the workers who would have moved have already moved.

Hiring fell in the industries that expanded fastest, which are the professional services and the technology firms that borrowed cheaply during the pandemic. Hiring held in health care and in hospitality, where the demand is not sensitive to the interest rate. A labour market that cools in one sector and holds in another is a labour market that is rotating rather than contracting. The number of people hired fell in the same report, which means the demand is falling on both sides, and a market that hires less is a market that will raise pay more slowly.

Market read the report as a reason to expect a cut. Yields fell and the probability of a September reduction rose, because the vacancies number is one of the few measures that shows the demand for labour before it shows up in the unemployment rate. A measure that leads is a measure that the committee uses to justify waiting. The committee has said it wants the ratio to fall to its pre-pandemic level, and the report shows it has almost arrived rather than that it has overshot.

What the month shows is that a labour market can loosen through fewer advertisements rather than through more dismissals, and that path is the one the central bank has been trying to engineer. A labour market that cools by advertising fewer jobs is one that avoids the human cost of the alternative, and that is why the report was treated as good news by the people who own bonds.
