title: "Intel posts a profit as the PC market steadies"
date: "2023-07-27T09:20:00+08:00"

description: "The quarter to June returned to a profit after two quarters of losses, and the company said the personal computer demand had stopped falling."
---

Intel reported a revenue of 12.9 billion dollars for the quarter to June, down about 15 per cent, and a net profit of 1.48 billion. The earnings per share of 0.13 dollars beat the forecast of a small loss. The shares rose about 5 per cent after the report, and the company said the personal computer market had bottomed. The revenue was about 700 million above the estimate, and the gross margin was about 35.8 per cent. The company said the restructuring had cut about 1.5 billion dollars of the costs on the annual basis, and the headcount was down about 10 per cent.

Its client business held. The revenue from the personal computers was about 6.8 billion dollars, down about 12 per cent, which is the smallest decline in a year. The company said the inventory at the customers had returned to the normal, and the orders had improved in June. The average selling price of the personal computer chips fell by about 5 per cent, and the volume by about 8. The company said the PC market would be about 270 million units this year, down from about 300 in 2022.

Data centre was weak. The revenue from the servers was about 3.7 billion, down about 15 per cent, as the cloud customers bought the chips from the rivals. The company said the new Sapphire Rapids had shipped about 1 million units, and the ramp would continue. The revenue from the network and the edge was about 1.4 billion dollars, down about 30 per cent.

Its foundry lost more. The foundry business reported a revenue of about 0.2 billion and an operating loss of about 0.2, and the company said the business would not break even before 2026. The capital spending was about 26 billion dollars, down from the plan of 30. The company has about 15 billion dollars of the cash, and the debt is about 47 billion.

Guidance was better than feared. The company expects the revenue of about 12.9 to 13.9 billion dollars in the current quarter, and the gross margin of about 43 per cent. The market had expected about 13.2 billion. The company said the capital spending would be about 20 billion dollars next year, and the government grants would cover about 3. The company said the gross margin would reach about 50 per cent by the end of the next year, and the target of 60 is still three years away.

This quarter is the one in which the cost cuts reached the bottom line. A company that earns 1.5 billion dollars after two quarters of losses is a company that has matched the production to the demand, and the matching is the part the margins show. The shares are up about 30 per cent this year, and the company is the worst performer among the large chip makers over three years.
