Shareholders of Tesla voted on 13 June to restore a pay package for its chief executive that a court had struck down in January, and to change the company's state of incorporation from Delaware to Texas. The package is worth about forty-four billion dollars at the current share price. The proposal passed with about three quarters of the votes cast, and the shares rose slightly, which is a reaction to the removal of the uncertainty rather than to the award.

Procedure is the reason the award was struck down. The court found that the board had not disclosed how the targets had been set and that the process had been described inaccurately to the shareholders, and the vote was the company's answer to that finding. A package that is reapproved is a package that the board has to justify with a procedure rather than with a price. The package ties the award to market value and to operating milestones, and the milestones that the court examined included targets that the company had already met.

Vote also settles who controls the company. The chief executive owns a large stake and the retail shareholders who follow him voted in proportion, which means the outcome was decided by the same audience that bought the shares during the rally of 2020. A shareholder base that is loyal to a person is a base that a board can rely on. The institutions that advise shareholders on governance recommended against the award, and their clients include the funds that hold the largest blocks after the founder.

Reincorporation in Texas is the smaller change with the longer effect. The company had left Delaware once before, and the state's courts and its rules on shareholder suits are the reason that the founders of other companies have followed. A jurisdiction that is chosen by the board is a jurisdiction that the shareholders inherit. Texas has a business courts system that the companies prefer for disputes with shareholders, and the rules on derivative suits are narrower than in Delaware.

None of the compensation has been paid yet. The award is in options that vest over several years and that require the share price to rise further, so the headline value assumes a price that the market has not sustained. An option that is underwater is a payment that the accounts do not record. The company has said the award is intended to keep the chief executive focused on the business, and the board's argument is that the alternative is a departure that the shares would price.

What the vote shows is that the governance of a company can be decided by its shareholders against the advice of the institutions that own the largest blocks, and the outcome will be cited in every case about executive pay for a decade. A vote about pay is a vote about who controls a company, and the majority that approved it will be the reference for every board that faces the same question.
