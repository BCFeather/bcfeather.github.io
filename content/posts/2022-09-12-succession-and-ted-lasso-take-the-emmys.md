Succession won best drama for the second time, and Ted Lasso won best comedy for the second year. The 74th Emmy Awards were held on 12 September at the Microsoft Theater in Los Angeles. The ceremony had no host, and the broadcast ran to three hours. Succession took four awards, including writing and directing, and its ensemble was the largest of any nominated show. The series had finished its third season in December, and the fourth was in production. Its creator said the show would end after the fourth season, which was confirmed in 2023. The ceremony was the first to be held in September since 2018, and the academy had moved it forward by a week.

Ted Lasso won four awards as well, and its star won best actor in a comedy for the second time. The show had been criticized in its third season for losing its edge, and the voters did not agree. Apple TV+ had 51 nominations across its programmes, more than any other streaming service. Its third season had averaged 1.7 million viewers per episode in the United States, down from 2.5 in the second.

Zendaya won best actress in a drama for Euphoria at 26, and she had won the same award at 24. She is the youngest person to win two acting Emmys. The award for best actor in a drama went to Lee Jung-jae for Squid Game, the first non-English performance to win that category. The comedy had been renewed for a fourth season, and its cast had signed for two more years.

HBO led the night with 38 awards across its networks. The limited series category went to The White Lotus, which took five awards in total. The show had been submitted as a limited series after its first season, and its second season was moved to the drama categories. Ratings were 5.9 million viewers, the second-lowest for the ceremony and slightly below the previous year. The broadcast had been moved to a Monday to avoid a football game, and the academy said the date would not be repeated. The ceremony returned to Sunday the following year. Squid Game had 14 nominations, the most for a non-English series, and it won two awards.

Streaming services had been allowed to submit programmes without a cinema release under the old rules, and this was the last ceremony under them. The academy changed the rules for documentaries in 2023, and the debate about what counts as a film continued. The next ceremony was scheduled for January 2024 because of the strikes.

Two of the winners gave speeches about the state of the industry. One said the business had changed more in five years than in fifty, and the other said the writers in the room were the reason anything worked at all. Both remarks were quoted in the trade press the next morning, and neither was scripted. The red carpet was shorter than in previous years, and the arrivals were streamed rather than televised.
