Caterpillar reported a revenue of 17.3 billion dollars for the quarter to June, up about 12 per cent, and a profit of 2.9 billion. The earnings per share of 5.55 dollars beat the forecast of 4.58 by a wide margin. The shares fell about 3 per cent as the dealers added to the inventory, which the market read as a warning. The revenue was about 1 billion above the estimate, and the operating profit was about 3.7 billion. The company said the currency effects had added about 1 percentage point to the revenue.

Construction did the work. The revenue from the construction industries rose by about 19 per cent, and the one from the energy and the transport by about 14. The resource industries, which sell to the mines, rose by about 8 as the commodity prices held. The sales in North America rose by about 20 per cent, and the one in the Asia-Pacific by about 5 as China stayed weak. The company said the backlog was about 30 billion dollars, up about 5 per cent, and the lead times were still long for the large machines.

Prices helped. The company said the price rises had added about 9 percentage points to the revenue, and the volume about 3. The margins rose to about 21 per cent from 13, and the company said the pricing had held despite the weaker demand in China. The company said the steel and the freight costs had fallen by about 5 per cent, and the pricing had more than covered them. The company said the machines for the data centres had been a new line of the demand, and the one for the power generation had doubled.

Dealers added stock. The inventory at the dealers rose by about 1 billion dollars, and the company said the restocking would be smaller in the second half. The market has been wary of the dealer inventory since the downturn of 2019. The dealers had about 5 billion dollars of the inventory more than they want, and the company said the production would slow.

Its guidance was raised. The company expects the operating margin of about 20 per cent for the year, and the revenue a little above the previous guidance. The management said the infrastructure spending in the United States had been the strongest part. The company said the capital spending would be about 6 billion dollars this year, and the one for the year after would be similar.

This quarter is the one the pricing carried. A company that earns 2.9 billion dollars while the volume grows by 3 per cent is a company whose profit is the price, and the price is the part that the cycle will test. The shares are up about 20 per cent this year, and the company is the best performer in the large machinery group.
