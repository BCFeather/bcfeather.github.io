title: "Eurozone inflation falls to 5.5 per cent as the energy base drops"
date: "2023-06-30T09:20:00+08:00"
categories: [economy]
description: "The June flash estimate showed the headline falling from 6.1 per cent, while the core measure eased to 5.4 per cent on the services."
---

Eurozone consumer prices rose by 5.5 per cent over the year to June, according to the flash estimate, down from 6.1 per cent in May and below the forecast of 5.6. The core measure, which strips the energy, the food, the alcohol and the tobacco, eased to 5.4 from 5.3, which is a small rise on the measure the bank watches. The figure is the lowest since January 2022, and the headline has now halved from the peak of 10.6 per cent in October. The unemployment rate was about 6.5 per cent in May, and the wage growth has been the strongest in the countries with the tightest labour markets.

Energy did the work again. The energy prices fell by about 5.6 per cent over the year, the third month of the annual decline, as the base of the summer of 2022 dropped out. The gas in the wholesale market was about 30 euros per megawatt hour, down from about 90 at the start of the year. The electricity fell by about 8 per cent over the year, and the fuels by about 3 as the base of the spring of 2022 dropped out. The governments have spent about 700 billion euros on the energy support since 2021, and the schemes are being wound down this year.

Food eased a little. The prices of the food, the alcohol and the tobacco rose by about 11.7 per cent, down from 12.5, and the one of the fresh food by about 9. The easing is the part the households will notice last, because the grocery basket is the one they fill every week. The prices of the oils and the fats fell by about 10 per cent over the year, and the one of the vegetables by about 5.

Services stayed firm. The prices of the services rose by about 5.4 per cent, and the one of the industrial goods by about 4.2 as the supply chains normalized. The negotiated wages have been rising by about 4.5 per cent, and the bank expects the services to hold around 5 into the autumn. The air fares rose by about 10 per cent, and the one of the hotels by about 8 as the summer bookings held.

National figures were uneven. The inflation in Germany was about 6.8 per cent, and the one in France about 5.3, and the one in Spain about 1.6. The Baltic states were above 9, and the Netherlands about 5.8. The inflation in Italy was about 6.7 per cent, and the one in Portugal about 3.2, which shows the different weight of the energy.

This report is the one before the July meeting. A headline at 5.5 with the core at 5.4 is still far from the target, and the council has said the July increase is the base case. The council meets on 27 July, and the market expects another quarter-point increase to 3.75 per cent on the deposit rate.
