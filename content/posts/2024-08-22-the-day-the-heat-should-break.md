The twenty-second of August carried the solar term that marks the end of summer. Chu Shu means the heat withdraws, which is a description of the average year rather than of this one. The forecast for the week was thirty-four degrees in the river valley. A high of thirty-four in late August is not unusual. Overnight low is what makes it hard. A night that stays above twenty-eight keeps the buildings warm until the morning.

The harder target is the evening peak. The peak hour has moved later in the past decade, from two in the afternoon to eight in the evening, because the load is now air conditioning in flats rather than machinery in factories. The evening peak is the harder one to meet. A plant can be asked to run at night. A household cannot be asked to switch off at eight. Pumped storage and gas turbines that run for two hours make up the evening reserve.

Farmers read the term differently. A thunderstorm that drops twenty millimetres in an hour cools the street for a day and floods the underpass for two. Drainage in an old district was built for a storm that returns every three years. The farmers read the term differently. A field that is ripening needs dry weather. A week of rain in late August damages the grain before the harvest. Same term that ends the heat begins the month the harvest is counted in.

Schools open in the first week of September. A classroom that has been shut for two months is unbearable in the first week. Schedule puts the physical education class in the morning for that reason. The autumn is shorter than it used to be. A city that had six weeks between the heat and the cold now has four. Season the poets wrote about has changed most.

A date rather than a temperature is what the term is. Calendar divides the year into twenty-four parts of fifteen days. Parts were fixed for a climate that moved more slowly than this one. City is hotter than the field beside it. A district of concrete and asphalt holds the heat of the day and releases it after sunset, and the difference between the centre and the edge of the built-up area is five degrees on a still night.

The busiest trade of the month is the installer. An air conditioning unit that is bought in July is fitted in August, and the technician who fits it works from seven to seven with a list of forty addresses. Stepped tariff puts the weight on the third step. A household that uses more than four hundred units pays a rate that doubles, and the bill that arrives in September is the one that the family argues about. The heat broke in the first week of September. A front came down from the north and the high fell by ten degrees in two days, which is the way the season ends in this part of the country.
