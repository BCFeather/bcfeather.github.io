President Joe Biden announced on 21 July that he would not seek re-election, ending a campaign that had been in trouble since a televised debate in June, and endorsed Vice President Kamala Harris in the same letter. The decision came four months before the vote and a few weeks before the party's convention. A president who withdraws is a president who has calculated that his presence costs more votes than his departure loses.

Debate was the turning point. A performance that confirmed the questions about the president's age produced a wave of defections among donors and elected officials, and the polling that followed showed losses in states the party had expected to hold. A single appearance is rarely decisive, and this one was. A debate in June is a debate that set the terms for four months of coverage and for one decision in July. The debate had been moved forward at the candidate's request, and the early date gave the party time to react.

Timing was the operative detail. A withdrawal after the primaries is a withdrawal that leaves the delegates to the winner of the primaries free to choose, and the party had to transfer its organization, its money and its message within weeks. A campaign that is rebuilt in a month is a campaign that runs on the candidate rather than on the plan. A party that rebuilds in a month is a party that has to rely on the volunteers who were already knocking on doors. The delegates were bound by the primary results, and the release of those delegates was the procedural step that made the transfer possible.

Endorsement was immediate and not unanimous. Some senior figures called for an open contest, and the argument was about legitimacy rather than about policy. A nominee who is chosen by acclamation is a nominee whom the other side will describe as anointed. A contested convention is a convention that would have split the party in public, which is the outcome the leadership avoided.

Money was the practical question. A campaign account that was raised for one candidate cannot simply be spent by another, and the rules on transferring funds were read differently by different lawyers. A campaign that has to solve a legal question in its first week is a campaign that starts behind. A legal question about a campaign account is a question that the lawyers answer in weeks and the press reports in days. The campaign had spent most of its cash in the spring, and the new candidate had to raise in weeks what had taken a year.

What the withdrawal shows is that a party can replace a candidate and cannot replace a year. A campaign that began with a debate about the economy ended with a debate about whether the candidate could finish a sentence. A year cannot be replaced, and the calendar became the argument that the campaign could not win.
