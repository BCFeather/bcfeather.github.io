---

The placenta is the interface between two genetically different individuals, and the interface is where tolerance is negotiated. Its anatomy is unusual: fetal cells invade maternal tissue, and maternal immune cells reside among them.

The map in *Nature*, 13 July 2023 resolves the arrangement. The authors use MIBI, an imaging method that labels many proteins at once, to identify cell types and their positions in the decidua and in the placental villi.

The finding is an architecture. Trophoblast subsets occupy distinct zones, and the maternal immune cells are distributed in a pattern that follows the trophoblast's invasion front.

The tolerance is the implication. A interface in which the mother's immune cells are positioned rather than uniformly mixed is one in which proximity is regulated, and the regulation is a candidate mechanism for tolerance.

The vascular component is part of the map. Spiral arteries are remodelled by trophoblast, and the map shows where the remodelling happens relative to the immune population.

The pathology is the motivation. Pre-eclampsia and fetal growth restriction involve a failure of that remodelling, and a map is the reference against which a pathological specimen can be compared.

The method's strength is the panel. A dozen or more proteins labelled in one section distinguishes cell states that a hematoxylin and eosin stain cannot.

The limitation is the sample. A placenta delivered at term is not a placenta at twelve weeks, and the map's window is the deliveries that the authors could obtain.

What the paper does not show is the dynamics. An interface is a moving front, and a fixed section shows a moment.

For reproductive biology, the value is an anatomy that was previously described in words. The interface's zones have been known qualitatively, and a map with coordinates and markers is a quantitative reference.

The trophoblast's subtypes are the map's central distinction. A villous trophoblast exchanges nutrients and an extravillous one invades, and the two have different markers and different positions.

The immune cells' composition is the second result. Decidual natural killer cells and macrophages dominate, and their ratio changes with gestation, which a single time point cannot show.

The imaging depth is the constraint. A section is thin, and a structure that extends through the tissue is reconstructed from serial sections.

The comparison with mouse models is the caution. A mouse placenta's architecture differs from a human one, and a mechanism validated in a mouse needs a human check.

The clinical use is a reference for pathology. A placenta from a complicated pregnancy can be scored against the map, and the score is a way to make a description comparable between centres.
The antibody panel's selection is the design. A marker for trophoblast, a marker for each immune subset and a marker for the vasculature are the minimum, and the authors' panel adds states within each class.
The tissue's fragility is a practical constraint. A decidua is soft and a section is hard to cut, and the authors' preparation is what preserves the arrangement that the map is meant to show.
