Barbie passed one billion dollars in the global box office on 6 August, 17 days after the release. The film is the fastest for Warner Bros to reach the mark, and the director, Greta Gerwig, is the first woman working alone to have a film at that level. The studio said the ticket sales had held through the third weekend. The domestic box office was about 460 million dollars, and the international about 540. The film had the best opening of the year in the United States, at about 162 million dollars.

Its audience was the surprise. The film drew about 60 per cent of the women, and the ones over 25 were the largest group, which is unusual for a film based on a toy. The repeat viewing was about 20 per cent, and the social media was the engine of the first week. The share of the men in the audience rose to about 40 per cent, which is higher than the studio had expected. The tickets sold were about 120 million, and the average price was about 11 dollars.

Marketing was the plan. The studio spent about 150 million dollars on the promotion, and the pink branding was in about 100 partnerships from the clothes to the ice cream. The chief executive said the campaign had been the largest in the history of the studio. The studio said the partnerships had added about 100 million dollars of the revenue, and the one of the soundtrack was about 30. The studio said the social media had produced about 4 billion views of the trailer and the clips, which is the largest for any film this year. The studio said the soundtrack had sold about 500,000 copies, and the one of the streaming was the largest of the year.

Competition helped. The film opened the same weekend as Oppenheimer, and the pairing became the internet event of the summer. The two films together have sold about 1.8 billion dollars of the tickets, and the studios have said the rivalry was the reason the audiences returned. The Oppenheimer has sold about 700 million dollars, and the two films were the largest of the summer.

China was weaker. The film has sold about 240 million dollars there, which is below the level of the other markets, and the local films have taken the share. The studio said the release had been the widest for a Hollywood film this year. The film was released in about 70 markets, and the one of the Japan is scheduled for the autumn.

This result is the one the industry needed after the pandemic. A film about a doll that earns a billion dollars in 17 days is a film that has found the audience outside the usual, and the usual is the part the studios have been worried about. The shares of the parent company are up about 10 per cent since the release, and the studio is the best performer in the media group.
