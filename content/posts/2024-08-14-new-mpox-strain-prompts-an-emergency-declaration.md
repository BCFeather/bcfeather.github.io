Officials at the World Health Organization declared mpox a public health emergency of international concern on 14 August, for the second time in two years, after a new variant spread beyond the country where it was first detected. The declaration is a coordination instrument rather than a funding decision. The declaration obliges member states to report cases and allows the organization to coordinate procurement, and it does not release money by itself.

Variant is the difference. The strain that circulated in 2022 spread mainly through sexual contact among adults, and the new one, known as clade Ib, spreads through households and affects children. A virus that spreads in a household is a virus that a clinic cannot contain by tracing contacts of adults only. Children in crowded households are the group that the new strain has hit hardest, and schools and clinics are the places where it is being passed on.

Geography has widened. Cases were confirmed in countries neighbouring the one where the outbreak began, and travel brought single cases to other continents, which is the pattern that turns a regional outbreak into an emergency. A case that arrives by air is a case that a health system has to recognize. Surveillance in the affected region is thin, which means that the reported count is a fraction of the real one and the models have to assume it.

Vaccines exist and the supply does not. A vaccine that was used in 2022 is effective against the new strain, and the doses that are available are a fraction of what the affected countries would need. A vaccine that exists and is not in the right place is a vaccine that has not been deployed. The manufacturer has said that it can expand production only if someone places an order, and the orders that arrived were small. A dose that costs a hundred dollars is a dose that a health ministry cannot afford for a population of millions without a donor.

Response is being run by underfunded institutions. The African centre for disease control declared its own emergency a day earlier, and its budget is a rounding error against the scale of the continent. An institution that depends on donors for surveillance is an institution that learns about an outbreak late. Border screening is the tool that rich countries reach for, and it catches the travellers who are symptomatic and misses the ones who are not.

What the declaration shows is that a virus that is controlled in one region can re-emerge in another with different properties. The lesson of 2022 was that a pox virus can travel, and the lesson of this year is that it can also change how it travels. A virus that changes its route of transmission is a virus that the vaccines and the contact tracing have to be redesigned around, and the redesign has begun.
