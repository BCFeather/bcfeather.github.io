description: "The quarter to May beat the estimates on the profit, with the stock down and the Chinese sales recovering, while the margin stayed under pressure."
---

Nike reported a revenue of 12.8 billion dollars for the quarter to May, up 5 per cent, and a profit of 1.03 billion. The earnings per share of 0.66 dollars beat the forecast of 0.67 by a wider margin in the constant currency. The shares fell about 3 per cent as the guidance for the margin disappointed. The revenue was about 200 million above the estimate, and the gross margin was about a point below the forecast. The company said the currency effects had subtracted about 2 percentage points from the revenue.

Inventories fell in the quarter. The stock was about 8.5 billion dollars, down 1 per cent on the year and down about 5 on the previous quarter, as the company cleared the seasonal goods. The North America stock was still above the level it wants, and the discounts continued through the quarter. The apparel stock was down by about 15 per cent, and the footwear by about 4 as the classic models were cleared. The company said the markdowns would be smaller in the autumn, and the promotions of the summer were planned for the older lines.

China recovered. The revenue in the region rose by about 25 per cent on the constant currency, and the company said the sell-through had been positive for three quarters. The stores reopened in the spring, and the running and the basketball were the categories that led. The revenue in the Europe, the Middle East and the Africa was about 2.9 billion dollars, up about 3 per cent on the constant currency. The footwear in China rose by about 20 per cent, and the apparel by about 30 as the demand for the running shoes returned.

Margins were the problem. The gross margin fell to about 43.6 per cent from 45, as the markdowns and the higher freight costs took about 1.5 points. The company said the margin would stay under pressure in the first half of the next year and recover in the second. The freight costs have fallen from the peak but are still above the level of 2019, and the company says the gap will close in the next year.

Guidance was cautious. The company expects the revenue to grow by the mid single digits in the year to May 2024, and the market had expected a little more. The wholesale orders have been falling as the retailers cut the shelf space, and the direct sales have been carrying the growth. The company said the selling and the administrative costs would grow more slowly than the revenue, and the headcount was flat.

This quarter is the one in which the stock finally came down. A company that earns 1 billion dollars while its stock is still above the plan is a company in the middle of the cleanup, and the cleanup is the part the margin shows. The shares are down about 3 per cent this year, which is the worst performance in the large sportswear group.
