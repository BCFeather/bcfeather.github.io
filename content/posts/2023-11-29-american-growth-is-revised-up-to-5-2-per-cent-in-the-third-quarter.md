American output grew at an annual rate of 5.2 per cent in the third quarter, the second estimate from the Bureau of Economic Analysis said on 29 November, up from the 4.9 in the first. The revision added about 0.3 points, and the quarter is now the fastest since the end of 2021. The revision was larger than the forecast of 5 per cent, and the quarter is now the strongest since the end of 2021.

Business investment was revised up. The spending on the structures rose by about 7 per cent as the factories for the chips were built, and the equipment rose by about 1 after the first estimate showed a fall. A factory that is built is a demand that the private money pays for. The spending on the computer and the chip factories rose by about 10 per cent, and the one on the offices fell for a ninth quarter. The spending on the software and the research rose by about 4 per cent, and the one on the transport equipment fell.

Inventories added slightly more, and the government spending was revised down a little as the state and the local outlays slowed. A stock that is built is a growth that is borrowed from the future. The final sales to the domestic buyers was revised down to about 3.2 per cent, which is the measure that strips the inventories.

Profits were the other number. The corporate profits rose by about 3 per cent in the quarter, and the financial sector did the most as the interest income grew. A profit that rises with the rates is a bank that benefits from the delay. The profit share of the output rose to about 11 per cent, and the wages share fell as the energy and the retail margins widened. The profit of the non-financial firms rose by about 1 per cent, and the one of the banks by about 9 as the interest margin widened. The share of the profit in the output is the highest since the 1960s outside the pandemic, and the wage share is the lowest.

Economists expect the fourth quarter to be about half the pace, and the Atlanta Fed's tracker has been running at about 2 per cent. A slowdown that is expected is a slowdown that the market has priced. The forecasters expect the growth to slow to about 1 per cent next year, and the unemployment to rise to about 4.5. The forecasters said the strikes in the car industry would cut about 0.3 points from the fourth quarter.

What the revision shows is that the quarter was stronger than the headline and the strength came from the private investment, and the private investment is the part that the rates will test. A quarter that is revised up is a quarter that the Fed can cite as the reason to wait, and the citation is the policy.
