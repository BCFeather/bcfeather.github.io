Meta reported a profit of 11.6 billion dollars for the third quarter on 25 October, up from 4.4 billion a year earlier, on the revenue of 34.1 billion, which rose 23 per cent. The shares fell about 3 per cent as the guidance for the costs disappointed. The revenue was above the forecast of 33.5 billion, and the operating margin was 40 per cent, the highest since 2021.

Advertising revenue recovered. The revenue rose by about 24 per cent in constant currency, and the number of the daily users of the family of the apps reached 3.1 billion. An advertiser that returns after a year of the cuts is a quarter that surprises. The advertising in the Reels format rose, and the company said the average price per advertisement fell while the number sold rose by about 30 per cent.

Efficiency was the year's work. The company has cut about 21,000 jobs since the autumn of last year, and the head count is about 8 per cent lower than a year earlier, which is the reason the margin doubled. A payroll that is smaller is a profit that is larger. The restructuring charges were about 1 billion dollars in the quarter, and the company said the work would be finished by the end of the year. The company said the head count will be about the same next year, and the hiring will be focused on the technical roles.

AI is the spending. The company said the capital spending would be 28 to 30 billion dollars this year and more next year as the data centers are built for the ranking and the models. A data center that is built is a cost that arrives before the revenue. The company said the AI is used to rank the content and to target the advertisements, and it has begun to test the tools for the advertisers. The spending has risen by about 30 per cent this year, and the guidance implies another increase of about 20 per cent next year.

Reality Labs lost about 3.7 billion dollars in the quarter, and the company said the losses would increase next year as the new headset ships. A unit that loses money is a bet that the shareholders have accepted for a decade. The headset unit is the legacy of the founder's bet on the virtual reality, and the company has said it will take a decade to pay.

What the quarter shows is that the advertising business has recovered faster than the market expected and the company is spending the gain on the machines, and the spending is what the guidance is about. A company that doubles the profit in a year and raises the spending is a company that the market will reward and punish in the same quarter. The guidance is the part that the market will hold the company to, and the holding will be judged against the spending on the machines.
