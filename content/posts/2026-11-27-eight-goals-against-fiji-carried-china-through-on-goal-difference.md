On the last night of Group H, eight goals against Fiji on 26 November won a match and repaired a goal difference. China's under-17 side closed the group with four points from three games, ten goals scored and seven conceded, and that record placed it second among the third-placed teams that fill the last eight places in the 32-team knockout round. The goal difference, more than the win itself, is what the table records. The three results read 0-5, 2-2 and 8-0, and the last of them turned a negative difference positive inside ninety minutes.

Because forty-eight teams play in twelve groups of four, the last night was decided by arithmetic as much as by football: the top two of each advance, and the eight best third-placed sides complete the round of 32, ordered by points, then goal difference, then goals scored. A 0-5 defeat by Spain in the opener therefore left a route open, provided the two remaining matches produced points and goals in sufficient quantity to survive a comparison across all twelve groups. Four of the twelve third-placed sides go out, so the threshold is a comparison rather than a fixed mark.

Drawing 2-2 with Morocco on 23 November, the side carried a point from two matches into the final fixture at the Aspire complex in Doha, which kicked off at 23:00 Beijing time on 26 November. Zhao Songyuan scored twice in that match, and the draw kept the calculation alive. A defeat there would have ended the tournament; a draw would have left the side dependent on results in other groups, where the third-place table was already crowded. China had not appeared at this tournament for twenty-one years, which is why the group stage carried weight of its own.

The margin did the work. Ten goals scored and seven conceded leave a difference of three, and each additional goal moved that figure further clear of the sides chasing the same place. The eight goals came against the weakest opponent in the group, which is the part a scoreline records accurately and a scouting report would treat with caution, since a defence that concedes eight has usually stopped defending before the last of them. Goal difference counts only the three group matches, so a heavy defeat and a heavy win cancel each other with unusual directness.

What the table cannot show is the distance to the next opponent. Fiji sat well below Spain and Morocco in the same group, so an eight-goal margin measures a mismatch as much as a performance, and the earlier matches produced two points. The association noted that no Chinese men's team at any level had won a match and advanced at a world finals since 2005, which sets this side's run against two decades of group-stage exits. Spain, the group winner, supplied the measure of a top seed, and the 0-5 is the figure that set the arithmetic in motion.

Serbia waits on 1 December, with the tie scheduled for 21:30 Beijing time at the same complex, and the winner goes into the round of 16. Knockout football removes the arithmetic that carried China this far, because no goal difference and no third-place ranking survive the group stage. A single match now decides the tournament's next round, and a side that has scored ten goals in three games meets an opponent whose group it did not share.
