date: "2023-11-02T09:20:00+08:00"

description: "The quarter to September beat the forecasts on the phones and the services, and the revenue fell for a fourth quarter as the sales in China slipped."
---

Apple reported a profit of 22.96 billion dollars for the quarter to September on 2 November, up from 20.7 billion a year earlier, on the revenue of 89.5 billion, which fell 1 per cent. The shares fell about 1 per cent in the after-hours trade on the guidance. The revenue was above the forecast of 89.3 billion, and the gross margin rose to 45.2 per cent from 42.3 a year earlier as the mix shifted to the services.

Services did the work. The revenue rose by about 16 per cent to 22.3 billion, a record for the quarter, and the margin on the segment is about 70 per cent, which is why the profit grew while the sales fell. A subscription that renews is a revenue that the hardware cannot match. The services include the App Store, the iCloud and the advertising, and the company has raised the prices of several of the subscriptions this year.

Phone business was steady. The iPhone revenue rose by about 3 per cent to 43.8 billion, and the company said the Pro models did the work as the buyers traded up. A phone that is more expensive is a revenue that offsets the units. The number of the installed devices reached 2.2 billion, and the company said about half of the buyers of the new phones were switching from the Android. The company said the buyers were choosing the models with more storage, and the average price of the phones rose by about 2 per cent.

China was the weak spot. The revenue in the greater China region fell by about 2.5 per cent to 15.1 billion, and the analysts said the local brands had taken share in the mid-range. A market that is contested is a price that is cut. The sales in the region have fallen for three quarters, and the company has been offering the discounts through the retailers during the holiday season. The wearables fell by about 3 per cent, and the company said the new watches had sold better than the older models in the same quarter last year.

Guidance was cautious. The company said the holiday quarter would be about the same as last year, and the finance chief said the Mac and the iPad would fall as the year compares with the reopening. A flat guide is a company that has no new category. The Mac revenue fell by about 34 per cent in the quarter as the year compares with the launch of the new chips, and the iPad fell by about 10. The services are expected to grow by about 10 per cent in the quarter, which is slower than the 16 per cent of the last three months.

What the quarter shows is that the services have become the engine of the profit and the hardware the engine of the revenue, and the two engines now grow at different speeds. A company whose profit grows while the revenue falls is a company that has become a subscription business with a hardware front, and the front is what the market still prices.
