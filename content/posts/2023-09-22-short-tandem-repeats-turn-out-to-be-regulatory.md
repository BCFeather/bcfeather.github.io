A short tandem repeat is a stretch of DNA in which a motif is copied several times, and the copy number varies between people. The variation has been treated as a marker rather than as a mechanism.

The study in *Science*, September 2023 treats it as a mechanism. The authors survey repeats in promoters across the genome and show that their length changes the expression of the gene they control.

The effect is the finding. A promoter with more copies drives a stronger expression, and the relationship is graded rather than switch-like, which makes the repeat a dial.

The mechanism is the binding. A repeat that matches a transcription factor's motif provides more binding sites, and the number of sites is the variable that sets the expression.

The variation's consequence is the population genetics. A repeat's length differs between people, and a length that changes expression is a variant with a measurable effect.

The disease link is the application. A repeat in a promoter that controls a growth gene is a candidate for a risk variant, and the authors' association analysis is the evidence.

The evolution is the frame. A repeat expands and contracts through replication slippage, and a regulatory element that is unstable is one that evolves quickly.

The limitation is the measurement. A repeat's length is hard to genotype with short reads, and the authors' long-read approach is what makes the survey possible.

What the paper does not show is the effect's size in a person. A promoter's contribution to a trait is one of many, and the paper's effect sizes are modest.

For genetics, the value is a class of variant that has been neglected. The genome's repeats have been filtered out of most analyses, and a functional role for them is a reason to keep them.

The slippage is the mutation's mechanism. A polymerase that stutters on a repeat changes the copy number, and the rate is higher for a longer repeat.

The motif's identity is the specificity. A repeat that matches one factor's site is a dial for that factor, and a repeat that matches several is a dial for a combination.

The survey's scale is the discovery. A genome-wide scan of promoters is what turns an anecdote into a class, and the authors' catalogue is the resource.

The comparison with enhancers is the useful frame. An enhancer is a regulatory element at a distance, and a repeat in a promoter is a regulatory element in place.

The genotyping's difficulty is the technical barrier. A repeat of a few hundred base pairs defeats a short read, and the long-read platform is the tool.

The clinical use is a risk score. A repeat's length is a genotype, and a genotype that shifts expression is a term in a polygenic score.
The chromatin is the context. A repeat in a promoter sits near a nucleosome, and the repeat's length can change the nucleosome's positioning, which adds a second mechanism.
The population data are the check. A repeat that is common and that shifts expression is one whose effect a genome-wide association study can detect, and the overlap is the paper's validation.
