Planners met on 11 and 12 December at the conference that sets the coming year's priorities and described monetary policy as moderately loose, a phrase that had not been used for more than a decade. It also promised a higher deficit ratio, more support for consumption and measures to stabilize the property and stock markets. A meeting that is closed to the public produces a communiqué, and the communiqué is the policy. A meeting held behind closed doors produces a communiqué, and the communiqué is the policy until the next one.

Language matters because the system runs on signals. A phrase that is changed is a phrase that tells every bank, ministry and state-owned enterprise what is permitted next year, and the shift from prudent to moderately loose is a permission to lend rather than an instruction. A bank that reads the phrase as permission is a bank that lends before the instruction arrives. A bank that reads the phrase as permission is a bank that lends before the instruction reaches its branch.

Consumption promise is the new element. Previous packages supported investment because investment is easier to direct, and the result was capacity that produced goods for a market that did not buy them. A policy that supports household demand is a policy that has to work through wages, transfers and prices rather than through a project list. A transfer to a household is a transfer that is spent or saved, and the saving is the part that disappoints. A transfer that a household saves is a transfer that does not reach a shop.

Property measures are the part that has not worked. A market that is clearing a decade of overbuilding needs buyers, and the buyers are waiting for prices to stop falling. A policy that supports the market without changing the expectation of falling prices is a policy that buys time rather than a recovery. A developer that is waiting for a buyer is a developer that cannot repay its lender.

External setting is the constraint. A new American administration was promising tariffs, and a country that exports a large share of its output cannot assume the demand will be there. A stimulus that is aimed at domestic consumption is also a hedge against a trade shock. A tariff that is announced is a tariff that an exporter can plan around, and a tariff that is threatened is one it cannot. A tariff that is threatened is a tariff that an exporter cannot plan around.

What the conference establishes is that the policy stance has moved and that the instruments have not been tested. A looser stance announced in December is a stance that the first quarter will reveal, and the market's reaction to the phrasing is a bet on the implementation. A stance that is announced is a stance that the data will test within a quarter.
