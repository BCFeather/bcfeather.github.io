Noah Lyles won the 200 metres at the world championships in Budapest on 25 August in 19.52 seconds, five days after taking the 100 in 9.83. He is the first man to complete the double at the championships since Usain Bolt in 2015. The American also ran the anchor leg of the relay that won the gold. The 200 was the fourth fastest of his career, and the one of 19.31 in 2022 is the American record. He became the first American to win the 100 and the 200 at the same championships since Carl Lewis in 1984.

His race was clear. Lyles had the lead by the turn and won by about three metres from the American teenager Erriyon Knighton, who took the silver in 19.75. The Liberian, Dubem Nwanyanwu, was not in the final, and the field was the youngest in the history of the event. The Knighton is 19 years old, and the silver was his second at the championships. The Canadian, Andre De Grasse, was fifth in 20.14, and the one of the Botswana, Letsile Tebogo, took the bronze.

Training was the plan. Lyles had moved to a new coach in 2022 and had cut the weight work for the speed endurance, and the 200 had been the priority. He said the 100 had been the bonus, and the two together had been the goal since the winter. He had run about 40 races this year, and the one of the 200 in the spring was 19.67. He said the second half of the race had been the part he had worked on, and the closing 100 metres was the fastest of the field.

His times were fast. The 100 of 9.83 was the fastest of the year at the time, and the 200 of 19.52 is the fastest in the world since 2019. The stadium in Budapest was about 1,000 feet above the sea, and the fast track had produced about 10 national records. The 4x100 relay was won in 37.38 seconds, which is the fastest of the year. The women's 200 was won by the Jamaican, Shericka Jackson, in 21.41 seconds, which is the second fastest in history.

Its sport needed it. The championships had lost about 30 of the big names to the injuries and the schedule, and the attendance was the lowest in 20 years. The Lyles double gave the event the story it had been missing, and the American said he wanted to be the face of the sport. The prize money was about 70,000 dollars for the gold, and the one of the double is the same as two medals.

This result sets up the season. A sprinter who wins both at the worlds is the favourite for the Olympics in Paris, and the favourite is the part he has said he wants. The Olympics are in about 11 months, and the one of the trials is in June.
