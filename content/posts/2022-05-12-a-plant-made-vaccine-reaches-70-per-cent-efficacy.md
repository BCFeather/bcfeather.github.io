A vaccine that can be grown rather than fermented is a vaccine that a country can make without a bioreactor, and the idea has been pursued since the 1990s without a licensed product. The pandemic supplied the trial that the platform needed. A facility that grows plants is a facility that a middle-income country can build without a stainless-steel plant, and that is the argument that has kept the platform alive through two decades of setbacks.

CoVLP is a virus-like particle that displays the spike protein on a lipid envelope, produced in leaves of *Nicotiana benthamiana* that have been infiltrated with a bacterial vector carrying the gene. It is given with the AS03 adjuvant. The phase 3 trial ran at 85 centres. The paper appeared in *The New England Journal of Medicine*, 4 May 2022. The plant is a production chassis rather than a food crop, and the leaves are harvested a few days after infiltration, which is a cycle measured in weeks and not in months.

The trial randomized 24,141 adults in a 1:1 ratio to two intramuscular injections 21 days apart or placebo, and the median age was 29 years. Symptomatic COVID from seven days after the second dose occurred in 165 participants in the intention-to-treat population, and every sample that could be sequenced carried a variant of the original strain. A trial that runs across four continents has to accept a heterogeneity in the circulating variants, and the sequencing of every sample is what allows the efficacy to be attributed to a defined set.

Efficacy against symptomatic disease caused by the five variants identified was 69.5 per cent, with an interval from 56.7 to 78.8. In a post hoc analysis efficacy against moderate to severe disease was 78.8 per cent, and among participants who were seronegative at baseline it was 74.0 per cent. A post hoc analysis carries less weight than a prespecified one, and the number is reported as such, with the prespecified endpoint as the figure that a regulator would use.

No severe cases occurred in the vaccine group, and the median viral load in breakthrough cases was more than a hundred times lower than in the placebo group. A lower viral load is a proxy for a shorter infectious period, and it is the kind of secondary observation that supports a claim about transmission.

Reactogenicity was higher than with the mRNA products: local adverse events occurred in 92.3 per cent of vaccine recipients and 45.5 per cent of placebo recipients, and systemic events in 87.3 and 65.0 per cent. The events were mostly mild and transient, and the adjuvant is the reason for the frequency. An adjuvant that produces a strong local reaction is an adjuvant that is doing its job, and AS03 is the same one that was used in the pandemic influenza vaccines that were associated with narcolepsy in some countries.

The plant platform is the durable contribution. A facility that grows tobacco plants can switch the inserted gene in weeks, which is the property that matters for a pandemic in which the variant changes faster than a production line can be rebuilt. A platform that can be redirected in weeks is a platform that can respond to a variant before it has finished spreading, and the regulatory pathway for such a change is the part that has not been tested.
