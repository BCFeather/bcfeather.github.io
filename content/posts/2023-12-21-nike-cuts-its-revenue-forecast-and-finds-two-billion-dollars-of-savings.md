Nike reported a revenue of 13.4 billion dollars for the quarter to November on 21 December, up 1 per cent, and a profit of 1.58 billion, which was above the forecast. The shares fell about 12 per cent on the guidance. The revenue was above the forecast of 13.4 billion, and the gross margin rose to 44.6 per cent as the discounts on the older stock were smaller. The shares had their worst day since 2001, and the analysts said the cut was the third in a year.

Guidance is the problem. The company said the revenue would fall in the low single digits in the next quarter and grow only slightly in the full year, which is below the estimate. A guide that is cut is a share that is sold. The company said the wholesale orders had been cut as the retailers reduced the shelf space, and the direct sales would carry the quarter. The company said the retailers had been ordering later and in the smaller quantities, and the lead times had been cut by about a third.

Inventory is the reason. The company has been cutting the inventories, and the footwear is still above the level it wants, which means the discounts will continue. A stock that is high is a price that is lower. The inventories fell by about 14 per cent over the year, and the footwear is still about 3 per cent above the level of last year. The company said the discounts on the apparel had been deeper than the plan, and the gross margin of the next quarter would be about a point lower.

Cost savings are the plan. The company said it would remove about 2 billion dollars of the costs over three years through the simpler products and the automation, and about 1 billion of it will come from the gross margin. A cost that is removed is a profit that appears. The company has cut about 700 jobs this year, and the savings will include the simpler product range and the fewer styles. The savings will also come from the fewer styles in the range, and the company has said the number would be cut by about a quarter.

Sales in China grew by 8 per cent. The sales in the region rose by about 8 per cent, and the company said the demand in the cities had held while the smaller ones were weaker. A market that grows is a market that the brand has to defend. The sales in the region rose as the run in the cities recovered, and the company has been opening the stores with the local partners.

What the quarter shows is that the brand is still selling but the market has moved to the value and the comfort, and the move is what the guidance admits. A company that cuts the guidance while the profit beats is a company that is paying for the past, and the payment is the discount. The investors have been asking for the detail of the savings, and the company said it would give the breakdown in the spring.
