Meta reported a revenue of 28.6 billion dollars for the quarter to March, up 3 per cent, and a profit of 5.7 billion. The shares rose about 12 per cent in the after-hours trading. The daily users across the family of the apps reached 2.04 billion. The earnings per share of 2.20 dollars beat the forecast of 2.02, and the operating margin rose to about 27 per cent from 25. The revenue was about 2 per cent above the estimate, and the company said the second quarter would be similar.

Advertising recovered. The impressions rose by about 26 per cent and the average price per ad fell by about 17 per cent, which means the company sold more of the cheaper inventory. The measure of the ad performance has been the one the buyers watch since the privacy changes of 2021. The advertising in the Asia-Pacific grew by about 7 per cent, and the one in Europe fell by about 1 as the brands held back. The count of the advertisers reached about 10 million, and the company said the smaller businesses were the fastest-growing part.

Reels is still a drag on the revenue. The format grows faster than the feed and the stories, and it earns less per minute. The company said the gap would close through the year as the advertisers learned the placement, and the monetization rate has been rising. The Reels revenue is now about 30 per cent of the total ad revenue, and the company said the format would be a drag through the year. The engagement on the app rose by about 5 per cent, and the time spent on the video formats grew by about 24 per cent.

Costs were cut. The headcount fell by about 1 per cent after the cuts of the winter, and the company lowered the forecast for the total expenses to about 86 billion dollars from 89. The operating margin rose to about 27 per cent. The company said the headcount would fall by another 1 per cent in the next quarter, and the office space was being consolidated. The capital spending was about 7 billion dollars, and the forecast for the year was cut to about 30 billion from 34.

Reality Labs lost about 4 billion dollars in the quarter. The company said the losses would grow this year and then peak, and the investors have accepted the plan while the core business recovers. A bet that is expensive is a bet that the balance sheet can carry. The unit sold about 6 million of the headsets, and the company said the next version would come in the autumn at a higher price. The losses in the unit have reached about 40 billion dollars since 2019, and the company has said the investment would peak in 2024.

This quarter is the first since 2021 in which the revenue grew. A company that sells more impressions at a lower price is a company that is trading the price for the volume, and the trade is working. The shares are up about 100 per cent this year, which is the best performance in the large technology group. A company that raises the price of the ad while the impressions grow is a company that has the demand to do it.
