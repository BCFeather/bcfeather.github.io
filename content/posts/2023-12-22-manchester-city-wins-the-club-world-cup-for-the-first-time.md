Manchester City beat Fluminense 4-0 in the final of the Club World Cup in Jeddah on 22 December, and the club has now won the trophy for the first time. The match was played in front of about 45,000 people, and the stadium in Jeddah was not full for the final. The match was the last one of the tournament, and the English side had reached the final by beating the Japanese and the Mexican champions. The English side had won the European title in June, which is the qualification for the tournament, and the club has been the favourite since.

Goals came early. The first was after about 40 seconds when the ball was played through and the striker finished, and the second came before the half hour. A goal that early is a plan that is broken. The striker scored twice in the first half, and the midfielder added the third after the break. The fourth came in the closing minutes when the substitute finished a counter, and the stadium emptied before the trophy was handed over.

Brazilian ball possession did not hold. Fluminense had won the Copa Libertadores with the older players and the possession, and the English side pressed them into the errors. A press that is high is a pass that is hurried. The Brazilian side had about 55 per cent of the ball, and it made about 10 shots against the 18 of the English side. The second goal came from a mistake at the back, and the third was a header from a corner early in the second half. The Brazilian side lost its captain to injury in the first half, and the changes did not help the shape.

A full set of titles is now complete. The club has won the Premier League, the FA Cup, the Champions League and the Super Cup, and the world title is the fifth. A set that is complete is a season that is finished. The manager said the title was the last one missing from the cabinet, and the players had asked for it in the summer. The manager has now won every trophy available to him at the club, and the players said the record was the reason for the season.

From 2025 the field expands to 32 teams, and the format moves to the summer. A format that grows is a calendar that is crowded. The new format will have 12 European clubs and 6 South American ones, and the prize money will be about 2 billion dollars. The European clubs have won the tournament in every year but one since 2007, and the record is the argument for the bigger field.

What the match shows is that the gap between the European champions and the South American ones has widened, and the widening is why the tournament is being rebuilt. A title that completes the set is a title that the club will use to sell the brand, and the selling is the business of the modern game.
