description: "The largest memory maker told investors that demand for conventional chips is weak and that the product its customers are queuing for is the one it has struggled to qualify."
---

Samsung's memory business reported a quarter in which conventional dynamic memory sold at prices below what the company had planned, and the high-bandwidth memory that the accelerator business needs was still being qualified by its most important customer. The result is a portfolio that is pointed the wrong way, with volume in the products that are cheap and delays in the product that is scarce. A portfolio that is long in the cheap product is a portfolio that loses money when the cycle turns.

High-bandwidth memory is made by stacking dies and connecting them through thousands of vertical channels, and the stacking is where the yield is lost. A stack of eight or twelve dies has to work as one device, and a single bad die ruins the stack. The process is closer to packaging than to wafer fabrication, and the expertise is concentrated. A stack that is twelve dies high is a stack whose thermal budget is as tight as its wiring. A channel that is a few microns wide is a channel that a machine drills and a test verifies.

Qualification is the gate. A customer that puts memory beside an accelerator runs it through its own tests, and a supplier that is not qualified cannot sell into the largest programme. Samsung has said it expects to qualify, and the delay has cost it the part of the cycle when prices are highest. A customer's qualification is a process that takes months and cannot be bought.

Conventional market is in a downturn that the company helped create. Every memory maker added capacity during the previous boom, and the capacity arrived just as demand for consumer devices flattened. A commodity that is produced by three firms is a commodity whose price collapses when all three expand at once. A commodity that three firms make is a commodity that three boards decide the price of. A price that is set by the least efficient producer is a price that the efficient producers barely cover.

Strategic response has been to shift capacity towards the advanced product. A line that can make high-bandwidth memory can also make conventional memory, and the allocation is a decision about which customer to serve. A company that moves capacity to the scarce product reduces its own revenue in the short term and protects its position in the long one. A line that is converted is a line that is unavailable for the other product for a quarter.

What the quarter exposes is that memory has become a specialty business with a commodity tail. The firms that can qualify for the accelerator programmes earn a premium, and the firms that cannot compete on a price that is set by the cost of the least efficient producer. A premium that comes from qualification is a premium that lasts as long as the qualification does. A customer list that is short is a customer list that every supplier courts.
