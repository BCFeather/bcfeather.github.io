Broadcom reported a revenue of 8.9 billion dollars for the quarter to July, up about 5 per cent, and a net profit of 3.3 billion. The earnings per share of 10.54 dollars beat the forecast of 10.43. The shares fell about 4 per cent as the guidance for the current quarter was a little below the estimate. The revenue was about 100 million above the estimate, and the operating margin was about 57 per cent. The company said the currency effects had subtracted about 1 percentage point from the revenue. The company said the bookings were about 9.5 billion dollars, and the backlog of the AI orders was about 10 billion.

Its AI business did the work. The revenue from the AI networking was about 3.1 billion dollars, up about 100 per cent, and the company said the custom chips for the cloud customers had been the largest part. The enterprise and the industrial revenue fell by about 5 as the customers cut the spending. The networking revenue was about 2.1 billion dollars, and the one of the wireless about 1.5 as the phone cycle stayed weak. The company said the custom accelerators had been sold to three of the large cloud customers.

Margins held. The gross margin was about 74 per cent on the adjusted basis, and the one of the software about 90. The company said the mix of the AI products had lifted the hardware margin by about 2 points. The company said the operating expenses were about 1.3 billion dollars, and the one of the research about 800 million.

A VMware deal is the plan. The company said the purchase of the software company would close in the autumn after the regulatory approvals in the large markets, and the debt would rise to about 60 billion dollars. The management said the software would add about 12 billion of the revenue. The company has about 12 billion dollars of the cash, and the one after the deal would be about 3.

Guidance was cautious. The company expects the revenue of about 9.27 billion dollars in the current quarter, and the one of the AI about 4 billion. The market had expected about 9.4 billion, and the shares fell on the difference. The company said the dividend would rise by about 10 per cent, and the buyback was about 10 billion dollars on the annual basis.

This quarter is the one in which the AI networking became the story. A company that grows the AI revenue by 100 per cent while the enterprise falls is a company that has been chosen by the cloud builders, and the choice is the part the guidance shows. The shares are up about 50 per cent this year, and the company is the best performer among the large chip makers after NVIDIA.
