Bitcoin was trading at about 42,000 dollars on 29 December, up about 150 per cent over the year, after falling by about 64 per cent in 2022. The token is still about 40 per cent below the peak of about 69,000 dollars in 2021, and the other large tokens rose by less. The token began the year at about 16,600 dollars, and it has more than doubled since October alone.

Fund applications did the work. Asset managers filed the applications for the spot funds in the summer, and the market has been pricing the approval since. A filing that is expected is a price that is bought. About a dozen applications are pending, and the commission has a deadline in the first week of January for the first of them. The token fell by about 64 per cent in 2022 after the collapse of the exchange and the lender, and the recovery began in January.

A supply cut is the second factor. The reward for the mining is cut in half every four years, and the next cut is due in the spring. A supply that is cut is a scarcity that is sold. The miners have been holding more of the tokens than they sell, and the reserves of the largest ones are near the record. The reward will fall from 6.25 tokens to 3.125 in the spring, and the miners have been buying the machines ahead of it.

Volume is thin. The trading has been about half the level of 2021, and the retail investors have not returned in the size they did in the last cycle. A market that is thin is a price that moves on the small orders. The futures market has been in a premium to the spot, which is the sign of the leverage in the trade. The number of the active addresses is about 10 per cent below the level of 2021, and the ones holding more than 1,000 tokens have grown.

Regulators are still wary. The chair of the securities commission has said the tokens are risky, and the banks have been told to keep away. A warning that is repeated is a market that is not legitimized. The exchanges have been paying the fines for the breaches of the rules, and the one of the largest has settled for about 4 billion dollars. The commission has lost the court cases about the rules, and the funds are the way it is being forced to move. The commission has approved the futures funds, and the spot ones are the difference the market has been buying.

What the year shows is that the price has recovered on the expectation of the funds and the usage has not, and the gap is the part the next cycle will test. A price that is driven by a filing is a price that the filing can undo, and the undo is the risk of the January. The price is a bet on the funds and the supply cut, and the two are the parts that next year will settle.
