Justine Triet won the Palme d'Or at the Cannes festival on 27 May for Anatomy of a Fall, a French courtroom drama about a writer whose husband is found dead below their chalet. The jury was led by Ruben Ostlund, who had won the prize the previous year. Triet is the third woman to take the award, after Jane Campion in 1993 and Julia Ducournau in 2021. The award was announced at the closing ceremony, and the director thanked the crew and the actors in a short speech. The film had been in the competition after a dispute with the French system over the window for the cinema release, and the director had criticized the government.

Its plot is a trial in the literal sense. The plot follows the prosecution of the novelist for the death of her husband, and the court hears the recordings of the couple's arguments. The performances and the script were the parts the critics praised, and the film has been sold to about 60 countries. The trial scenes take about half of the running time, and the camera stays in the courtroom for the cross-examinations. The film won the prize of the dog in the festival's side programme, and the animal has become a small celebrity in the press.

Second prize went to The Zone of Interest, Jonathan Glazer's film about the family of a commandant who lives beside a camp. The director accepted the prize and said the film was about the banality of the ordinary life beside the violence, and the jury called it the most formally radical of the selection. The film was shot in Poland with the German-language dialogue, and the family's house was built for the production beside the camp.

Acting prizes went elsewhere. Koji Yakusho won the best actor for Perfect Days, and Merve Dizdar won the best actress for About Dry Grasses. The best director prize went to Tran Anh Hung for The Pot-au-Feu, a film about a 19th-century cook. The actress had been the favourite of the critics, and the jury said the decision was close. The jury gave a special prize to the first feature of the Vietnamese director, and the selection was noted for the number of the debuts.

About 20 films competed, and the buyers returned in force after the pandemic years. The market reported about 1 billion euros of the deals, and the streamers were the largest spenders. The market reported the strong sales for the Asian and the Latin American distributors, and the streamers bought about 30 titles.

This prize is the one that sets the year's art-house schedule. A jury that gives the top award to a courtroom drama is a jury that has rewarded the writing over the spectacle, and the writing is what the distributors will sell. The festival will be held in May again next year, and the director of the event has said the selection would be smaller.
