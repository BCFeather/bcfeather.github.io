Democratic Party and its satellite list won about 175 of the 300 seats in the National Assembly election of the tenth of April, according to the count published by the election commission, while the governing People Power Party and its ally took about 108. Turnout was 67 per cent, the highest for a general election in decades, and the result was a larger majority for the opposition than the one it won in 2020.

System that produced the seat totals is unusual. South Korea uses a mixed system in which voters cast two ballots, one for a district representative and one for a party list, and the two main parties created satellite parties to maximize their list seats. That practice has been criticized as a way of gaming the proportional rules, and both parties used it again in April.

Campaign was dominated by the cost of living and by the president's style. Prices for food and for energy rose faster than wages, and the government's attempt to control them through price caps on some staples was described by economists as ineffective. Yoon's approval rating had fallen to about thirty per cent, and the opposition made him the subject of the election rather than the candidates.

Specific controversies that hurt the governing party included a dispute over the appointment of a minister who had defended the country's colonial past, and a row over a plan to increase the number of medical school places, which the doctors' association opposed with a strike. Neither issue was as important as the general sense, reported in surveys, that the president did not listen.

Consequences for the president are institutional. Yoon has three years left and a parliament that can block his legislation, reject his appointments and investigate his administration. He said after the result that he would change his approach and work with the assembly, and he appointed a new prime minister after Han Duck-soo and several senior aides resigned. The opposition said it would use its majority to pursue investigations into the first lady and into the government's handling of a marine's death.

The assembly also has the power to confirm the prime minister, and the opposition said it would use that power to press for a figure acceptable to both sides. Several of the president's own legislators said after the result that they expected him to change his staff and his approach to the press. The result also ended the career of several senior figures in the governing party, who announced that they would not stand again.

Meaning of the tenth of April is a presidency that has lost the ability to set the agenda. Yoon was elected in 2022 by a margin of less than one percentage point, and his party has now lost two elections in a row. The result does not remove him from office, and it does not give the opposition the two thirds needed to impeach him. What it does is to make every piece of legislation a negotiation and to turn the remaining years of his term into a contest over the next presidency.
