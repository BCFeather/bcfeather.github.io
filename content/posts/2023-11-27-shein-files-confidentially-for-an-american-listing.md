Shein has filed the confidential paperwork for a listing in America, the company confirmed on 27 November, and the offering is expected to be one of the largest in New York in years. The filing is confidential, which means the details will be public only about three weeks before the roadshow. The company has been based in Singapore since last year, and the filing lists the Cayman entity as the issuer.

Valuation is the question. The company was valued at about 66 billion dollars in the funding round in the spring, and the bankers have been sounding the investors about a range below that. A valuation that falls is a listing that is delayed. The company has been raising the prices by about 10 per cent this year, and the analysts say the valuation depends on the margin that follows. The investors who bought in the spring have the shares at about 66 billion dollars, and the bankers say the range would be 55 to 60.

Washington is the obstacle. The lawmakers have asked the regulators to examine the supply chain, and the tariff exemption for the parcels under 800 dollars has been challenged in the Congress. A rule that is questioned is a model that is exposed. The exemption allows the parcels below 800 dollars to enter without a duty, and the bill in the Congress would end it for the apparel.

Price is the business. The company ships the clothes directly from the factories in China, and the average order is about 70 dollars with the delivery in about a week. A parcel that is cheap is a shop that is not needed. The company has about 10,000 suppliers in the Guangzhou area, and it keeps about 1 per cent of the inventory that a traditional retailer holds. The company has been adding the warehouses in the American market, and it has opened a distribution centre in Indiana this year.

Growth has slowed. The revenue was about 23 billion dollars last year, and the analysts say the growth this year has been in the single digits as the competitors copied the model. A model that is copied is a margin that is lost. The number of the active users is about 90 million, and the app has been the most downloaded shopping app in the American stores this year. The company has been expanding into the home goods and the cosmetics, and the category count is now above 20.

What the filing shows is that the company wants the American capital before the American rules change, and the timing is the strategy. A company that files before the rules change is a company that has read the calendar, and the reading is the risk. The offering would be the first large test of the appetite for the Chinese-founded firms in the American market since the crackdown. The bankers expect the offering in the first half of next year, and the window depends on the market for the new listings.
