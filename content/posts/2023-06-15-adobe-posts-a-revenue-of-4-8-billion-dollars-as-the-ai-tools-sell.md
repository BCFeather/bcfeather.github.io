Adobe reported a revenue of 4.82 billion dollars for the quarter to June, up 10 per cent, and a net profit of 1.30 billion. The earnings per share of 2.82 dollars beat the forecast of 2.79. The shares rose about 5 per cent after the report, and the company raised the revenue forecast for the year. The revenue was about 100 million above the estimate, and the constant currency growth was about 11 per cent. The company said the remaining performance obligations were about 15 billion dollars, up about 12 per cent.

Subscriptions did the work. The digital media revenue rose by about 10 per cent to 3.5 billion, and the net new annualized revenue was about 470 million, above the guidance of 400. The company said the bundles had sold better than the single applications, and the pricing change of the spring had gone through with little churn. The creative revenue rose by about 9 per cent, and the one of the document products by about 12. The churn from the price increase was about 1 per cent, which the company said was below the plan.

AI tools were the news. The company said the generative features had been used about 10 billion times since the launch in March, and the Firefly model had been trained on the licensed images. The enterprise customers had signed about 30 agreements for the indemnified version, and the demand was the part the guidance rests on. The Firefly model has generated about 500 million images, and the company says the output is labelled as the synthetic.

Document cloud grew by about 12 per cent. The revenue was about 700 million dollars, and the company said the sign-ups had accelerated with the new pricing. The marketing cloud grew by about 10 per cent, and the company said the pipeline for the second half was the strongest in two years. The marketing cloud added about 50 million dollars of the revenue on the quarter, and the analytics products were the strongest.

Costs were flat. The headcount was about 26,000 after the cuts of the winter, and the operating margin was about 35 per cent. The company said the investment in the AI models had been offset by the lower spending on the sales and the marketing. The company has cut about 1,000 jobs since the winter, and the savings are about 400 million on the annual basis.

Its forecast was raised. The company expects the revenue of about 19.3 billion dollars for the year, up from 19.2, and the earnings per share of about 15.65. A software company that grows by 10 per cent while it sells the generative tools is a company that has found the next product, and the product is the part the market is paying for. The shares are up about 40 per cent this year, and the company is the best performer in the large application software group.
