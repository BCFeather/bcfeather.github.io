NVIDIA became the most valuable listed company in the world on 18 June, closing with a market value of about 3.34 trillion dollars and passing Microsoft, which had held the position since the winter. The shares have risen by more than 160 per cent this year. The move had been expected since the previous week, when the gap between the two companies had narrowed to a few billion dollars, and the session that decided it was quiet.

Concentration is the feature of the move. The company's value now exceeds the entire energy sector of the index, and it is worth more than the banks of several large countries, which is a comparison that the index providers have begun to discuss. A single stock that is larger than a sector is a stock that changes how the index behaves. The funds that track the index have to buy the shares in proportion, and that buying has been one of the reasons the price has risen faster than the earnings.

Guidance issued in May is the foundation. The company said in May that its revenue would reach about 28 billion dollars in the current quarter, and it announced a ten-for-one split, and the customers who account for most of the revenue have said they will keep spending. A number that is confirmed by order books is a number that the market can price. The customers are the cloud operators that report their own capital spending, and the sum of their budgets is the best guide that the market has to the demand.

Packaging capacity is the constraint. The newest systems are sold out for the year, and the company has been paying its suppliers in advance to hold capacity, which is the behaviour of a firm that expects the demand to outlast the cycle. A customer who waits a year is a customer whose budget may change before the delivery. Capacity is being added at the packaging stage, the part of the process that the industry has found hardest to expand, and the shortage is expected to last into next year.

Comparisons with the previous leaders are the instructive part. The companies that held the position before were consumer franchises with a decade of earnings behind them, and the new leader sells a component whose demand depends on a technology that is two years old. A valuation that is built on a cycle is a valuation that a cycle can end. The shares trade at about forty times the earnings expected next year, a multiple that assumes the growth continues at the pace of the last four quarters.

What the session shows is that the market has decided that the demand for computing is the largest story in the economy, and the price of one company is now the measure of how much it believes. A market that is led by one company is a market whose index is a bet on one product, and the bet is now the largest single position that most savers hold.
