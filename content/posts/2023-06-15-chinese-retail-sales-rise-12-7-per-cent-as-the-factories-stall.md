---

China's retail sales rose by 12.7 per cent over the year in May, above the forecast of about 10 per cent, as the comparison with the lockdowns of 2022 lifted the number. The industrial output rose by 3.5 per cent, below the estimate of 4, and the fixed asset investment by 4 per cent in the first five months. The figure was the fastest since the middle of 2021, and the month-on-month change was about 1.3 per cent. The survey of the households by the central bank showed the confidence at the lowest since 2009, and the saving rate above the level of 2019.

Consumption is recovering slowly. The two-year average growth of the retail sales is about 3.5 per cent, which is well below the pace of 2019, and the household surveys show the intent to spend is still weak. The catering revenue rose by about 35 per cent, and the travel bookings reached the level of 2019 in the holiday weeks. The sales of the cosmetics rose by about 12 per cent, and the one of the jewellery by about 25.

Factories are the weak part. The output of the steel fell by about 3 per cent, and the one of the cement by about 8 as the construction slowed. The cars rose by about 20 per cent, and the exports of the electric vehicles were about 500,000 in the first five months. The output of the industrial robots fell by about 5 per cent, and the one of the integrated circuits by about 2.

Property is still contracting. The investment in the real estate fell by about 7.2 per cent in the first five months, and the new starts by about 22. The sales of the new homes fell by about 0.9 per cent in May, and the prices in the largest cities fell by about 0.2 on the month. The land sales by the local authorities fell by about 20 per cent, and the revenues of the local governments are the part the property crisis has hit.

Youth unemployment was about 20.8 per cent in May, the highest on record for the second month. The government has promised the support for the graduates and the private firms, and the measures announced in the spring have been small. The government has announced about 1 trillion yuan of the special bonds for the infrastructure, and the spending has been slow to start.

These figures leave the case for the stimulus unresolved. A retail sector that grows by 12.7 per cent on a weak base while the factories slow is an economy that needs the demand, and the demand has not arrived. The economists expect the second quarter to grow by about 6 per cent on the base of the lockdowns, and the third to slow to about 4.
