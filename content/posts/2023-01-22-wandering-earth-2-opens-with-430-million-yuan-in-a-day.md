---

Opening on 22 January, the first day of the lunar new year, the film took about 430 million yuan at the box office that day. It runs 173 minutes, longer than most releases in the holiday slot, and cinemas scheduled it four times a day rather than six. The first instalment had been the surprise hit of 2019, and expectations for the sequel had been building for three years. Advance sales opened a week earlier and reached 200 million yuan before the first screening, which is high for the slot.

Story and structure make it a prequel, which is unusual for a sequel and useful for a production that had to rebuild its world. It follows the construction of the engines that move the planet, and most of the running time is spent on engineering problems rather than on battles. Reviews compared it to the Hollywood films it borrows from and said the effects were better than the budget suggested. Trade reviews praised the production design and criticised the length, and the audience score ran higher than the critics' score.

Production cost about 600 million yuan, according to the studio, and much of it went to sets that were built rather than drawn. The production designer said the team had built 200,000 square metres of sound stages, and that the largest set was a rotating structure weighing 300 tonnes. Chinese effects houses did most of the work, and the studios involved have since been hired for other productions. The sets were built in a studio outside Qingdao, and the production employed 2,000 people at its peak.

That slot is the most valuable of the year, and the film shared it with six others. Ticket prices in the largest cities were about 55 yuan, higher than in previous years, and the average across the country was 48. Analysts said the total for the holiday week would decide whether the year could recover to the 2021 level. Concessions and merchandise added to the takings, and cinema chains reported their best week since 2021.

Overseas release was limited but larger than for the first film. It opened in 30 markets, including the United States and Britain, and earned 20 million dollars outside the country in the first two weeks. Distributors said the audience was mostly the diaspora, and that the reviews in the English-language press were mixed. The overseas release was handled by a distributor in Los Angeles, and the subtitles were made in two versions.

Its run finished in March at about 4 billion yuan, which made it the second-highest grossing release of the year behind a comedy about a detective. Streaming rights were sold in the spring, and the studio said a third film was in preparation, with a release planned before 2027. The studio has said the third film will shoot in 2024, and the director has not yet confirmed the cast.
