Target reported a revenue of 24.8 billion dollars for the quarter to July, down about 5 per cent, and a profit of 835 million. The earnings per share of 1.80 dollars beat the forecast of 1.39. The comparable sales fell by about 5.4 per cent, and the shares rose about 3 per cent as the margin improved. The revenue was about 200 million above the estimate, and the operating margin was about 5 per cent. The company said the digital sales fell by about 10 per cent, and the same-day services were about flat. The company said the comparable sales in the digital channel fell by about 10 per cent, and the one in the stores by about 4.

Discretionary goods were weak. The apparel fell by the high single digits, and the home goods by the mid single digits. The food and the beauty were about flat, and the essentials have been holding the traffic. The beauty grew by about 8 per cent, and the one of the food by about 3 as the customers traded down. The company said the food and the beauty had gained about 2 points of the share of the sales, and the apparel had lost about 3.

Margins improved. The gross margin was about 27 per cent, up about 2 points, as the markdowns and the freight costs fell. The company said the inventory was down about 8 per cent, and the clearance had been lighter than a year ago. The company said the freight costs had fallen by about 20 per cent from the peak, and the effect on the margin was about 1 point.

Theft is the problem. The company said the shrink had cut about 0.5 points from the margin, and the organised retail crime had been the largest part. The management said the locks and the staffing would cost about 500 million dollars this year. The company has about 1,900 stores, and the one of the new small-format stores is about 20 this year. The company has been closing about 10 of its stores a year, and the remodels have been about 200 this year.

Its guidance was kept. The company expects the comparable sales to fall by the mid single digits for the year, and the earnings per share of about 7 to 8 dollars. The market had expected a cut, and the shares rose on the confirmation. The company said the back-to-school season had started slowly, and the one of the holiday would be the test.

This quarter is the one the essentials carried. A retailer that earns 835 million dollars while the apparel falls is a retailer that is leaning on the grocery, and the grocery is the part that the margin cannot grow. The shares are down about 10 per cent this year, and the company is the weaker of the two large discount retailers.
