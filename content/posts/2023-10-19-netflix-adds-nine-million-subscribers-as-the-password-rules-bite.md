Netflix added 8.8 million subscribers in the third quarter, the company said on 18 October, taking the total to 247 million, and the revenue rose 7.8 per cent to 8.5 billion dollars. The profit was 1.7 billion dollars, and the shares rose about 12 per cent after the results. The figure was above the 6 million that the company had forecast, and the shares were among the best performers of the year in the technology sector.

A password crackdown produced the growth. Netflix has been charging the households that share an account outside the home, and about 30 per cent of the borrowers have converted into the paying members, which the company said was better than it had forecast. A rule that is enforced is a revenue that appears. The company said the enforcement had been rolled out in 100 countries by the summer, and the cancellations were fewer than the converts, which is the arithmetic of the plan.

Advertising is the second lever. The cheaper plan with the advertisements has about 15 million users, and the company said it will raise the price of the basic plan in America to 11.99 dollars and the premium to 22.99. A price rise after a growth quarter is a company that believes it has the power. The advertising business is still small, and the company said it will not be material to the revenue until 2025, which the investors have accepted.

Content is thinner because of the strikes. The writers and the actors stopped work for the summer, and the company said the schedule for next year would be affected, which is the reason the shares gave up part of the gain in the days after. A pipeline that is delayed is a churn that arrives in the spring. The production in Britain and the rest of Europe continued during the American strikes, and the company has been shifting part of the schedule to those studios.

Free cash flow was 1.9 billion dollars, and the company said it would buy back 2.5 billion dollars of the shares. The borrowings are about 14 billion dollars, and the company has been paying them down from the cash. A balance sheet that is repaired is a company that can choose. The company has said it will not raise the prices again this year, and the analysts expect another increase in the spring as the churn settles.

What the quarter shows is that the growth of the streaming business now comes from the price and the enforcement rather than from the new markets, and the price is the part that the subscribers can refuse. A subscriber who pays more is a subscriber who is asked to value the library, and the library is what the cash is buying. The library is what the cash is buying, and the cash is the part that the shareholders have begun to value again.
