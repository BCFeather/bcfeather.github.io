Brian Harman won the Open Championship at Royal Liverpool on 23 July, finishing 13 under par and six strokes ahead of the field. Scottie Scheffler, Sepp Straka, Tom Kim and Jon Rahm were tied for second at seven under. The win is the first major for Harman, who is 36. He had won twice on the tour before, and the best finish in a major was the second at the US Open in 2017. The purse was about 16.5 million dollars, and the winner's share was about 3. He said the week had been the first in which he had felt no nerves on the greens, and the caddie had been the one who kept him calm.

His putter did the work. Harman gained about eight strokes on the greens over the week, and he holed about 60 feet of putts in the third round. He said afterwards that the reads had been the best of his career, and the speed of the greens had suited him. He hit about 80 per cent of the fairways, which is the highest of the field, and the driver was the club he cited. The third round of 69 was the only one under 70 on the Saturday, and the lead grew from one to five.

Weather was the test. The first round was played in the rain and the wind, and the average score was about 74.5. Harman opened with a 67 and led from the second day, and the lead was never less than three strokes after the turn. The wind was about 20 miles an hour on the Friday, and the afternoon starters lost about two strokes to the morning ones. The rain came on the Thursday and the course took about 0.3 inches, and the greens held the approach shots.

Royal Liverpool was hard. Royal Liverpool played to about 7,300 yards with the rough grown and the bunkers deep, and the winning score of 13 under was the lowest since 2017. The par fives played about even for the field, and the short holes were the ones that decided the week. The rough was about five inches, and the players who missed the fairway averaged about a stroke more on the hole.

Contenders faded. McIlroy finished in the top ten for the fifth time in six years, and the putter was the part he lost again. Rahm had the best ball-striking of the field on the weekend, and the four bogeys on Saturday were the damage. The Spaniard finished at seven under, and the American at the same score, and the two were the only ones within 10.

Victory moves Harman to about 10th in the world from 26th. A player who wins by six on a links course is a player who has mastered the wind, and the wind is the part the modern tour rarely tests. The next major is the PGA Championship in May 2024, and the tour returns to the links in two years.
