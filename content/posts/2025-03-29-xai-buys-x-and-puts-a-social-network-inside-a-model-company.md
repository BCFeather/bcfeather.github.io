Both companies were already intertwined. xAI engineers worked on the platform's systems, the model called Grok was trained on posts from the platform, and the two companies shared staff and office space. The transaction formalized an arrangement that had been operating informally since xAI was founded in 2023, and it gave the model company the distribution channel that it had been borrowing.

The price is a mark for both assets. Musk bought Twitter for 44 billion dollars in 2022 and loaded it with debt, and the equity value of 33 billion is a discount to that purchase and a recovery from the level at which the banks had marked the loans a year earlier. xAI was valued at 400 billion in a funding round in December and at 80 billion in the combination, which is a different number for the same company and reflects the terms that the parties agreed rather than a market price.

The strategic argument is about data and distribution. A model company needs a stream of text that is fresh and large, and a social network produces one, and the platform needs a reason to be used by people who have alternatives. Combining the two gives the model a proprietary corpus and the network a product, and the risk is that the corpus is the posts of a platform whose users are a subset of the population.

Regulators will look at the structure. The deal combines two companies under common control, which is the kind of transaction that competition authorities examine for the effect on rivals, and the platform is a venue where those rivals also speak. A company that owns the square and the microphone in the square has an advantage that the antitrust law of both the United States and the European Union treats with suspicion.

Advertising is the business that has to fund it. The platform's revenue comes from advertisers who buy attention, and the model is a cost centre that consumes compute, and the combined company has to persuade the advertisers that the artificial intelligence makes the platform a better place to buy space. That argument is the one that the platform's own financial results had not supported before the transaction.

Employees and users reacted in the way that they react to a Musk announcement. Some users said they would leave, and the platform's traffic had already been falling, and some advertisers said they would wait to see whether the content moderation changed. A transaction that is announced in a post is a transaction whose reception is visible in the same place, and the visibility cuts both ways.

What the deal does for the industry is to make a vertical merger the model for artificial intelligence companies. A laboratory that owns a distribution channel, a corpus and a billing relationship is a laboratory that does not have to pay a platform for access, and the competitors that lack one will either build their own or buy one. That is the pattern that the transaction establishes, and the regulators will decide how much of it is allowed.
