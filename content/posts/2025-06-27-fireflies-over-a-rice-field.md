A firefly is a beetle that produces light through a chemical reaction, and the light is a mating signal. The adults live for a few weeks, they do not feed, and their whole purpose is to find each other in the dark. That is why artificial light is fatal to them: a street lamp that outshines the insect's signal removes the only thing it does.
