JPMorgan reported a profit of 14.4 billion dollars for the quarter to June, up about 67 per cent, and a revenue of 41.3 billion. The earnings per share of 4.37 dollars beat the forecast of 3.96, and the shares rose about 1 per cent. The net interest income was about 21.9 billion, up about 30 per cent. The revenue was about 2 billion above the estimate, and the return on the tangible equity was about 25 per cent. The bank said the gain from the purchase of the failed First Republic had added about 2 billion dollars to the profit.

Rates did the work. The bank has been earning more on the reserves than it pays on the deposits, and the gap widened as the rate rises passed through. The management said the net interest income would be about 87 billion dollars for the year, up from the 84 billion it had guided in the spring. The yield on the earning assets was about 5.4 per cent, and the cost of the deposits about 2.1. The management said the net interest income would fall in the next year as the deposit costs catch up.

Provisions rose. The bank set aside about 2.9 billion dollars for the credit losses, up from 1.1 a year ago, and the charge included about 0.9 billion for the commercial real estate. The office loans have been the part the market watches, and the bank said the losses were covered. The net charge-offs were about 1.4 billion dollars, and the one of the card business about 1 billion. The bank said the office loans were about 4 per cent of the total, and the losses on them had been about 0.3 billion so far this year.

Trading was weaker. The markets revenue fell by about 2 per cent, and the one of the investment banking by about 6 as the deals stayed scarce. The bank said the pipeline for the second half was better than a year ago, and the equity capital markets had started to reopen. The fixed income trading revenue was about 4.6 billion dollars, and the one of the equities about 2.1.

Deposits fell. The average deposits were about 2.4 trillion dollars, down about 6 per cent, as the customers moved the money to the higher-yielding products. The bank has been borrowing from the Federal Home Loan banks to replace them, and the cost is the part the margin will show. The bank had about 4 trillion dollars of the assets, which is the largest of the American banks.

This quarter is the one the rate cycle pays for. A bank that earns 14.4 billion dollars while it builds the reserves is a bank that is using the good times to prepare, and the preparation is the part the regulators have asked for. The shares are up about 15 per cent this year, and the bank is the best performer among the large lenders.
