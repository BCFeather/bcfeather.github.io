Moana 2 opened on 27 November and by the end of the five-day holiday had taken an estimated 221 million dollars in the United States and Canada and 386 million worldwide. That is the biggest five-day opening any animated film has recorded, and it beat a record set earlier in the year by a film about a video game character. A sequel that arrives four years after its predecessor is a sequel that has been waiting for an audience to grow up.

Holiday frame is the reason the number is so large. Thanksgiving is a four-day weekend in which cinemas sell tickets to families, and a release timed to that weekend gets a boost that a release in October does not. A studio that plans a Thanksgiving launch is a studio that is buying the busiest week of the autumn. A studio that owns the holiday weekend owns the year's biggest opening.

Film also had two competitors in the same market. A musical adaptation and a historical epic opened within a week, and the three together produced the largest Thanksgiving box office the industry has measured. A market with three wide releases is a market that can absorb them, which is what the exhibition business had been waiting for. A market that can carry three wide releases is a market that has recovered from the pandemic's collapse. A frame with three wide releases is a frame that exhibitors had not seen since 2019.

Sequel's path is the unusual part. It began as a series for a streaming service and was converted into a feature, which is why its release was announced late and its marketing was compressed. A film that arrives with a shorter campaign is a film that relies on the recognition of the first one. A series converted into a film is a project whose story was written for episodes rather than for a two-hour arc.

Audience for the first film explains the opening. Moana was released in 2016, did modest business at the box office and became one of the most watched titles on streaming, and the children who watched it repeatedly are now old enough to buy tickets. A property that grew through repetition is a property that a studio can revive. A title that a child watches twenty times is a title that a parent will pay to see again in a cinema.

What the weekend shows is that the theatrical business is still capable of a surprise when a title lands on the right date. The industry had spent the year announcing that audiences had left, and the holiday proved that they leave for the films they do not want to see. A holiday that produces a record is a holiday that will be used to schedule the next record. A record that is set over a holiday is a record that the next holiday will be scheduled against.
