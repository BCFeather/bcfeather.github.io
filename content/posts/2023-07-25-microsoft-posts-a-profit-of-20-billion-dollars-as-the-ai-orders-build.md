---

Microsoft reported a revenue of 56.2 billion dollars for the quarter to June, up 8 per cent, and a net profit of 20.1 billion. The earnings per share of 2.55 dollars beat the forecast of 2.53. The shares fell about 2 per cent as the growth in the cloud was a little below the estimate. The revenue was about 500 million above the estimate, and the operating income was about 24.3 billion. The company said the currency effects had subtracted about 3 percentage points from the revenue and about 4 from the cloud.

Azure did the work. The revenue from the Azure and the other cloud services rose by about 26 per cent on the constant currency, and the total cloud revenue was about 30.3 billion dollars, which is the first quarter above 30. The company said the AI services had added about 2 points to the growth. The Azure growth was about 27 per cent on the reported basis, which is the slowest in three years. The company has about 1,100 customers using the OpenAI models, and the number has doubled since the spring.

Its office business held. The revenue from the productivity suite rose by about 10 per cent, and the number of the commercial seats was about 400 million. The company said the price increase of the spring had gone through with little churn, and the Copilot features had been sold as an add-on. The revenue from the Dynamics rose by about 26 per cent, and the one from the LinkedIn by about 5.

Capital spending rose. The company spent about 10.7 billion dollars on the property and the equipment, up about 35 per cent, and the management said the spending would rise again in the current quarter. The data centres have been built for the AI training, and the capacity has been the constraint. The company said the depreciation would rise by about 1 billion dollars in the current year, and the gross margin would fall by about a point.

Guidance was mixed. The company expects the revenue of about 23.6 billion to 23.9 billion in the cloud in the current quarter, which is a little below the forecast, and the total revenue of about 59 to 60 billion. The capital spending for the year is expected to be about 40 billion dollars, up about 15 per cent from the previous year.

This quarter is the one in which the AI spending starts to show in the costs. A company that earns 20 billion dollars while it doubles the capital budget is a company that is buying the capacity for the demand it sees, and the demand is the part the guidance cannot yet measure. The shares are up about 40 per cent this year, and the company is the second largest by the market value.
