Samsung said on 9 January that its operating profit for the fourth quarter would be about 2.8 trillion won, down by roughly a third from a year earlier and below what the analysts expected. Revenue was about 67 trillion won. The figure was a third below the same quarter of the previous year and about a fifth below the consensus, and the shares rose anyway because the market had feared worse. The shares rose after the release because the market had feared a number closer to two trillion won.

Memory is the business that decides the result. The price of the chips that go into servers and phones fell through the year as the customers worked through the stock they had bought during the shortage, and the company's semiconductor division has now lost money for four quarters. A price that is set by the inventory of the customers is a price that the maker cannot control. The company's high-bandwidth memory, which is the type that the accelerators use, has been sold out, but it is a small share of the total output.

Samsung has been cutting production. It reduced the output of the older memory types and slowed the spending on new lines, which is the response that the industry has used in every previous downturn. A cut that reduces the supply is a cut that sets up the recovery. The capital spending for the year is expected to be lower than the previous one, which is the first annual decline since 2019.

Handsets are the steadier business. The handsets sell at a margin that is thinner than the chips but does not swing with the cycle, and the launch of the new models in January gives the quarter a floor. A product that is replaced every year is revenue that the calendar can predict. The phone margins have been supported by the sales of the cheaper models in the emerging markets, where the volumes are larger than in the rich ones.

Competitors are in the same position. The other two makers of memory have reported losses and have also cut the output, and the industry has a record of recovering within two quarters of a coordinated cut. A market with three suppliers is a market that can agree without meeting. The three suppliers have all announced cuts within a month of each other, and the price of the benchmark chip has stopped falling since December.

What the warning shows is that the demand for the chips that power artificial intelligence has not yet reached the memory makers in the volumes that the chip designers have seen, and the timing of that arrival is the whole of the investment case. A company that earns its profit from a commodity with a three-year cycle is a company whose shares move on the price of that commodity, whatever the rest of the business does.
