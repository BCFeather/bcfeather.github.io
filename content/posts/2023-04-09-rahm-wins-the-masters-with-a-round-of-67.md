Jon Rahm won the Masters on 9 April with a final round of 67, five under par, and a total of 12 under. Brooks Koepka finished second at eight under after a round of 75. The win is the second major for Rahm, who took the US Open in 2021. He finished four strokes clear of Koepka, and the margin was the largest at Augusta since 2019. The win was his eleventh on the tour, and the first since the US Open at Torrey Pines two years ago.

His round turned at the turn. Rahm was four strokes behind Koepka when he started the final day, and he birdied the third and the fourth before the leaders made the turn. He played the back nine in two under and did not drop a stroke after the eighth hole. He made the turn in three under for the day, and the birdie at the eleventh gave him the lead for good. His driving was the strength of the week: he hit about 80 per cent of the fairways and averaged about 300 yards off the tee.

Koepka faded after the turn. The four-time major winner had led by two after the third round, and he made the bogeys on the fourth, the ninth and the eleventh. He said afterwards that the pace of the greens had changed with the rain, and the putts that had dropped on Saturday slid past on Sunday. He had led after the second round and the third, and the lead was two when the final group started. He said the putter had felt different in the rain, and the three putts on the fourth set the tone for the afternoon.

Conditions helped. About 30 millimetres of rain fell overnight, and the course was softer than it had been all week, which rewarded the players who could stop the ball on the greens. The wind stayed down until the last hour, and the scores were the lowest of the week. The greens were measured at about 13 on the stimp meter before the rain, and they slowed to about 11 by the afternoon. The players who teed off early had the calmer wind, and the ones in the last groups played the harder half of the day.

Phil Mickelson finished tied for second at eight under with a round of 65. He is 52 years old and had missed the cut in three of the previous four Masters. A round of 65 at his age is the kind of number that the course gives once a decade. He made eight birdies and one bogey, and he played the four par fives in four under. He said the crowd had carried him through the back nine, and that he had never heard the noise on the sixteenth.
