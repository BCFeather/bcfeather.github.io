October opened the prizes' annual week, and the announcements carried a roll call more than a century old. The discoveries were recognized in the fields that the prizes cover, the careers were honoured with the citations that the committees prepare, and the fields' direction was signalled by the choices that the announcements made; the week is science's annual performance, and the performance's meaning is the measure of the work that the year's coverage otherwise cannot see. The announcements were made with the formality that the tradition requires, and the formality is the institution's guarantee that the choices will be weighed.

Substance draws the community's careful analysis, as the choices always command. The laureates' work, described in the citations that the committees draft, is the work that the fields have been building on, and the citations' emphasis signals the fields' current direction; the choices are the committees' reading of the science, and the reading's reception will be conducted through the week's commentary. The substance is the science, and the science is the week's real content.

Beyond the choices, ceremony carries a meaning accumulated over the prizes' history. The institutions that the prizes honour, the discoveries that the names carry, the stories that the laureates tell, all of it is the record of the science that the modern era has built, and the record's annual renewal is the week's demonstration of the science's continuity; the continuity is the reassurance that the coverage of the year's events cannot supply. The week is the science's annual reminder of its own depth, and the reminder is the prizes' lasting value.

Read against the week, the year's science appears as a record the citations assemble: the biology, the physics, the chemistry, the discoveries that the committees have judged most consequential. The record is the field's trajectory, and the trajectory's direction is the subject of the commentary that the week generates; the commentary will be conducted through the days of the announcements, and the conduct will be the week's intellectual content. The science is moving, and the movement's measure is the roll call.

The week's meaning will be completed by the ceremonies that the autumn will hold, the lectures, the banquets, the meetings that the laureates will attend, and the meaning will be the one that the prizes have always carried: the recognition of the work that the world's problems and the world's possibilities are built on. The scientific prizes opened their annual week with the roll call that the committees have conducted for over a century, and the roll call's names will be added to the record that the history of science keeps; the announcements were made, the commentary was begun, and the week's work, the recognition and the weighing, will be conducted through the autumn's ceremonies.
