A slow-moving storm system that meteorologists named Boris brought rainfall equal to a month's average in four days across parts of central Europe in mid-September, and rivers in Poland, the Czech Republic, Austria and Romania broke their banks. The death toll rose past twenty as the flood wave moved downstream. The system had been forecast a week earlier and the warnings had been issued in time, which is the part of the disaster that worked.

Rainfall was the record rather than the flood. Some stations in the Czech mountains recorded more than three hundred millimetres in four days, and the total across the region was the highest measured since records began. A storm that stalls is a storm that delivers its water to one place. The rivers that flooded are the ones that drain the mountains quickly, and a catchment that is steep and short gives the towns downstream only hours to prepare.

Damage is in the towns that were built on the flood plain. Embankments that had been raised after the floods of 1997 held in some places and failed in others, and the water entered streets that had been rebuilt with the understanding that the defence would work. A defence that is designed for a previous flood is a defence that has priced the previous flood. The towns that flooded in 1997 rebuilt with embankments and with a rule that new buildings go above the flood line, and the water this year reached the level of the older flood.

Politics are about money and insurance. Governments promised reconstruction funds within days, and the insurers said that premiums in the affected valleys would rise, which is the mechanism that moves people away from the risk without any policy saying so. A premium is a planning instrument that no ministry controls. The commission that manages the river basin has said that retention areas upstream would have reduced the peak, and the areas that were designated for that purpose have been built on.

Science is about a warmer atmosphere. Air that is one degree warmer holds about seven per cent more moisture, and a stalled system that has more water to give delivers more rain, which is the mechanism that connects a global average to a local record. A record that is set in four days is a record that the next decade will beat. The models that attribute a single event to a warmer climate take months to run, and the preliminary results for this storm will not be published until the winter.

What the flood shows is that the defences are calibrated to the past and the rainfall is not. A town that has rebuilt after three floods in thirty years is a town that is being asked to consider a different arrangement, and the arrangement will be about where not to build. The reconstruction will take years, and the towns that are rebuilt will be rebuilt to a standard that assumes the next flood is larger than the last.
