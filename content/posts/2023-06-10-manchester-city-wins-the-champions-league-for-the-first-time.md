Manchester City beat Inter Milan 1-0 in the Champions League final in Istanbul on 10 June, with Rodri scoring in the 68th minute. The win is the first European title in the club's history, and it completes the treble of the Premier League, the FA Cup and the Champions League. The club is the second English side to do it, after Manchester United in 1999. The goal came from a move that began with a throw-in, and the build-up was the least spectacular of the night. The club had lost the final in 2021 to Chelsea, and the memory was the one the players mentioned after the whistle.

A tight match produced few chances. City had about 60 per cent of the ball and about 15 shots, and the best chances fell to Erling Haaland and to Phil Foden. Inter defended deep and had two chances on the break, and the final was decided by one deflection. The expected goals were about 1.6 for City and about 0.8 for Inter, and the possession was the highest of any final since 2011. The Inter goal was ruled out for the offside in the second half, and the video check took about two minutes.

A deflection decided it. Rodri arrived at the edge of the box and side-footed a low shot that took a slight touch off a defender. It was his fourth goal of the season and the first in the competition, and he was named the player of the match. He said the shot had been the only one he had hit cleanly all season, and the players laughed at the comment.

Ederson was the reason. Ederson made three saves in the last 20 minutes, including one from Romelu Lukaku at close range. The Brazilian had been the subject of the criticism through the season, and the final was the answer. The defence had conceded about 0.9 goals per game in the competition, which is the best record of the season. He saved the first with his legs and the second with the chest, and the second save was the one the pundits replayed.

Guardiola has now won it. Pep Guardiola had not won the competition since 2011 with Barcelona, and the wait had been the one line missing from his record. He said afterwards that the trophy had been the obsession of the club since the owners arrived in 2008. He has now won the competition three times, and the club has spent about 1.5 billion pounds on the players since 2008.

A treble settles the argument about the season. A team that wins 89 points in the league and the European cup is the best side in Europe by the arithmetic, and the arithmetic is the part the arguments have not changed. The club's revenue was about 700 million pounds last year, the highest in the English game.
