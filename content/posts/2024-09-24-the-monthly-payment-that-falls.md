---

Central bank announced a package on the twenty-fourth of September, with a cut to the rate on existing loans. Average cut is about half a percentage point. It applies to loans that were signed before the end of August. The measure is aimed at the household rather than at the developer. A family that bought in 2021 at a rate of five per cent pays a monthly instalment that is a third of its income. A cut of half a point returns a few hundred yuan a month.

Banks carry the cost. A mortgage is the largest asset on a Chinese bank's book. A cut that is ordered by the regulator reduces the interest income on a stock of loans that is measured in tens of trillions of yuan. The compensation is in the funding cost. A cut to the reserve requirement ratio releases money that the bank does not have to hold. Cheaper funding is meant to offset the lost interest on the mortgage book.

The second-home rule changed as well. A buyer who already owns one property faces a deposit requirement of fifteen per cent rather than twenty-five, which is the same rate as a first-time buyer in most cities. The local governments are the ones who decide whether it applies. A city that still has a restriction on purchases may keep it. Cities that removed the restriction in the spring saw viewings rise briefly, then fade within a month.

Refinancing is the mechanism a household uses. A borrower who wants the new rate applies at the bank. Bank that holds the loan is required to offer the option. Process takes a month and it does not require a new valuation. The prepayment was the behaviour that the banks feared. A household with savings and a loan at five per cent is better off repaying than saving. A wave of prepayments in the spring reduced the interest income that the cut now reduces again.

The property market is the target, and the effect is slow. A rate cut changes the monthly cost. A buyer who is waiting for the price to stop falling is not moved by a hundred yuan a month. The message is the part that matters to the market. A package that arrives in one announcement rather than in six separate ones is read as a decision to act. Equities that had been falling for a year rose on the day.

Branches are where the measure is delivered. A mortgage desk that processed four refinancing applications a month processed forty in the first week, and the staff who explain the option to a customer do it with a printed table. The developer is the party that gains least. A company with unsold stock benefits from a buyer who is moved by the monthly payment, and the ones that are still building are waiting for a line of credit rather than for demand. The household that benefits does not spend it all. A saving of three hundred yuan a month goes first to the credit card, and the consumption that the measure expects arrives in the following quarter if it arrives at all.
