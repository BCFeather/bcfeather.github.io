Golden Globe nominations announced on 11 December put Barbie at the top with nine and Oppenheimer close behind with eight, and the ceremony is set for 7 January in Los Angeles. The nominations were the first under the new ownership, and the ceremony will be televised on a new network after the previous contract ended. The list was read out in the morning, and the ceremony will be the first since the body was sold to a new owner and its membership was rebuilt.

Box office is the backdrop. The two films were the largest earners of the summer, and the nominations confirm the awards season will be built around them. A film that sells is a film that the voters notice. Barbie has earned about 1.4 billion dollars, and Oppenheimer about 950 million, both far above the expectations of the studios. The two films were released on the same weekend in July, which the industry called the counter-programming, and both have been in the theatres for five months.

Acting races are crowded. The nominations put the three leads of Oppenheimer in the drama categories and the two of Barbie in the comedy ones, which is the split the studio had hoped for. A category that is crowded is a campaign that is expensive. The best drama actor list includes three of the Oppenheimer cast, and the film is also nominated for the directing and the score. The supporting actor lists are the most crowded, and the campaigns have been running since the autumn.

Television went to the streamers. The Bear and Succession led with five each, and the broadcast networks had almost nothing. A screen that is streamed is a network that is not watched. The comedy categories are led by the films with the smaller budgets, and the streamers took most of the television slots. The Bear and Succession are both on the streaming and the cable, and the broadcast networks were shut out of the main categories.

Its organizers are still repairing the ceremony after the disputes. The show was sold after the ethics scandals, and the new owners have added about 300 voters from the industry. A vote that is wider is a award that is harder to buy. The voters now include about 300 people from outside the United States, and the body says the membership is about 60 per cent larger than in 2020. The new owner is a private equity firm, and the sale price was about 50 million dollars for an award that once drew 20 million viewers.

What the list shows is that the awards season has a clear front-runner pair and a rebuilt voter pool, and the two together are the test of whether the ceremony can recover. An awards body that is rebuilt is a ceremony that must be believed, and the believing is the part that the ratings will show. The ratings for the ceremony have fallen for a decade, and the new owner has said the show will be shorter and funnier.
