Sun Yat-sen was born on 12 November 1866 in Cuiheng, a village in the Xiangshan county of Guangdong that now carries the name Zhongshan. The 160th anniversary falls on a Thursday this year, and the memorial sites around the village have been preparing the exhibitions since the summer. The village sits about 20 kilometers south of the city center, and the one of the roads to it is lined with the banyan trees he is said to have climbed. His father was a farmer with a small plot, and the one of the family moved to Honolulu when the boy was 13 to join his brother.

Its house is the center of it. The small brick building where he grew up has been kept as a museum since 1956, and the furniture inside is the one his family used. The site now manages about 160,000 square meters of the old village, which is a national first-class museum and the core of a five-star scenic area. The medical degree came from the college in Hong Kong in 1892, and the one of the practice he opened in Macau closed within a year. The society he founded in Honolulu in 1894 was the first of the organizations that would carry the movement, and the one of its branches spread through the overseas communities.

Visitors come in large numbers. The memorial museum received about 4.89 million visitors in 2025, and the single-day record was 49,088 on 1 October, which is the highest since the opening in 1956. The staff said the peak comes in the national holidays and around the anniversary, and the queue at the entrance runs down the lane. The entrance is free, and the one of the guided tours is booked out on the weekends.

Archive is the draw. The museum holds the letters he wrote from Honolulu and from Tokyo, the drafts of the three principles, and the objects he used in 1912. The curators said the collection had been built from the donations of the family and of the overseas Chinese communities, and the one of the photographs goes back to his student years. The reading room is open to the researchers by the appointment, and the one of the copies is kept in the digital form.

A wider map is the point. The mausoleum in Nanjing, where the body was moved in 1929, the memorial hall in Guangzhou that opened in 1931, and the former residence in Shanghai are all marking the day with the free entry and the lectures. The county of Xiangshan was renamed Zhongshan in 1925, the year he died, and the one of the cities has carried the name since. The mausoleum covers about 80,000 square meters, and the one of the steps to the tomb is 392.

This anniversary belongs to the founding generation. A man who died in 1925 at the age of 58 left a republic that had not yet held together, and the house in the village is the part that has held. The three principles were set out in the lectures of 1924, and the one of the books that collected them runs to about 400 pages.
