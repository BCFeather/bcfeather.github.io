Xiaomi opened orders for the Yu7 on the evening of 26 June, and within three minutes the company had 200,000 refundable reservations. Within an hour the figure was 289,000, and by the next morning it had locked 240,000 non-refundable orders. The numbers are the largest for any Chinese car launch, and they are a marketing event as much as a commercial one. The car is a five-metre electric SUV with a range of about 835 kilometres in the longest version.

The product is an SUV with a starting price of 253,500 yuan, three versions up to 329,900, and a standard fitment that includes a lidar and an 800-volt architecture.
