description: "The quarter to June grew by 11 per cent, and the North American retail returned to a profit as the cost cuts and the advertising paid off."
---

Amazon reported a revenue of 134.4 billion dollars for the quarter to June, up 11 per cent, and a net profit of 6.75 billion. The earnings per share of 0.65 dollars beat the forecast of 0.35 by a wide margin. The shares rose about 8 per cent after the report, and the guidance for the current quarter was above the estimate. The revenue was about 3 billion above the estimate, and the operating income was about 7.7 billion dollars. The company said the currency effects had subtracted about 1 percentage point from the revenue. The company said the revenue from the physical stores had been about flat and the one from the third-party sellers had grown by about 18 per cent.

Its retail turned. The North American segment produced an operating profit of about 3.2 billion dollars after the losses of the previous year, and the international segment about 0.9. The company said the cost per package had fallen, and the advertising revenue had grown by about 22 per cent. The advertising business was about 10.7 billion dollars in the quarter, and the one of the subscription services about 10. The company said the same-day delivery had reached about 90 per cent of the American population, and the cost of it had fallen.

Its cloud slowed. The revenue from the AWS was about 22.1 billion dollars, up 12 per cent, which is the slowest growth since the unit was broken out. The operating income was about 5.4 billion, and the company said the customers had been signing the shorter contracts. The AWS operating margin was about 24 per cent, down from 26 as the company spent on the AI capacity.

Layoffs are done. The company has cut about 27,000 jobs since the autumn, and the headcount was about 1.46 million, down about 4 per cent. The fulfilment network has been regionalized, and the company said the delivery times had improved. The company said the capital spending would rise to about 50 billion dollars this year, up from 48. The company has opened about 20 of the new fulfilment centres this year, and the robotics have cut the cost per item by about 10 per cent.

Guidance was strong. The company expects the revenue of about 138 to 143 billion dollars in the current quarter, and the operating income of about 5.5 to 8.5 billion. The market had expected about 138 billion. The shares are up about 55 per cent this year, and the company is the best performer in the large technology group after Meta.

This quarter is the one in which the retail business finally earns. A company that earns 6.7 billion dollars while the cloud grows by 12 per cent is a company whose retail has stopped being a subsidy, and the subsidy was the part the investors had given up on. The company has about 1.5 million employees, which is the largest private workforce in the world.
