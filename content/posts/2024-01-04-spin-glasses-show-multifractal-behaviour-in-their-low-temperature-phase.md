A spin glass is a magnet with competing interactions, and its low-temperature phase has been described without agreement on what the order looks like. A paper in *PNAS* on 4 January 2024 reports multifractality. A spectrum is a richer object than an exponent, and richer objects are what a mature debate moves towards. Statistical mechanics of disordered systems is a field that advances by simulation as much as by proof, and the machine is part of the method. A spectrum that a theory must reproduce is a benchmark, and a benchmark is what a field needs to compare models.

Multifractality means a single exponent does not describe the scaling, and a set of exponents is needed instead. The signature is a spectrum rather than a number. Purpose-built hardware is an argument in itself, since the statistics that a claim needs are the statistics that general hardware cannot supply. A method that is part of the argument is a method that has to be described in the paper, and the description occupies space. Simulation at this scale requires dedicated silicon, and dedicated silicon is a commitment that few groups can make.

The measurement comes from Janus II, a machine built to simulate spin glasses at a speed general-purpose hardware cannot reach. Purpose-built hardware is what makes the statistics adequate. Replica symmetry breaking was the proposal, and a multifractal spectrum is what the proposal did not anticipate. A commitment of that kind explains why the result arrived when it did, and timing is part of the scientific story. A protocol that travels is a protocol that other groups can run, and running it is what makes the benchmark a benchmark.

The low-temperature phase shows the signature in the overlap distribution. A broad spectrum is a distribution that neither replica symmetry nor simple symmetry breaking describes. Discriminating quantities are what turn a disagreement into an experiment, and the experiment is the contribution. A theory that reproduces the spectrum will be a theory that was built to, and building to a target is how theory progresses.

On the modelling side, the result refines a debate four decades old by supplying a quantity that the competing pictures predict differently. A quantity that discriminates is worth more than an argument. Ultrametric structure is the geometry of states, and a geometry that is confirmed constrains every later model. A benchmark is only useful if it is reproducible, and reproducibility in this field means the same machine and the same protocol.
