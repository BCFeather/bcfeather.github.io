Agreed on 9 February, a few hours before the deadline, the trade sent Kevin Durant and T.J. Warren to the Phoenix Suns. Brooklyn received Mikal Bridges, Cam Johnson, Jae Crowder and four unprotected first-round picks. A fifth pick was added through a swap, and two second-round picks completed the package. The deal was reported by two journalists within minutes of the agreement, and the team confirmed it after midnight.

Phoenix had been looking for a second scorer since the previous summer, when it lost in the second round of the playoffs. The team's new owner had said he would spend into the tax, and the deal took the payroll above the threshold. Durant was 34 and under contract for three more years, which made the picks the expensive part. The tax bill was estimated at 50 million dollars, and the owner said he was willing to pay it. Phoenix had four first-round picks available because it had traded down in two previous drafts. The four picks were 2023, 2025, 2027 and 2029, and the swap gave Brooklyn control of a fifth.

Brooklyn had built around three stars and had traded two of them within a year. The return was described by the front office as a reset around defence and depth, and the team went 13-13 after the deadline. Bridges averaged 26 points in that stretch, which was more than he had scored in any previous month. The team had also traded for a defensive wing in January, and the roster turned over by half. Brooklyn owed its own picks to Houston from the earlier Harden deal, which is why it wanted unprotected ones back.

Two other deals had already reshuffled the western conference. The Dallas Mavericks acquired Kyrie Irving from Brooklyn two days earlier, and the Los Angeles Lakers traded Russell Westbrook in a three-team deal. Three of the eight playoff teams in the conference changed their starting lineups within a week. Irving had asked for a trade in early February, and the Mavericks sent two players and a pick for him. Westbrook was owed 47 million dollars, and the Lakers attached a protected pick to move the contract. Two other playoff teams traded only second-round picks, and one traded a first-round pick for a salary dump.

Phoenix finished fourth in the conference and beat the Clippers in the first round. Durant played 8 games in the regular season for the new team and averaged 26 points, and the team lost in the second round to Denver. The picks Brooklyn received became the most valuable asset in any deal of the season. Denver won the series in six games, and the Suns' payroll for the following year passed 190 million dollars. Phoenix's owner had bought the team for 4 billion dollars in 2022, the largest price paid for a franchise.

Deadline day produced 14 trades involving 40 players, according to the league's summary. Two teams made no moves at all, and one traded its leading scorer for salary relief. The summer produced more, when a pair of free agents changed the map again.
