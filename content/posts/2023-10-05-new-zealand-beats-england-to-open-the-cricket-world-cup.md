New Zealand beat England by nine wickets in the opening match of the World Cup in Ahmedabad on 5 October, chasing 283 with 82 balls remaining. Devon Conway made 152 not out and Rachin Ravindra 123 not out, and the pair put on an unbroken 273. England made 282 for nine, with Joe Root top-scoring 77, and the total looked about 30 short on a pitch that offered the batsmen the ball coming on. The chase was the fourth-highest in a World Cup match, and the pair's stand was the largest for any wicket in the tournament's history.

Batting made the difference. England lost three wickets inside the first ten overs and never recovered the tempo, while the New Zealand openers played the spin from the fifth over onwards without a false shot. A chase that is planned is a chase that does not need the big hits. Conway faced 121 balls and hit 19 fours and a six, and Ravindra, who is 23, was playing his first World Cup match. England's seamers took the new ball well and then found no movement as the ball aged, and the spinners were not able to turn it past the bat.

A venue among the largest in the tournament holds, with the stadium holding about 130,000 people, and the crowd was reported at about 45,000 for a match that started in the afternoon heat. An empty seat in a vast stand is a reminder of how much of the tournament depends on the India matches. The tickets were priced from about 500 rupees, and the organizers have said the attendance for the India matches will be close to capacity. The tournament is being played across ten cities, and the organizers expect about a million spectators over the six weeks.

Shadows of 2019 hang over the result. The final at Lord's was decided by the count of boundaries after a tie and a super over, and the rule was changed afterwards so that another such finish would go to a second super over. A rule that is rewritten is an admission that the ending was wrong. The boundary count rule was abolished by the International Cricket Council within weeks of the 2019 final, and the change was made with the consent of both finalists. England had won the toss and chosen to bat, which the analysts said was the right call on a surface that had been used for the warm-up matches.

England's title defense now has a dent. The team has four years of the same squad and the same approach, and the captain said afterwards that the batting had been 20 runs short rather than the bowling being poor. A defeat in the opener is a table that is already uneven. England's next match is against Bangladesh in Dharamsala, and the conditions there favor the seamers more than the surface in Ahmedabad. The captain has been criticised for the selection of the squad, which has four all-rounders and only three specialist seamers.

What the match shows is that the format rewards the side that bats through rather than the side that hits hardest, and the tournament has nine weeks to test the proposition. A tournament of nine weeks is a tournament that rewards the squad rather than the eleven, and the squad is where the defending champions are thinnest. The nine-week schedule means that the side that starts well can manage the workload, and the workload is what wins the knockout matches.
