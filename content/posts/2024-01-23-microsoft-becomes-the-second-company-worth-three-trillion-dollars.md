Microsoft became the second company to be valued at more than three trillion dollars on 24 January, when its shares rose about 1.4 per cent in the session. Apple had crossed the level in December. The move came after the company announced that its assistant would be available to the business customers who had not yet bought it, which the analysts read as a price increase in disguise.

Valuation is a bet on a business that sells to other businesses. The company's cloud division grew by about a fifth last year, and the seats it sells for its office software renew every year, which gives the earnings a stability that a consumer product does not have. A subscription that renews is a cash flow that the market can discount. The commercial bookings grew faster than the revenue, which means the contracts that were signed will be recognized later, and the backlog is the number the analysts watch.

Artificial intelligence is the accelerant. The company has been charging for the assistants that it added to its software, and the customers who bought them have renewed at a rate the company describes as normal for a new product. A feature that is charged for is a feature that has to earn its price. The capital spending on data centres rose by more than a third, and the company has said the capacity it is building is pre-sold.

Comparison with the consumer company is instructive. Apple earns most of its revenue from a device that is replaced every few years, and Microsoft earns most of its revenue from contracts that renew, and the market has been willing to pay more for the second. A business that is boring and recurrent is a business that the market likes in a high-rate world. The valuation is about thirty-five times the earnings expected this year, which is above the average of the last decade and below the peak of 2021. The company has said the growth of the cloud will slow this year as the capacity is absorbed, and the guidance was the reason the shares moved modestly after the release.

Index concentration is the consequence. Two companies now account for about a tenth of the value of the American market, and the funds that track the index hold both in proportion. A market that is concentrated is a market whose index is a bet on a handful of firms. The other large technology companies are worth between one and two trillion, and the gap between the first two and the rest has widened.

What the session shows is that the largest companies are now valued on the durability of their contracts rather than on the growth of their products, and the mark is a statement about the kind of earnings the market wants. A company that reaches a round number is a company that the market uses as a symbol, and the symbol is worth more than the arithmetic behind it.
