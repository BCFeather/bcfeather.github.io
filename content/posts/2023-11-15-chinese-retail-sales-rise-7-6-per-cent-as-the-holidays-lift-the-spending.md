Chinese retail sales rose 7.6 per cent in October from a year earlier, the statistics bureau said on 15 November, up from 5.5 per cent in September and above the forecast of 7. The figure was the strongest since the spring, and the sales in the first ten months rose by about 7 per cent. The figure was the strongest since the spring of last year, and the sales of the cars rose by about 11 per cent as the discounts were extended.

Holidays did the work. The eight-day break at the start of the month brought about 800 million trips, and the spending on the travel and the restaurants rose by about 20 per cent. A holiday that is long is a till that is full. The box office over the holiday was about 2.7 billion yuan, and the domestic films took most of it. The average spending per trip was about 900 yuan, which the analysts said was below the level of 2019 in the real terms.

Industrial output rose by 4.6 per cent, and the production of the electric cars and the solar cells grew by about 30 per cent. A line that runs is a demand that the orders support. The output of the industrial robots fell by about 3 per cent, and the one of the cement fell by about 4 as the construction slowed. The output of the cars rose by about 9 per cent, and the exports of the electric ones reached about 1.2 million in the ten months.

Property kept falling. The investment dropped 9.3 per cent in the first ten months, and the sales of the floor space fell by about 8 per cent. A site that is unfinished is a balance sheet that is stuck. The floor space sold fell by 7.8 per cent, and the prices of the new homes in the largest cities fell by about 0.3 per cent on the month.

Unemployment was steady. The rate in the cities was 5 per cent, and the youth rate was about 17 per cent after the method changed in the summer. A job that is not found is a spending that is deferred. The number of the young people out of work is still above 2 million, and the government has promised the support for the hiring. The rate for the people aged 25 to 59 was about 4.2 per cent, and the one for the graduates is the part the policy is aimed at.

What the data show is that the consumer has responded to the holidays and the discounts while the construction has not responded to the stimulus, and the gap is the shape of the recovery. A recovery that runs on the holidays is a recovery that needs the next holiday, and the calendar is the constraint. The industrial output rose for a fifth month, and the retail has now beaten the forecast for two months in a row.
