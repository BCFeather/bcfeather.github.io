Everything Everywhere All at Once won seven awards, including best picture, best director for the pair known as Daniels, and three of the four acting prizes. The 95th Academy Awards were held on 12 March at the Dolby Theatre in Los Angeles. Michelle Yeoh became the first Asian woman to win best actress, and Ke Huy Quan won best supporting actor thirty-eight years after his first nomination as a child. The ceremony was the first since the academy added a category for popular film, which was later dropped.

Editing, original screenplay and supporting actress went to the same production, which cost about 15 million dollars. The film had eleven nominations and won in almost every category it could. That budget is small for a best picture winner, and the film had been made by a company that specializes in low-budget genre work. The Daniels had made two features before, and the film was shot in 38 days in Los Angeles.

Ratings rose 12 per cent on the previous year, to 18.7 million viewers, which is still among the lowest in the history of the broadcast. The ceremony had a single host for the first time since 2018, and it ran to three hours and forty minutes. The academy had cut two categories from the live show and restored them after complaints from members. The broadcast had lost a third of its audience since 2014, when 43 million people watched.

All Quiet on the Western Front, a German production for a streaming service, won four awards including international feature and cinematography. It had nine nominations, the most of any film, and it won fewer than expected. The awards for sound and production design went to smaller films, which is normal for the technical categories. The German film was made for a streaming service and had a budget of 20 million dollars.

Speeches were shorter than usual, and the academy had asked winners to keep to 45 seconds. The documentary prize went to a film about a Russian opposition leader, and the short film prizes went to productions from India and Ireland. The honorary award was given to a stunt performer, and the academy announced a new category for casting from 2026. The governors had voted on that change in the previous year. The honorary award had been given to a stunt performer only once before, in 1995. Two winners used the time to raise the war in Ukraine, and one spoke about the earthquake in Turkey. The orchestra played over three speeches, which is the standard cue and the standard complaint. The academy's members number about 9,000, and the acting branch is the largest.

What the result said about the industry was that the middle had fallen out. The best picture nominees included three films made for less than 20 million dollars and two made for more than 100 million. The films in between, which used to fill the category, were being made for streaming or not at all. The next ceremony was scheduled for 10 March 2024. Two of the best picture nominees were released in cinemas in fewer than 200 screens.
