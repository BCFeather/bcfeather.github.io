A first estimate for September put inflation in the eurozone at 1.7 per cent, below the central bank's two per cent target for the first time since 2021, and the figure was published two weeks before the governing council's next meeting. The debate inside the bank has moved from whether to cut to how quickly. The estimate is a flash reading based on the early national figures, and it will be revised once the full data for the month arrive in the middle of October.
