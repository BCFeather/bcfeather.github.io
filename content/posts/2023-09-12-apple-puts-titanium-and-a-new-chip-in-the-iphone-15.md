Apple introduced the iPhone 15 at its headquarters on 12 September, with a titanium frame, a new chip and a brighter screen. The phones go on sale on 22 September in most markets. The company also introduced the Watch Series 9 with a new chip and a gesture that lets the wearer answer a call by tapping two fingers. The company said the phones will be available in more than a hundred countries by the end of the month, and the pre-orders open on 15 September.

Titanium is the visual change. The metal is lighter than the stainless steel it replaces, and the company said the frame is stronger for the same weight. A material that saves grams is a design that the rivals will copy within a year. The titanium is about a third lighter than the steel, and the company said the phones will be the lightest of the large models.

Chip is the functional one. The A17 processor is the first made on a three-nanometre process, and it carries the hardware for the ray tracing that the games use. A smaller transistor is a battery that lasts or a task that runs faster. The chip has about 19 billion transistors, and the company said the graphics performance is 20 per cent higher than the previous generation. The company said the chip will let the phone run the console games that the developers have been porting, which is a claim it has made before and not delivered.

Cameras took the largest share of the presentation. The main sensor is bigger, the periscope lens has arrived on the largest model, and the company showed the portraits that the software can reframe after the picture is taken. A camera that reframes is a feature that sells the upgrade. The new lens folds the light through a prism, which gives the smaller model the reach of a telephoto without the bulk of a larger case.

Prices are unchanged in America and higher in most of the rest of the world. The company has absorbed the currency movements in the dollar and passed them on elsewhere, which is the pattern of a firm that defends its home market. A price that is held is a margin that is lost. The company said the trade-in program will cover the older models, and the carriers have been offering the discounts that the regulators have questioned.

What the event shows is that Apple has run out of new categories and is competing on the details, and the details are enough for a customer who replaces a phone every four years. The analysts expect the shipments to be about the same as last year, and the company has told its suppliers to prepare for a flat cycle. The details are the product, and the product is the reason the customers stay. The wait is the strategy, and the strategy buys the company another year.
