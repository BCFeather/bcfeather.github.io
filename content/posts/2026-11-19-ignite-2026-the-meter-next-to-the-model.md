50 million agents are registered in Microsoft's control plane, Agent 365, the company said as it opened its Ignite conference in San Francisco on 17 November. Beside that count came a product for pricing the fleet: Agent 365 Business Analytics, which the company calls an ERP system for AI usage and which links spend to work completed. The rest of the keynote supplied the agents to be counted, in Copilot forms, purpose-built models and new Azure silicon.

Copilot, presented as the operating system for work, now has four named forms. Home merges chat and cowork, Code builds apps and dashboards from a prompt, Autopilot runs tasks continuously across a team's tools, and Workspaces gives people and agents one shared room with common context. Two of the four are available today, since Home and Office inside Copilot are generally available. Code is promised within weeks, and Autopilot began reaching customers in the Frontier early-access programme on the day of the keynote. Work IQ indexes the people, documents and meetings beneath all of it.

Model announcements were organized by task and by price per task. Microsoft-Decision-1.1 has reached general availability with multimodal input, and the company reports 4,000 customers using it for routing, labelling, content filtering and personalization. Code 2 and Code 2 Flash are generally available in GitHub Copilot and Microsoft Foundry, sold on cost per task in production work. Code 2 Flash takes the high-volume jobs: explanations, targeted edits and routine fixes. Cyber 2 Flash, built for security work, is being wired into Perception. Foundry also gains GPT 6.2 and Meta's newest models, while MAI Licensed Weights will let trusted customers hold Microsoft's own weights.

Second half of the keynote went to governance. Perception, a security system that runs teams of red, blue and green agents through playbooks, is generally available, with playbooks deciding when those teams act. The Microsoft AI Transparency Initiative adds a standards track, with work inside the NIST agent standards effort and 40 organizations signed up to define independent verification of what a model sees and does. On the application side, plugins for Dynamics 365 arrived, and Salesforce content is coming into Copilot.

Every performance figure at the keynote came from the company. Maia 300, the next accelerator, is claimed at 40 per cent more tokens per watt than leading GPU-based systems, and Cobalt 200 virtual machines are sold as Azure's highest-performing general-purpose instances; neither claim carries a published benchmark. Business Analytics promises a line from spend to outcome, at a stage when adoption is still being counted. The transparency standard is in drafting, with the partners named and the tests unwritten.

What is shipping is also what can be bought. Home, Office, Perception and Cobalt 200 instances are available today. Code arrives within weeks, Autopilot is confined to Frontier customers, and MAI weights stay inside the trusted-customer programme. The metering that would make that fleet legible shipped alongside the security agents that read it, which puts a price on agents before anyone has a measurement of what they return.
