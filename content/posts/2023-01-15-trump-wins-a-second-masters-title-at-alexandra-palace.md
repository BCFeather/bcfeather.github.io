Played over two sessions at Alexandra Palace, the Masters final finished after midnight on 15 January. Judd Trump beat Mark Williams 10-8, and the scoreline hides how close the last four frames were. Trump had won the title in 2019, and this was his second, which puts him in a small group of players with more than one. The arena holds 2,000 and every session was sold out, including the afternoon ones usually played to a half house.

Williams, at 47, was the oldest finalist since the event moved to the palace in 2012. He had beaten Ronnie O'Sullivan in the quarter-final and had led 4-2 after the first session. His long potting was the best in the tournament, and the crowd applauded him at the end for longer than they applauded the winner. Williams had not reached a final at the venue since 2003, and he said the crowd had carried him through the semi-final.

Everything turned on the fifteenth frame. Williams missed a red along the cushion with the score at 7-7, and Trump cleared to go ahead. He then made a break of 90 in the next frame, and the last two were negotiated rather than made. Neither player looked comfortable, and both said afterwards that the table had been quick all week. The fifteenth frame lasted 32 minutes and contained one re-rack after the balls clustered near the black. The break in the sixteenth frame was the highest of the match, and it was the only century of the final.

Prize money was 250,000 pounds for the winner, unchanged from the previous year. The tournament is one of the three events that make up the triple crown, along with the World Championship and the UK Championship, and its invitation list is the top sixteen in the world rankings. Three players withdrew before the start, two through illness and one through a positive test. The runner-up took 100,000 pounds, and the two losing semi-finalists 50,000 each.

Television audience figures were the highest for the event since 2019, according to the broadcaster, with a peak of 2.4 million in the final session. That matters for a sport whose sponsorship has been thin since the pandemic, and the governing body said the new betting sponsor had signed for three years. The BBC broadcast every session, and the figures were the highest for a non-final session since 2018. The sponsor replaced a betting firm that had withdrawn in 2020, and the contract runs to 2026.

Williams said afterwards that he had enjoyed the week more than the result. Trump said the second title meant more than the first, because he had won it while not playing well. The world championship at Sheffield begins in April, and both will be in the field. The next triple crown event is at Sheffield in April, where the qualifying rounds begin in early March. The world championship qualifying draw was published in February, with 128 players entered.
