Chinese consumer prices fell 0.2 per cent in October from a year earlier, the statistics bureau said on 9 November, after showing no change in September. The producer prices fell 2.6 per cent, the thirteenth month of decline. The figure was below the forecast of a flat reading, and the measure has now been below zero in two of the last four months. The reading was the second below zero this year, and the economists have begun to use the word deflation in the notes to the clients.

Pork is the largest single item. The price of the pork fell by about 30 per cent as the herds were rebuilt after the African swine fever and the supply has outrun the demand. A pig that is raised in a year of the glut is a price that must fall. The pork price is about a third below its peak in 2019, and the farmers have been cutting the herds as the losses deepen. The herds are about 4 per cent larger than a year earlier, and the price of the live pigs has fallen below the cost of the feed in the main producing provinces.

Services are the other side. The prices of the travel and the hotels rose by about 1.5 per cent, and the rents were flat in the large cities, which suggests the demand for the services is holding while the goods are deflating. A service that is priced up is a demand that has shifted. The core measure, which excludes the food and the energy, rose by about 0.6 per cent, the slowest since the spring of 2021.

Producer prices follow the construction. The steel and the cement have fallen for the year as the property investment dropped by about 9 per cent in the first ten months. A site that is not started is a material that is not bought. The property investment fell 9.3 per cent in the ten months, and the new construction starts fell by about a quarter.

Economists are split on the cause. The official reading is that the base effects and the food supply explain most of the fall, and the private ones say the demand is the problem and the stimulus is too small. A reading that is disputed is a policy that is not settled. The central bank has cut the rates twice this year, and the economists expect more easing before the end of the year. The central bank has cut the reserve ratio by about 0.25 points twice, and the economists expect a further cut before the spring.

What the month shows is that the deflation has become broad enough to appear in the headline, and the appearance is the part that the policy must answer. A deflation that is led by the food is a deflation that the shoppers feel at the market rather than in the statistics, and the feeling is what the policy must address. The stimulus has been aimed at the infrastructure and the property, and the prices show that the money has not reached the shoppers.
