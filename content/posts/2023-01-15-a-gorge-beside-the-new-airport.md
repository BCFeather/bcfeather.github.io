The aircraft left Kathmandu at half past ten in the morning for a flight of twenty-seven minutes to Pokhara, and it crashed into the gorge of the Seti River short of the runway. The airline identified it as an ATR 72-212A and said there were sixty-eight passengers and four crew aboard. By the evening the civil aviation authority had confirmed that all seventy-two had died, which made it the worst air accident in Nepal in three decades. The flight was the crew's third sector of the day, on a shuttle route the airline flies several times each morning.

The airport it was approaching had opened on the first day of the year. Pokhara International Airport was built with Chinese financing on the eastern side of the valley, and the old airport, which the town had used since the 1970s, sits a few kilometres to the west. The aircraft came down between the two, on the bank of the river, and the rescue teams had to descend into the gorge to reach the wreckage. The wreckage was spread across the slope, and the search for the last passengers took until the following afternoon.

A video recorded inside the cabin by a passenger was circulating within hours, and it showed the seats, the windows and the quiet of an ordinary flight in its last minute. The weather was clear. Witnesses on the ground described the aircraft in a steep descent, and the flight recorders were recovered and sent abroad for reading. The passenger who recorded it was one of the fifteen foreign nationals on board.

Nepal's aviation record is a subject that the country's own regulators discuss with fatigue. A flight from Bangladesh crashed on approach to Kathmandu in 2018, a Tara Air flight went down in the mountains in 2022, and a Pakistan International Airlines flight crashed in 2020. The European Union has banned Nepali carriers from its airspace since 2013, and the reasons it gave were about oversight rather than about aircraft. The airline suspended its Kathmandu-Pokhara flights for two days, and the government declared a day of mourning.

Built to change the economics of a town that is the gateway to the Annapurna trekking routes, the airport had been open for two weeks. It has a runway that is longer than the old one and a terminal built for international traffic, and the airlines had begun to shift their schedules to it in the first fortnight of January. The approach passes over terrain that gives pilots little room, and the last two minutes of the flight are the part that every operator in the country has learned to treat as the dangerous part.

An airport built to bring tourists to a lake town is also a place where the final descent decides everything. The investigation will take a year, and the families will be given a report that explains the mechanics and cannot explain the morning. The trekking season begins in March, and the airport will be open again before the first trekkers arrive.
