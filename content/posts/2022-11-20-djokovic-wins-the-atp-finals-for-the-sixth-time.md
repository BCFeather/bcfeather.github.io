Novak Djokovic won the ATP Finals in Turin on 20 November, beating Casper Ruud 7-5, 6-3 in the final. The title was his sixth, which ties the record held by Roger Federer. He had won the event in 2008, 2012, 2013, 2014 and 2015, and he had not played it in 2021. The final was his 18th match of the tournament, and he had won 17 of them.

He had beaten Stefanos Tsitsipas, Andrey Rublev and Daniil Medvedev in the group stage and Taylor Fritz in the semi-final. The match lasted 1 hour 33 minutes, and Djokovic did not lose a set in the tournament. He was 35 and was the oldest winner since the event moved to the round-robin format. He had withdrawn from the event in 2021 because of a positive test, and he had not played since.

He had not played the Australian Open or the US Open because of his vaccination status, and he had played 11 tournaments. The season had been shortened for him. He won five of them, including Wimbledon and the ATP Finals, and he finished the year with a record of 42 wins and 7 losses. He had won 90 matches in the year, the most on the tour, and he had lost to Alcaraz in Madrid. He had won 71 of 79 hard-court matches in the year, and he had lost once indoors.

Prize money was 4.7 million dollars, the largest for a single tournament, and he took it without losing a match. The tournament had moved to Turin in 2021, and the attendance was 180,000 over the week, a record for the event. The final was watched by 3 million people in Italy.

Ruud was the runner-up for the second time. He had reached the final of the French Open and the US Open in the year, and he had lost both. He was 23, and he finished the year ranked third, which is the highest of his career. The rankings were affected by the decisions about the vaccination. Djokovic finished fifth, and Carlos Alcaraz finished first at 19, the youngest ever. The ranking was calculated on 52 weeks, and the points from Wimbledon did not count because the tour had stripped the tournament of ranking points.

He had led the tour for 12 years, and he was replaced by a former player. The tournament was the last for the long-time chief executive of the men's tour, who stepped down at the end of the year. The tour announced a rise in prize money for 2023 of 20 per cent. Djokovic won the Australian Open in January 2023 after the vaccination requirement was lifted, and he won three of the four majors in the year. He finished 2023 ranked first, and he passed 400 weeks at number one in 2024. The Finals win in Turin was the last of his six.
