---

Apple refreshed its professional laptops on 17 January without a launch event, putting the M2 Pro and M2 Max into the 14-inch and 16-inch MacBook Pro and leaving the design untouched. The company says the chips bring a 20 per cent faster processor and a 30 per cent faster graphics core than the M1 Pro, and that the smaller machine will run for 22 hours on a charge. The refresh came three months after the M2 Air, which had been the first machine to carry the second generation of the chip.

Both processors are built on a five-nanometre process and add cores rather than clocks. The M2 Pro carries up to twelve processor cores and nineteen graphics cores, with 200 gigabytes per second of memory bandwidth. The M2 Max doubles the bandwidth and offers up to thirty-eight graphics cores, aimed at video work and model training on a desk rather than in a data centre. Memory is soldered to the package, so the amount chosen at purchase is the amount the machine will ever have.

Ports stay as they were, which is the point of the machine for the people who buy it. Three Thunderbolt 4 sockets, an HDMI output that now drives an 8K display at 60 frames a second, an SD card slot and MagSafe charging are all present, and the headphone jack still supports high-impedance headphones. External displays are limited to two of the older standard or one of the newer, a restriction that annoyed buyers of the Max.

Prices rose slightly in several markets. The 14-inch model starts at 1,999 dollars in the United States, and the 16-inch at 2,499 dollars, with the Max versions adding between 500 and 700 dollars depending on memory. The Mac mini also received the M2 and M2 Pro, and its starting price stayed at 599 dollars, which makes it the cheapest route into the new silicon. The 16-inch model weighs 2.1 kilograms and the 14-inch 1.6, which is why the smaller one is the one people carry.

Reviews in the following week concentrated on the same question, whether the gains matter outside a studio. Export times for a ten-minute video came down by roughly a third against the M1 Max, and compiles of a large code repository took about a fifth less time, while ordinary browsing and word processing showed no difference at all. The base configuration still begins with 512 gigabytes of storage, and the step to a terabyte costs 200 dollars.

Shipments began at the end of January, in the middle of a wider slowdown in personal computer sales. Industry trackers had reported four consecutive quarterly declines through 2022, and Apple was one of the few makers to hold volume, which gives the refresh a job beyond speed, since it has to persuade buyers that a machine bought in 2021 is now due for replacement. Apple reported a 30 per cent fall in Mac revenue in the following quarter, so the machines arrived into a shrinking market.
