British consumer prices rose 3.9 per cent in the year to November, the statistics office said on 20 December, down from 4.6 per cent in October and below the forecast of 4.3. The figure was the lowest since the autumn of 2021, and the measure is now below 4 per cent for the first time in two years. The figure was the lowest since the autumn of 2021, and the measure has fallen in four of the last five months.

Food prices did the work. The prices rose by about 8 per cent, down from 10, and the one of the milk and the cheese has been falling since the summer. A basket that stops rising is the measure the shopper feels. The bread and the cereals rose by about 7 per cent, and the vegetables fell by about 2 as the harvest was good. The meat and the dairy have fallen for three months, and the analysts expect the food to add almost nothing to the index next year.

Energy was flat. The prices rose by about 2 per cent after the fall of the previous month, and the cap on the bills will rise in January. A bill that is stable is an index that waits. The regulator will raise the cap by about 5 per cent in January, and the analysts expect the headline to rise by about 0.2 points. The gas and the electricity are about 15 per cent below the level of last year, and the effect will fade out of the index in the spring.

Services inflation fell to 6.3 per cent from 6.6, and the bank had forecast about 6.9 in its report a week earlier. A surprise that is lower is a cut that is closer. The services measure has been the bank's focus, and the fall is the first clear one since the spring. The wages have been rising by about 7 per cent in the private sector, and the bank has said the services will follow the pay with a lag.

Core inflation fell to 5.1 per cent, and the economists said the pace was faster than the bank had expected in the three months. A fall that is broad is a policy that is working. The traders now price the first cut for the summer, and the two-year yield fell to the lowest since the spring. The measure is the one the bank has said it watches most, and the fall of about 0.3 points is the largest since the spring.

What the month shows is that the disinflation has reached the food and the services at the same time, and the simultaneity is what the bank asked for. A headline below 4 per cent is a headline the government will cite, and the citation is the political part of the number.
