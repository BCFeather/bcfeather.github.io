A Long March 7 lifted off from Wenchang at 05:34 on 15 July carrying the Tianzhou-9 cargo ship, and the spacecraft separated about ten minutes later and docked with the rear port of the Tianhe module at 08:52. The whole journey took a little over three hours, which is the fast rendezvous that the programme has made routine. The cargo was about 6.5 tonnes, including propellant, food, experiments and a new spacesuit.

The fast rendezvous is a control problem rather than a propulsion one.
